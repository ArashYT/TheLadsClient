2. Source-Integrated / Clean-Room Reimplementations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* ItemPhysic (creative item drop / physics rendering)
  Clean-room reimplementation in `com.thelads.core.client.itemphysic.*`.
  Original mod by CreativeMD (LGPL-3.0).

* Blur (in-game GUI background blur)
  Clean-room reimplementation in `com.thelads.core.client.blur.*`.
  Original mod by Motschen / tterrag1098 (MIT License).

* Dynamic Lights / LambDynamicLights
  Clean-room reimplementation of smooth entity and hand-held item lighting.
  Original mod by LambdAurora (MIT License).

* Wavey Capes (cloth physics simulation for player capes)
  Clean-room implementation in `com.thelads.core.client.capes.physics.*`.
  Original mod by tr7zw (LGPL-3.0-only).

* 3D Skin Layers (extruding player second layer into 3D voxels)
  Bundled as official mod jar in 1.8.9 (`skinlayers3d`).
  Original mod by tr7zw (tr7zw Protective License).

* Sound Physics Remastered (reverberation, occlusion and sound absorption)
  Clean-room implementation in `com.thelads.core.client.sound.*`.
  Original mod by henkelmax / sonicether (GPL-3.0-or-later).

* Schematica (read-only blueprint ghost overlay, strictly without printer/placement)
  Clean-room classic .schematic NBT parser and wireframe renderer in `com.thelads.core.client.schematic.*`.
  Original mod by Lunatrius (Modified BSD License).
