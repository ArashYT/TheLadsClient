Modified by The Lads: repackaged into Lads Core (com.thelads.core.v1_20_1.embedded.fastipping) and ported to
Minecraft 1.20.1 from the Lads 26.3 embedded copy (which is derived from the tag and commit above): the Minecraft
1.17+ Fabric path of the preprocessed source, the unused pre-1.17 and Forge variants are left out and the log line
moves to Core's client initializer. The 26.3 mixin patches the InetAddress inside ServerAddressResolver's lambda, a
synthetic method whose name differs between the dev, intermediary and Forge-mapped game; on 1.20.1 the mixin wraps the
resolver call in ServerNameResolver.resolveAddress instead (ServerNameResolverMixin) and rebuilds the resolved
address with the patched InetAddress, which has the same effect: the patched address is what AddressCheck and the
connection see. A QA probe was added. The complete modified source files ship in this jar under
META-INF/lads-sources/fastipping/v1_20_1. The embedded copy stays inactive while the original Fast IP Ping jar is
installed (Fabric jar, or Forge jar under Sinytra Connector).
