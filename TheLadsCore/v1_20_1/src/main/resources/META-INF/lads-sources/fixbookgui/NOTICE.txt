Modified by The Lads: repackaged into Lads Core as com.thelads.core.v1_20_1.embedded.fixbookgui
(mixin config theladscore.v1_20_1.embedded.fixbookgui.mixins.json) and ported to Minecraft 1.20.1 from the
Lads 26.3 embedded copy: the multi-version preprocessor branches are resolved for 1.20.1, and the mixins are skipped
while the original fixbookgui mod is installed (Fabric jar, or Forge jar under Sinytra Connector).

Behavior is the same as in 26.3 (the book screens sit a third of the free vertical space below the top of the
screen, instead of at the very top). 1.20.1's screens have no overridable top (backgroundTop) and no separate
BookSignScreen (signing is part of BookEditScreen), so the mixins move 1.20.1's literal heights instead: the buttons
created in init, the book texture, the page indicator, the signing labels, and the text/caret/selection mapping of
the edit screen. The offset is never negative (26.3 lets it go negative on screens lower than the book). A QA
probe was added.

The complete modified source is in META-INF/lads-sources/fixbookgui/v1_20_1 in this jar.
To use a modified version, change those files in the Lads source tree
(TheLadsCore/v1_20_1/src/main/java/com/thelads/core/v1_20_1/embedded/fixbookgui) and rebuild Core
with TheLadsCore/gradlew.bat :v1_20_1:build, or compile them against this jar, Minecraft 1.20.1 and
Fabric Loader and replace the classes of that package in a copy of this jar.
