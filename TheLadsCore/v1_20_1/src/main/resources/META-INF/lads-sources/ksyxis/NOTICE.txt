Modified by The Lads: repackaged into Lads Core (com.thelads.core.v1_20_1.embedded.ksyxis) and ported to
Minecraft 1.20.1 from the Lads 26.3 embedded copy (which is derived from the tag and commit above). The 26.3 copy
keeps only the injections for the 1.21.9+ player-spawn preparation and the 500 ms world-open delay, neither of which
exists in 1.20.1. On 1.20.1 the same idea is applied to MinecraftServer.prepareLevels: the radius-11 START ticket
around the world spawn is not added and the "Preparing spawn area" wait for 441 ticking chunks ends at once, so the
world opens without generating the 21 x 21 start region (the chunks around the player load when the player joins).
As with Ksyxis itself, the spawn chunks are therefore no longer kept loaded. The legacy-version mixins and loader
shims of Ksyxis are left out. A QA probe was added. The embedded copy stays inactive while the original Ksyxis jar is
installed (Fabric jar, or Forge jar under Sinytra Connector).
