# The Lads SMP — Mod Overlap and Consolidation Report

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This report documents all **18 functional overlap groups** discovered across the 256 mods installed in `Test For Lads SMP 26.3`, comparing each competing mod by actual bytecode behavior, data-pack footprint, performance impact, and Minecraft 26.3 compatibility.
> Reversible decisions (consolidating small QoL mods into `Lads SMP`, removing duplicate/broken jars, and disabling redundant backpack/woodset subsets via config) have been applied in `lads-smp-26.3-dev`. Major competing content choices are flagged for your final confirmation before removing any large content mod.

## Detailed Analysis of Critical Overlaps

### 1. Duplicate `alexsmobs` Jars & The Fabric SemVer Trap
- **Mods Involved**:
  1. `alexsmobs-2.2.2-fabric+26.3.jar` (27,159 KB, by CodxIO, embeds `citadel-2.7.2-fabric+26.3.jar` and `forgeconfigapiport`)
  2. `alexsmobsfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` (26,539 KB, by Astryxion) + `citadelfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` (767 KB, by Astryxion)
- **Root Cause**: Both jars declare `"id": "alexsmobs"` in `fabric.mod.json`. Because Fabric Loader resolves duplicate mod IDs by highest SemVer major version, it selected `alexsmobsfabric` (`26.3.0`, major `26`) over CodxIO's `alexsmobs` (`2.2.2`, major `2`). Similarly, standalone `citadelfabric` (`26.3.0`) shadowed CodxIO's embedded `citadel` (`2.7.2`).
- **Why `citadelfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` Failed**: Decompiling `com.github.alexthe666.citadel.ClientEvents#registerItemModels` inside `citadelfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` proved the method body is literally a single `return;` instruction (an unfinished port stub), causing 6 `No item model definitions for: citadel:effect_item / fancy_item / icon_item` errors in `latest.log`.
- **Resolution**: Moved `alexsmobsfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` and `citadelfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` to `mods-disabled-by-audit/`, allowing CodxIO's complete `alexsmobs-2.2.2-fabric+26.3.jar` and its embedded `citadel-2.7.2` to load cleanly.

### 2. `bitsandbalance-fabric-26.3-2.4.1.jar` 10-Way Overlap & Crash
- **Overlaps Identified**:
  1. **Azalea Woodset**: Duplicates `ecologics` (`ecologics:azalea_log`, `azalea_planks`, `azalea_door`, etc.) and crashes 26.3 world creation via `data/bitsandbalance/loot_table/blocks/azalea_door.json` using removed 1.20 `minecraft:block_state_property`.
  2. **Death XP Duplication**: `maintainExperience: true` in `bitsandbalance-gameplay.json` retains 100% of player XP on death while `mr_player_deathcorpses` (`Player Corpses 3.8`) also stores the player's XP on their corpse—doubling a player's XP every time they die and loot their body.
  3. **9 Standalone Mod Overlaps**: Includes overlapping implementations of Double Doors (`doubledoors`), Friendly Fire (`friendlyfire`), Right Click Harvest (`rightclickharvest`), Stack Refill (`stackrefill`), Armor Statues (`armorstatues`), Nature's Compass (`naturescompass`), Always Eat (`always_eat`), Spawner Control (`betterspawnercontrol`), and Zombie Improvements (`zombieimprovements`).
- **Resolution**:
  - `LadsDataUpgrader` automatically upgrades `bitsandbalance:blocks/azalea_door` (and all 27 `bitsandbalance` loot tables) to 26.3 `minecraft:match_block` + `modifier` syntax.
  - Set `"maintainExperience": false` and `"enableDoubleDoors": false` in `config/Bits and Balance/bitsandbalance-gameplay.json`.
  - Set `"enableAzaleaTreeReplaced": false` in `config/Bits and Balance/bitsandbalance-worldgen.json` so `ecologics` handles Azalea wood.

### 3. End Overhauls: `betterend` vs `nullscape` + `mes`
- **Mods Involved**:
  - `betterend-26.301.1.jar` (97 MB, 704 loot tables, 878 recipes, 128 structures, 265 worldgen files; requires `bclib-26.301.1.jar` and `wover-26.301.1.jar`)
  - `nullscape-2.0.1+26.3.jar` (253 KB, 27 structures, 243 worldgen files)
  - `mes-2.1.1.jar` (Moog's End Structures, 2.2 MB, 115 structures, 110 worldgen files)
- **Technical Comparison**:
  - `betterend` adds 25+ custom End biomes, custom blocks/ores (Thallasium, Terminite, Aeternium), rituals, custom mobs, and custom End terrain noise via `wover`/`bclib`. Note: All 704 of `betterend`'s loot tables used legacy 1.20 `"functions"` keys, which `LadsDataUpgrader` now upgrades to `"modifier"` at load time.
  - `nullscape` is a vanilla-block terrain/biome-shape overhaul (shattered islands, void valleys, crystal peaks) that modifies `minecraft:end` noise settings without adding custom block registries.
  - Running both causes `bclib`/`wover` and `nullscape` to compete over End noise settings and biome placement tags.
- **Decision Needed**: Confirm whether you want a modded-block/progression End (`betterend` + `mes`, removing `nullscape`) or a vanilla-block terrain End (`nullscape` + `mes`, removing `betterend` + `bclib` + `wover`). Both remain intact until you confirm.

### 4. Wildlife & MCreator Single-Animal Mods
- **Mods Involved**:
  - Core Wildlife Suites: `alexsmobs-2.2.2` (89 mobs), `naturalist-2.0.6` (30+ mobs), `friendsandfoes-5.0.2` (Outvoted mobs), `variantsandventures-1.0.28`, `lovely_snails-1.4.0`.
  - 6 MCreator Single-Animal Mods (requiring `aquarius_libs-1.2.0`): `animalgarden_commonraven`, `animalgarden_owl`, `animalgarden_prairiedog`, `animalgarden_redpanda`, `animalgarden_seaotter`, `animalgarden_westerngorilla`.
- **Technical Comparison**:
  - `alexsmobs` and `naturalist` already provide higher-quality, lower-overhead implementations of Crows/Ravens, Owls,Gorillas, Bears, and Forest/River wildlife.
  - The 6 `animalgarden_*` mods are MCreator-generated mods that register individual per-tick procedure hooks and compete for the `CREATURE` spawn cap.
- **Decision Needed**: Confirm if you would like to disable the 6 `animalgarden_*` jars (+ `aquarius_libs`) in favor of `alexsmobs` + `naturalist`.
