## 3. Special Case Note: `reg's more foods 1.4.2 26.1.jar`

- **Filename**: `reg's more foods 1.4.2 26.1.jar` (3,160 KB)
- **Packaging Anomaly**: This MCreator-generated jar contains `META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml` and `data/regs_more_foods/` (40 loot tables, 316 recipes), **but lacks a `fabric.mod.json` descriptor**.
- **Runtime Behavior on Fabric 0.19.5**: Because Fabric Loader only loads mods with `fabric.mod.json`, its Java classes (`net.mcreator.regsmorefoods.*`) are ignored at startup, though datapack scanners or recipe viewers scanning jars may see its `data/` tree. Furthermore, its `data/regs_more_foods/loot_table/entities/ender_dragon.json` attempts to add a 30% extra `minecraft:dragon_egg` drop.
- **Mitigation**: `Lads SMP` blocks `minecraft:dragon_egg` in `lootOnlyDisabledItems` so no loot table can ever generate bonus Dragon Eggs.
