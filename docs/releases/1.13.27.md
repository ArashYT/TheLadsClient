# Lads Client 1.13.27

Other players and mobs move smoothly again on 26.3.

## Added

- **Smooth Movement** (Visuals > Players & Mobs): 26.3 made other players and mobs look choppy, freezing for a moment between updates. This brings back the smooth 26.2 movement. The Lads Network turns it on for you. On other servers it stays off unless you turn it on, because some anticheats there may not expect it.
