# Lads Client 1.14.3

A new Play page in the launcher: one big Play button, your profile and account next to it, and quick cards for The Lads Network and your experiences.

## Added

- Launcher: the Play page shows the game version, how many mods the profile has and when you last played.
- Launcher: "Jump in" cards under the Play button for The Lads Network, Zero SMP and Lads Horizon. Each shows its status and has one button to join, install, play or open it.

## Changed

- Launcher: Play, the profile picker and the account picker sit together at the top of the Play page. The empty space in the middle of the page is gone.
- Launcher: the side menu is sorted into Play, Content and App and fits without scrolling at the normal window size.
- Launcher: "Aspect Ratio" and "Reset Size" moved from the top bar to Settings, under Window.
- Launcher: the CPU, memory and download line at the bottom is smaller.

## Fixed

- Launcher: the Zero SMP card on the Experiences page no longer overlaps the cards next to it in a narrow window.
- Experimental Horizon: your Lads controls are now kept the first time Horizon starts. Before, the game dropped them.
