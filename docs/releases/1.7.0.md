# The Lads Client 1.7.0

## Highlights
- **A new HUD editor.** The game now sits in a framed live preview with a list of every HUD element beside it, so what you drop is exactly where it shows in game. Grouping only happens when you hold Shift as you drop.
- **Toggle Sprint and Toggle Sneak are now one module, "Toggle Sprint & Sneak",** with one HUD line, no more sprint flicker, toggle states that survive death and restarts, and optional separate toggle keys.
- **New HUD pieces:** Armor HUD in hotbar slots next to your offhand, Simple Voice Chat's icons and group list as movable Lads HUD elements (26.2 and 26.3), and chat with no 100-line limit.
- **3D Skin Layers is back** on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3, with the SkinLayers module switching it on and off.

## Added
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak keys.** "Sprint (Toggle)" and "Sneak (Toggle)" are in the Controls screen's Movement section, right under Sprint and Sneak. They are unbound by default, so Sprint and Sneak toggle as before. Bind one and that key toggles while the normal key goes back to vanilla hold-to-sprint or hold-to-sneak. The module page shows the bound key, and clicking it opens Controls.
- **Armor HUD Hotbar Slots style** (the new default): four hotbar-style slots next to the offhand slot, each with the worn piece and its durability bar, or a faint outline for an empty slot. The old style is still there as Style: List.
- **Simple Voice Chat HUD (26.2 and 26.3).** "Voice Chat" shows the talking, whispering, muted, deafened or disconnected icon, with an optional label. "Voice Chat Group" lists your group's players with their heads, green while they talk. Both are on by default, can be moved and scaled in the HUD editor, and replace Simple Voice Chat's own fixed icons while they are on. Without Simple Voice Chat installed they show as unavailable.
- **Chat options:** Infinite History (chat keeps up to 100,000 messages instead of 100, and scrolls back through all of them) and Text Shadow. Both are on by default.
- **Scoreboard option Custom Text Color** (off by default). Off, the sidebar shows the server's own colours.
- **Title screen:** Mods and Screenshots buttons on the main button grid, a fullscreen button at the top right, and a Switch button next to your account name at the bottom left.
- **Pause menu icons:** every pause menu button has a small pixel-art icon next to its label (1.8.9, 26.2, 26.3).
- **3D Skin Layers** (by tr7zw) is bundled again on 26.2 and 26.3, and the Forge build is new on 1.8.9. The SkinLayers module turns 3D layers on and off for every player, and keeps its 1.6.0 settings.

## Changed
- **HUD editor** on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3:
  - A toolbar across the top (Group, Ungroup, Lock, Snap, Done) and a list of HUD elements on the left with search, a settings gear and an on/off switch for each one, plus Colors and Reset.
  - Only switched-on elements are listed and previewed. Show disabled (or the A key) adds the others, dimmed, so you can switch them on from the list.
  - Selecting in the list selects in the preview and the other way round.
  - A plain drop still snaps and lines up against other HUDs but never groups. Hold Shift while dropping to group. The Group and Ungroup buttons, the right-click menu, Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G work as before. G toggles snapping.
  - Your saved layouts and groups from 1.6.0 carry over unchanged.
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak** replaces the separate Toggle Sprint and Toggle Sneak modules. Your 1.6.0 settings move over by themselves: on/off, modes, Disable on sneak, favourite, HUD position, lock and groups. The HUD line reads like "[Sprinting (Toggled)] [Sneaking (Key Held)]".
- **Toggle states are remembered.** A toggled sprint or sneak stays on through death, respawn, changing worlds, servers or dimensions, disconnecting, restarting the game and switching the module off and on.
- **Toggle Sprint no longer forces sprinting.** It acts like holding the Sprint key, so Minecraft's own rules (hunger, blindness, walls, water, using items) decide when you sprint, exactly as with a held key.
- **Scoreboard colours.** The sidebar now shows the server's colours (on 26.x the colours were always lost before). Its Text Shadow option no longer depends on the global HUD shadow switch.
- **Armor HUD** moves to the new Hotbar Slots style for everyone, including existing configs. If you have Attach to hotbar off, your own position is kept. Pick Style: List to get the 1.6.0 look back.
- **1.7 Animations:**
  - Eating and drinking never show a swing, on every version.
  - Blocking, the bow and eating now start straight away while you are mining a block, like 1.7. Vanilla ignored the use key until you stopped mining. This checks both hands on 26.x, so a shield or food in the offhand also starts while you mine.
  - With Legacy Swing on, blocks you hold (including torches, flowers and rails) keep vanilla's placing animation and only Legacy Swing animates them.
- **Title screen:** the rows are now Lads Mods | Mods, Options | Screenshots, More... | Quit Game. Mods, Screenshots and Accounts are no longer in More, which keeps Realms, Language and (on 26.x) Friends, Accessibility, Credits and Open Replays. On 1.8.9 the Screenshots button shows while BetterScreenshots is on.
- **Lads menu, Installed mods:** mods are shown in two columns, like the module cards. Narrow windows fall back to one column.
- **1.8.9 world loading is much faster.** Opening a world, going through a portal and leaving no longer wait on forced memory clean-ups or slow polling. In testing: opening a world went from about 1.5 s to 0.35 s, the Nether from 0.57 s to 0.13 s, back to the Overworld from 0.48 s to 0.05 s, and leaving from 0.67 s to 0.24 s.
- **Rogue ACE (5 kills) kill banner:** the head now drops in with its ring and stays centred in it, as in kills 1 to 4.
- **Bundled mods.** NoPackCompatCheck is removed on 26.2 and 26.3; No Resource Pack Warnings, which is still included, already hides the outdated-pack warning. Updated on 26.3: Balm 26.3.0.3, C2ME 0.4.2-alpha.0.89, Chat Heads 1.3.1, Collective 8.41, Fzzy Config 0.7.7+fix3, Iris 1.11.7, Jasione 1.0.9, JEI 31.9.0.56 (beta: JEI only publishes betas for 26.3), MezzConfig 0.6.8, MoreCulling 1.9.0 (now a stable release) and OBE 1.1.50. Updated on 26.2: C2ME 0.4.2-alpha.0.56, Chat Heads 1.3.1, Collective 8.41, Jasione 1.0.9, MezzConfig 0.6.8, OBE 1.1.50 and SuperMartijn642's Config Lib 1.1.8a.
- Singleplayer worlds get a small new file, `lads-cheats.properties`, that remembers whether players who join may use commands.

## Fixed
- **Sprint flicker.** On 1.8.9 every hit, wall or item use made the sprint stop and start again, sending a pair of packets each time. No version forces sprinting any more, and after Minecraft stops a toggled sprint it waits a quarter of a second before asking for it again, which also removes the stop/start pairs at water edges. In-game tests on all three versions saw at most one sprint packet per real stop or start.
- **Cheats in singleplayer did not stick.** Essential kept its own per-world cheats switch and put it back every time the world opened, so turning cheats on in World Options, Multiplayer Options or Open to LAN was undone on the next visit (on 26.2 and 26.3 the World Options switch did not even work during the session). The world's own setting now wins, every change you make is passed on to Essential, and the "allow commands for other players" choice is saved per world. On 1.8.9, Open to LAN with Allow Cheats now also turns your own cheats on and saves them with the world.
- **Minimap flickered with the HUD FPS cap on (26.2 and 26.3).** Xaero's minimap was missing on every frame between HUD redraws. It now shows on every frame, and the cap keeps its benefit.
- **Auto Hide HUD:** on 26.x the hotbar frame came back fully visible between HUD redraws while its items stayed faded. The frame, selected slot, offhand, items, counts, durability bars and attack indicator now fade and hide together. On 1.8.9 Auto Hide HUD did nothing before; it now fades the hotbar, items, status bars, XP bar and the Lads HUD together.
- **1.8.9: two boss bars.** With the Lads Boss Bar module on, Forge's own boss bar is no longer drawn as well.
- **26.2 and 26.3: the 1.7 Animations Instant sneak camera could drop twice** after a quick sneak tap on a server. It now follows your own sneak key.
- **26.2 and 26.3: Vertical Bobbing snapped level** the moment you started flying, swimming or riding. It now eases back to level, the same at any frame rate.
- **Gaps in 3D held and dropped items.** Item models now get the same small edge overlap Forge uses, on 26.2 and 26.3 (also with Sodium) and for models Minecraft's own generator makes on 1.8.9 (such as OptiFine custom item textures).

## Known limitations
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak:** after Minecraft stops a toggled sprint, it takes a quarter of a second (5 ticks) before sprinting is asked for again, where a held key retries on the next tick. A separate Sneak (Toggle) key has only been unit-tested, not tried in game. Leaving and rejoining a world without restarting was not tested on 26.x.
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak migration:** if only Toggle Sneak was on in 1.6.0, the new module is on with Sprint set to Vanilla. If both were off, it stays off and starts from the new defaults.
- **Scoreboard:** every upgraded config shows the scoreboard's own colours once. If you chose a scoreboard colour in 1.6.0, turn Custom Text Color back on.
- **Simple Voice Chat HUD** was tested with one player. The deafened icon was real; talking, whispering, muted, disconnected and the group list were checked with sample data. There is no Simple Voice Chat for 1.8.9, so both elements are unavailable there.
- **Infinite chat** stops at 100,000 messages. The oldest messages past that point are dropped.
- **HUD editor** was checked in game only at GUI scale 2. At GUI scale 4 on a 1280x720 window the list shows about three rows at a time (search and scrolling still work). On 1.8.9, vanilla's "Press E to open your inventory" tip can cover the right end of the toolbar.
- **Cheats:** on 26.x, Create World with Allow Commands and Essential's own cheats toggle were not tried in game, and no second player ever joined a test world. On 26.x, /publish and Essential's hosting still set guest cheats for that session only.
- **1.7 Animations:** the 1.8.9 fly, swim and ride camera still settles in about two ticks as stock 1.8.9 does; the slower ease is 26.x only. The sneak camera fix could not be seen in single player, where there is no network delay, and has not been tried on a real server. 1.7 Animations sneak on 26.x keeps 26.x's camera height (the 1.8 height would put the camera inside blocks under low ceilings).
- **3D Skin Layers:** only your own player was photographed in testing. Other players use the same code.
- **Item gap fix:** no gaps were visible in testing before the fix either, so the screenshots only show nothing broke. The Sodium part could not be confirmed in a running game. On 1.8.9, normal items already had Forge's fix and look the same.
- **1.8.9 loading times** were measured on a busy test computer and joining a multiplayer server was not timed.
- **Title screen** layout at other window sizes and GUI scales is covered by layout tests only. On very cramped screens the Switch button shows only its icon.
- **Minecraft 1.21.11** gets no new testing. It still builds with this release and keeps NoPackCompatCheck.
- Each change in this release passed its own in-game checks on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3, but the combined 1.7.0 build has not yet been run through the full in-game self-test.
