# The Lads Client 1.7.0

## Highlights
- **A new HUD editor.** The game now sits in a framed live preview with a list of every HUD element beside it, so what you drop is exactly where it shows in game. Grouping only happens when you hold Shift as you drop.
- **Toggle Sprint and Toggle Sneak are now one module, "Toggle Sprint & Sneak",** with one HUD line, no more sprint flicker, toggle states that survive death and restarts, and optional separate toggle keys.
- **New HUD pieces:** Armor HUD in hotbar slots next to your offhand, Simple Voice Chat's icons and group list as movable Lads HUD elements (26.2 and 26.3), and chat with no 100-line limit.
- **3D Skin Layers is back** on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3, with the SkinLayers module switching it on and off.
- **Every Valorant kill banner.** Kill Banner now has all 91 skins from Kingdom Archives, each with its own kill sounds for 1 to 5 kills, and the Base banner is now Valorant's real default banner.
- **Item Physics:** dropped items lie flat, tumble through the air and float on water and lava, with ItemPhysic-style rules in singleplayer (off by default).
- **The launcher in your language:** pick one of 25 languages in Settings, translated by Google Translate.
- **1.8.9 Borderless Fullscreen should now show in OBS and Discord screen share.** The borderless window is one row taller than your monitor (the extra row is off screen), which is what Windows needs to stop treating it as exclusive fullscreen. The HUD is laid out in the rows you can see, so the hotbar is not cut off.
- **Remade as Lads' own code:** AppleSkin, AutoReconnect, Async Logger, Better F3, Chat Signing Hider, Chat Heads, Configured Defaults, Custom FOV, Durability Tooltip, Custom Crosshair, Jasione, Dynamic Lights, Mouse Tweaks, Network Protocol Disconnect, Paper Doll and Raised. Nothing was copied from these mods. Each feature was written from scratch from what the mod does.
- **New:** Better Resolution, Flashback Settings, and Ctrl+R to refresh the server list.
- **Fewer mods to download on 26.2 and 26.3.** Async Logger, Better F3 (26.2), Chat Heads, Configured Defaults, Custom FOV, Jasione, LambDynamicLights, Mouse Tweaks and Network Protocol Disconnect are now built in, and LibJF is no longer needed. The launcher moves the copies it installed out of your mods folder on the next launch. A copy you added or changed yourself is left alone, and the built-in version then switches itself off so the two never run together.

## Added
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak keys.** "Sprint (Toggle)" and "Sneak (Toggle)" are in the Controls screen's Movement section, right under Sprint and Sneak. They are unbound by default, so Sprint and Sneak toggle as before. Bind one and that key toggles while the normal key goes back to vanilla hold-to-sprint or hold-to-sneak. The module page shows the bound key, and clicking it opens Controls.
- **Armor HUD Hotbar Slots style** (the new default): four hotbar-style slots next to the offhand slot, each with the worn piece and its durability bar, or a faint outline for an empty slot. The old style is still there as Style: List.
- **Simple Voice Chat HUD (26.2 and 26.3).** "Voice Chat" shows the talking, whispering, muted, deafened or disconnected icon, with an optional label. "Voice Chat Group" lists your group's players with their heads, green while they talk. Both are on by default, can be moved and scaled in the HUD editor, and replace Simple Voice Chat's own fixed icons while they are on. Without Simple Voice Chat installed they show as unavailable.
- **Chat options:** Infinite History (chat keeps up to 100,000 messages instead of 100, and scrolls back through all of them) and Text Shadow. Both are on by default.
- **Scoreboard option Custom Text Color** (off by default). Off, the sidebar shows the server's own colours.
- **Title screen:** Mods and Screenshots buttons on the main button grid, a fullscreen button at the top right, and a Switch button next to your account name at the bottom left.
- **Pause menu icons:** every pause menu button has a small pixel-art icon next to its label (1.8.9, 26.2, 26.3).
- **3D Skin Layers** (by tr7zw) is bundled again on 26.2 and 26.3, and the Forge build is new on 1.8.9. The SkinLayers module turns 3D layers on and off for every player, and keeps its 1.6.0 settings.
- **The launcher in your language.** Settings → General → Language lists English and 25 other languages, each under its own name (Español, Deutsch, 日本語, العربية…). The launcher is translated by Google Translate when you pick one and switches right away, with no restart. Translations are saved on your PC, so after the first time a language loads instantly and works offline. Without internet the launcher stays in English and tries again later. Pick English to switch back.
- Your own things are never sent to Google or translated: account names, profile, world, skin and modpack names, server names, addresses and MOTDs, mod names, file names, paths and version numbers. Mod descriptions are translated. The game's own language is not changed.
- **Item Physics** (1.8.9, 26.2, 26.3; off by default). Dropped items lie flat on the ground and blocks rest on a side, items tumble through the air when thrown or falling and settle without snapping, and items afloat on water or lava lie on the surface, turning and rocking gently. Stacks still show 1 to 5 copies, like vanilla. This works on every server, because it only changes how your game draws items.
- **Item Physics rules for singleplayer and worlds you host** (marked SP in its settings), each with its own switch: wood, wool and other light things float up in water while stone and metal sink; only flammable items burn in fire and lava, the rest survive and float on lava; a burning flammable item lying on a flammable block can set it alight (only where fire is allowed to spread); cactus no longer destroys items; a despawn time from 1 to 30 minutes (5 by default, as vanilla); right-click to pick items up instead of walking over them (off by default); and hold the drop key to throw further, with a small bar under the crosshair. On multiplayer servers these rules do nothing, since the server decides how items behave.
- **88 new kill banner skins**, from Aemondir to Xerøfang, including the Champions banners that change with each kill and Phaseguard's coloured variants. Skins with variants (up to four) let you pick one.
- **Kill sounds for every skin**, a different sound for each kill in a streak, up to five kills.
- **Skin search** in the Kill Banner settings: type part of a name to filter the skin tiles, the Custom choices and the Randomize "Chosen" list.
- Clicking a skin tile plays its first-kill sound.
- "Skin and variant" randomizing now picks from all 91 skins.
- **Better Resolution (all versions)** replaces Render Scale, and your Render Scale settings carry over. It adds two new upscaling methods: Smooth (sharper than Linear, without halos) and Sharp (crisp, with light sharpening). Smooth is the default for new settings. The Scale slider moves in 5% steps and only works with the Custom preset. On 26.2 and 26.3 Iris shaders also run at the scaled resolution, so a lower scale gives shader packs a large FPS boost.
- **Flashback Settings (26.2, 26.3)** in the Lads menu: choose the replay folder (or Browse... for one), the PNG compression level, and an "OpenH264 to GPU" switch that hands OpenH264 video exports to your graphics card's encoder (NVENC, QSV or AMF). Opaque PNG sequence exports are written without an alpha channel, so those files are about 6-7% smaller. Exports are not faster: Flashback 0.43.6 already includes most of what Flashback Turbo added. If the chosen folder can't be used, Flashback's own folder is used, so a recording is never lost.
- **Chat Heads (all versions):** a player's head in chat, drawn just before the sender's name as the Chat Heads mod does ("<[head]Steve> hi", after any timestamp or rank). It finds the sender behind ranks and prefixes, by account name, tab-list name or Lads nickname. Options: Position (Before name, the default, or Start of line, which puts the head at the start of the message), Detect by name, and Keep text aligned (for Start of line).
- **Better F3 (all versions):** the F3 screen sorted into Performance, Position, World, Target and System sections, with coloured labels, the FPS in green, yellow or red, a slide-in animation, an optional background, and a switch for each section. Lines added by OptiFine, Forge and other mods are kept. This is new on 1.8.9 and 26.3.
- **Custom FOV (all versions):** sliders from 0% to 100% for how much sprinting, Speed and Slowness, flying, aiming a bow, the spyglass (26.x) and being underwater change your FOV, plus Static FOV. At 100% everything behaves as it does in vanilla.
- **Mouse Tweaks (all versions):** right-drag to drop one item in each slot, left-drag to gather matching items, shift-drag to move stacks, and scroll to move items one at a time between inventories. Every change goes through the game's normal clicks, so it works on servers.
- **IgnorePacketErrors (all versions, on by default):** a packet the game can't read, or one whose handler fails, is skipped instead of kicking you with "Network Protocol Error". Timeouts, lost connections and kicks still disconnect you as before.
- **Ctrl+R on the multiplayer screen** refreshes the server list (all versions). It does nothing while you are typing in a text box.
- **Jasione (all versions, on by default):** the game and mods no longer make a new copy of an enum's values each time they only read them, which cuts memory churn. A change applies after a restart.
- **Async logging (all versions, built in):** the game writes its log on a background thread, and every line still reaches latest.log, even when the game crashes.
- **Pack defaults (launcher, all versions):** files in a `configureddefaults` folder are copied into the game folder when they are missing. Missing `options.txt` keys are added, and nothing you changed is overwritten.
- **Dynamic Lights on 26.2 and 26.3** is now built into Lads (Light Radius, Fast or Fancy, Entities, Dropped Items, Underwater). Held torches, lava buckets, glowstone, burning mobs, blazes, glow squids and more light up their surroundings. On 1.8.9 the module turns OptiFine's own Dynamic Lights on and off, and stays in step with OptiFine's Video Settings.
- **1.8.9: Raised** (the hotbar and its bars sit 2 px higher, more while chat is open) and **durability tooltips** (Numbers, Bar and Text styles) are built in. While chat is open, chat moves up with the raised hotbar, so the hotbar does not cover the newest chat line (as on 26.2 and 26.3).

## Changed
- **HUD editor** on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3:
  - A toolbar across the top (Group, Ungroup, Lock, Snap, Done) and a list of HUD elements on the left with search, a settings gear and an on/off switch for each one, plus Colors and Reset.
  - Only switched-on elements are listed and previewed. Show disabled (or the A key) adds the others, dimmed, so you can switch them on from the list.
  - Selecting in the list selects in the preview and the other way round.
  - A plain drop still snaps and lines up against other HUDs but never groups. Hold Shift while dropping to group. The Group and Ungroup buttons, the right-click menu, Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G work as before. G toggles snapping.
  - Your saved layouts and groups from 1.6.0 carry over unchanged.
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak** replaces the separate Toggle Sprint and Toggle Sneak modules. Your 1.6.0 settings move over by themselves: on/off, modes, Disable on sneak, favourite, HUD position, lock and groups. The HUD line reads like "[Sprinting (Toggled)] [Sneaking (Key Held)]".
- **Toggle states are remembered.** A toggled sprint or sneak stays on through death, respawn, changing worlds, servers or dimensions, disconnecting, restarting the game and switching the module off and on.
- **Toggle Sprint no longer forces sprinting.** It acts like holding the Sprint key, so Minecraft's own rules (hunger, blindness, walls, water, using items) decide when you sprint, exactly as with a held key.
- **Scoreboard colours.** The sidebar now shows the server's colours (on 26.x the colours were always lost before). Its Text Shadow option no longer depends on the global HUD shadow switch.
- **Armor HUD** moves to the new Hotbar Slots style for everyone, including existing configs. If you have Attach to hotbar off, your own position is kept. Pick Style: List to get the 1.6.0 look back.
- **1.7 Animations:**
  - Eating and drinking never show a swing, on every version.
  - Blocking, the bow and eating now start straight away while you are mining a block, like 1.7. Vanilla ignored the use key until you stopped mining. This checks both hands on 26.x, so a shield or food in the offhand also starts while you mine.
  - With Legacy Swing on, blocks you hold (including torches, flowers and rails) keep vanilla's placing animation and only Legacy Swing animates them.
- **Title screen:** the rows are now Lads Mods | Mods, Options | Screenshots, More... | Quit Game. Mods, Screenshots and Accounts are no longer in More, which keeps Realms, Language and (on 26.x) Friends, Accessibility, Credits and Open Replays. On 1.8.9 the Screenshots button shows while BetterScreenshots is on.
- **Lads menu, Installed mods:** mods are shown in two columns, like the module cards. Narrow windows fall back to one column.
- **1.8.9 world loading is much faster.** Opening a world, going through a portal and leaving no longer wait on forced memory clean-ups or slow polling. In testing: opening a world went from about 1.5 s to 0.35 s, the Nether from 0.57 s to 0.13 s, back to the Overworld from 0.48 s to 0.05 s, and leaving from 0.67 s to 0.24 s.
- **Rogue ACE (5 kills) kill banner:** the head now drops in with its ring and stays centred in it, as in kills 1 to 4.
- **Bundled mods.** NoPackCompatCheck is removed on 26.2 and 26.3; No Resource Pack Warnings, which is still included, already hides the outdated-pack warning. Updated on 26.3: Balm 26.3.0.3, C2ME 0.4.2-alpha.0.89, Collective 8.41, Fzzy Config 0.7.7+fix3, Iris 1.11.7, JEI 31.9.0.56 (beta: JEI only publishes betas for 26.3), MezzConfig 0.6.8, MoreCulling 1.9.0 (now a stable release) and OBE 1.1.50. Updated on 26.2: C2ME 0.4.2-alpha.0.56, Collective 8.41, MezzConfig 0.6.8, OBE 1.1.50 and SuperMartijn642's Config Lib 1.1.8a. Chat Heads, Jasione and the other mods Lads now builds in are no longer downloaded (see Highlights).
- Singleplayer worlds get a small new file, `lads-cheats.properties`, that remembers whether players who join may use commands.
- **Base now plays Valorant's default kill sounds**, one per kill, instead of the experience-orb chime. Your saved choice of Base, Reaver, Rogue or Custom is kept.
- The Kill Banner settings show the chosen skin's variants, and Custom's banner and sound choices, above the list of skins, so you no longer scroll past every skin to reach them. Skins are shown four to a row.
- Text Color is no longer listed for Kill Banner: it only coloured the old Base banner's label.
- With Item Physics on, it lays dropped items down itself, so 1.7 Animations' 2D dropped items stand aside until it is switched off.
- **AppleSkin** has been rewritten as Lads' own code. Holding food shows a pulsing preview of the hunger, saturation and health it will give. Your current saturation shows as gold outlines on the hunger bar, with an exhaustion band behind it. Food tooltips show icons on 26.x and a text line on 1.8.9, and there is a food line on F3. Off-hand food is supported on 26.x. On 26.x servers that run AppleSkin, the exact saturation and exhaustion the server sends are used, as before.
- **AutoReconnect** has been rewritten. Kicks, bans, whitelist refusals and duplicate logins no longer count down to reconnect, and timeouts and lost connections now do. If you edited your reconnect lists, your own lists are kept. Realms still reconnect on 26.x: a retry joins the Realm again the way the Realms screen does. The `/ladsreconnect` command and the one-time import of the old AutoReconnect mod's settings were removed; the editors are still in the module's options.
- **Raised and Paper Doll** have been rewritten as Lads' own code. Raised now has Hotbar (2 px by default), Distance (how far up the hotbar goes while chat is open) and Chat options. A custom 1.6.0 Raised layout keeps only the hotbar and chat heights. Raised is now on by default on 1.8.9 too. In Paper Doll, a Display Time of 0 now means "only while the action lasts"; use Always Display to keep the doll on screen.
- **Durability tooltips have a new look** on every version. The durability line is now always one line: *Numbers* shows "Uses left: 1461 / 1561", *Bar* shows a gauge of 20 pips with a percentage, and *Text* shows a condition word for each quarter of the item's life ("Like new", "Good", "Worn", "Battered", "About to break"). With Varying colours the line follows the colour of the item's own durability bar. Excluded Mods also takes single items (`mod:item`). Your durability settings are kept.
- **Chat signing:** the "Chat messages can't be verified" toast is hidden as well as the signing tags. Hide Signing Indicators (on by default) no longer depends on the Chat module being on.
- **Dynamic Lights** now starts on, set to Fancy, with torches going out underwater, as LambDynamicLights did. The 1.6.0 settings card could never be changed, so untouched 1.6.0 settings are reset to these defaults. On 1.8.9 the module starts from OptiFine's saved setting.
- **Better F3** is now on by default on every version. Hide Inessential (on by default) also hides 26.x's "press F3+F6" hint.
- **Render Scale is now called Better Resolution**, and its settings move across automatically.
- **1.8.9 Crosshair Tweaks:** Dynamic Attack Gap now follows the arm swing. Before, it did nothing.
- **1.8.9 borderless:** the window is your monitor's size plus one row off screen. F2 screenshots taken while borderless include that extra row, as on 26.x.

## Fixed
- **Sprint flicker.** On 1.8.9 every hit, wall or item use made the sprint stop and start again, sending a pair of packets each time. No version forces sprinting any more, and after Minecraft stops a toggled sprint it waits a quarter of a second before asking for it again, which also removes the stop/start pairs at water edges. In-game tests on all three versions saw at most one sprint packet per real stop or start.
- **Cheats in singleplayer did not stick.** Essential kept its own per-world cheats switch and put it back every time the world opened, so turning cheats on in World Options, Multiplayer Options or Open to LAN was undone on the next visit (on 26.2 and 26.3 the World Options switch did not even work during the session). The world's own setting now wins, every change you make is passed on to Essential, and the "allow commands for other players" choice is saved per world. On 1.8.9, Open to LAN with Allow Cheats now also turns your own cheats on and saves them with the world.
- **Minimap flickered with the HUD FPS cap on (26.2 and 26.3).** Xaero's minimap was missing on every frame between HUD redraws. It now shows on every frame, and the cap keeps its benefit.
- **Auto Hide HUD:** on 26.x the hotbar frame came back fully visible between HUD redraws while its items stayed faded. The frame, selected slot, offhand, items, counts, durability bars and attack indicator now fade and hide together. On 1.8.9 Auto Hide HUD did nothing before; it now fades the hotbar, items, status bars, XP bar and the Lads HUD together.
- **1.8.9: two boss bars.** With the Lads Boss Bar module on, Forge's own boss bar is no longer drawn as well.
- **26.2 and 26.3: the 1.7 Animations Instant sneak camera could drop twice** after a quick sneak tap on a server. It now follows your own sneak key.
- **26.2 and 26.3: Vertical Bobbing snapped level** the moment you started flying, swimming or riding. It now eases back to level, the same at any frame rate.
- **Gaps in 3D held and dropped items.** Item models now get the same small edge overlap Forge uses, on 26.2 and 26.3 (also with Sodium) and for models Minecraft's own generator makes on 1.8.9 (such as OptiFine custom item textures).
- **1.8.9: the inventory no longer slides to the right when you have a potion effect.** The survival and creative inventories stay centred on the screen, and the effect list is still drawn to their left. In a narrow window or at a large GUI scale, where the full list does not fit, the effects become a column of small icons (hover one for its name and time), so they never cover the inventory.
- **The Base kill banner was a placeholder** (a flat picture with a "KILL" label). It is now Valorant's real default banner, with its emblem, ring and a pip for each kill, drawn at the same size and place as Reaver and Rogue.
- **1.8.9 Borderless Fullscreen showed black in OBS and Discord screen share.**
- **Better Resolution (26.x): the sky, sun, moon and stars were missing at scales other than 100%.** With Iris they were drawn in the wrong place at 50% and 200%.
- **Iris shader packs failed to load on 26.2 and 26.3** ("GLSL source pre-processing failed") because of the class transformer that came with Jasione and LibJF. Both jars are gone now. This has not yet been checked again with a real shader pack.
- **1.8.9: leaving a singleplayer world could freeze the game (Not Responding)** when the world's server stopped on its own at the same moment, for example after a disconnect or kick. The game now stops waiting once the server has stopped.

## Known limitations
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak:** after Minecraft stops a toggled sprint, it takes a quarter of a second (5 ticks) before sprinting is asked for again, where a held key retries on the next tick. A separate Sneak (Toggle) key has only been unit-tested, not tried in game. Leaving and rejoining a world without restarting was not tested on 26.x.
- **Toggle Sprint & Sneak migration:** if only Toggle Sneak was on in 1.6.0, the new module is on with Sprint set to Vanilla. If both were off, it stays off and starts from the new defaults.
- **Scoreboard:** every upgraded config shows the scoreboard's own colours once. If you chose a scoreboard colour in 1.6.0, turn Custom Text Color back on.
- **Simple Voice Chat HUD** was tested with one player. The deafened icon was real; talking, whispering, muted, disconnected and the group list were checked with sample data. There is no Simple Voice Chat for 1.8.9, so both elements are unavailable there.
- **Infinite chat** stops at 100,000 messages. The oldest messages past that point are dropped.
- **HUD editor** was checked in game only at GUI scale 2. At GUI scale 4 on a 1280x720 window the list shows about three rows at a time (search and scrolling still work). On 1.8.9, vanilla's "Press E to open your inventory" tip can cover the right end of the toolbar.
- **Cheats:** on 26.x, Create World with Allow Commands and Essential's own cheats toggle were not tried in game, and no second player ever joined a test world. On 26.x, /publish and Essential's hosting still set guest cheats for that session only.
- **1.7 Animations:** the 1.8.9 fly, swim and ride camera still settles in about two ticks as stock 1.8.9 does; the slower ease is 26.x only. The sneak camera fix could not be seen in single player, where there is no network delay, and has not been tried on a real server. 1.7 Animations sneak on 26.x keeps 26.x's camera height (the 1.8 height would put the camera inside blocks under low ceilings).
- **3D Skin Layers:** only your own player was photographed in testing. Other players use the same code.
- **Item gap fix:** no gaps were visible in testing before the fix either, so the screenshots only show nothing broke. The Sodium part could not be confirmed in a running game. On 1.8.9, normal items already had Forge's fix and look the same.
- **1.8.9 loading times** were measured on a busy test computer and joining a multiplayer server was not timed.
- **Title screen** layout at other window sizes and GUI scales is covered by layout tests only. On very cramped screens the Switch button shows only its icon.
- **Minecraft 1.21.11** gets no new testing. It still builds with this release and keeps NoPackCompatCheck and the mods Lads now builds in on 26.2 and 26.3 (Chat Heads, Mouse Tweaks and the others); the new Lads modules show there as not connected.
- The new skins are built from Kingdom Archives' still images, so they appear with a short scale-in and fade out. Only Reaver and Rogue play their full in-game animation.
- A sixth kill shows the five-kill banner (the sixth-kill art and sounds a few skins have are not included).
- Custom's sound list no longer has the old chime: picking Base there plays the default Valorant kill sound.
- The Lads Client jar is about 14 MB larger with the new art and sounds.
- **Item Physics** was not tried on a real multiplayer server, with shaders, or together with 1.7 Animations' 2D dropped items. Right-click pickup only applies to the host in a LAN world.
- **Launcher translations** come from Google Translate and can be off for short labels with no context. A few fixed-width dropdowns cut long words short. Translating needs internet the first time you pick a language.
- **Borderless on 1.8.9:** the black capture could not be reproduced on the test PC. The old window was captured fine in most runs, and the new window was captured by all four Windows capture methods in every run, but OBS and Discord themselves were not tested. Please tell us if it is still black. Launching straight into fullscreen from the launcher was not tested; pressing F11 was.
- **Flashback Settings:** OpenH264 to GPU is off by default because NVENC was slower than OpenH264 on the test PC; it may help on PCs with a weak CPU. Browse... and the GPU fallback were not tested. Flashback Settings is not available on 1.8.9.
- **AppleSkin on servers:** exact values in singleplayer, in LAN worlds you host and (26.x) on servers that run AppleSkin. On other servers, and on every server on 1.8.9, there is no exhaustion band and saturation updates only when your health or hunger changes. Reading AppleSkin's server data was checked with injected test data, not on a real AppleSkin server. On 1.8.9 food tooltips are text only.
- **Dynamic Lights:** creeper and TNT flashes, beacon and guardian beams, item frames, the Glowing effect and resource-pack light overrides are not lit. Iris with a shader pack is untested. On 1.8.9 only OptiFine's Off, Fast and Fancy are available.
- **Raised:** free groups, per-layer anchors and moving the boss bar, scoreboard, effects, tab list, titles and toasts were not remade (Lads' HUD editor covers those). The built-in Hovering Hotbar still adds its own 2 px on 26.x, as in 1.6.0. While chat is open, the raised health and food bars are drawn over the chat line just above the newest one, on every version.
- **Mouse Tweaks:** settings from the old `MouseTweaks.cfg` are not imported. On 1.8.9 it does nothing in the creative inventory. Left-drag gathering skips a stack that would overflow your cursor.
- **Chat Heads:** with Before name, a message that wraps leaves the head's width free on every line, not only the first. If the sender's name is not on a message's first line, the head goes at its start. On 1.8.9 the sender is found by name only.
- **Custom FOV** starts at 100% (vanilla) everywhere; settings from the old Custom FOV mod are not imported. Better F3 has fixed section colours plus Rainbow, not a colour picker for each section.
- **AutoReconnect** was tested against a local world and a closed port, not a real remote server or Realm.
- **IgnorePacketErrors** can keep you connected to a broken or hostile server, and the world may then get out of sync. Switch it off if that happens.
- **Better Resolution** was tested with a synthetic GPU-heavy shader pack, not a real one such as Complementary. With an OptiFine shader pack on 1.8.9 it stays off, as Render Scale did.
- **Jasione (26.x):** if a mod adds constants to an enum while the game is running, after that enum was first used, the mod may still see the old list. A change to the Jasione switch applies after a restart.
- **Async logging:** if the game is killed outright (Task Manager, a power cut), the last log lines still waiting to be written can be lost. A normal quit and a crash keep every line.
- **Pack defaults** live in the launcher, not in the game: files in `configureddefaults` are copied in before each launch, only when they are missing.
- **In-game checks ran on one test PC.** Other hardware was not tested.
