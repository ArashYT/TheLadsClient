# Lads Client 1.13.15

Parties, best friends and pinned worlds, a calmer chat panel, and ModSync that reuses its modpack.

## Highlights

- **Party**: invite friends to a party; the leader can take everyone to any server, and each member chooses whether to follow.
- **Best friends** and **pinned worlds**.
- **ModSync reuses its modpack**: the same world keeps one modpack and only updates its mods.

## Added

- **Party tab** on the Friends screen (invite, promote, kick, leave, take the party to a server address or the server you are on).
- **Best Friend** in a friend's menu: a star, listed first on the Friends screen and in its own section in the chat sidebar.
- **Pin worlds** on the world list: a pin on each world row keeps it at the top.
- **Hide installed** in the launcher's Add Content tab.

## Changed

- **Chat panel** slides out slower and softer, with a lighter dim behind it.
- **Friends screen** has no dark background: the menu panorama shows behind it (on 26.3), or your world in game.
- **Modpack dependencies** are installed for every mod in a modpack, from the mod sites' own dependency lists, including CurseForge imports and updates.
- **ModSync** keeps one modpack per friend's world and updates its mods in place instead of making a new pack each time.
- **Correction**: 1.13.14's "Faster loading" note overstated it. Measured start time did not change; the memory settings are in place but gave no measurable speedup.

## Fixed

- **Friends in the chat sidebar** show their skins instead of a letter.
- **ModSync restart**: after the game closes to load a friend's mods, a "Starting Minecraft" window now shows right away until the friend's modpack opens. Before, nothing was on screen for about a minute, so it looked as if no restart happened.
- **Launcher opened twice**: opening the launcher while it already runs (hidden in the tray) now brings that one back instead of starting a second launcher, which could start the same game twice.
- **Party**: the leader's toast says who joined their party (it said "You joined the party").
- **World pins** sit under the world's size instead of on top of its play time.
