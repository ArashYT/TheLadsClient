# The Lads Client 1.7.1

## Highlights
- **Discord Rich Presence, in the game and the launcher.** Your Discord profile now shows what you are doing in The Lads Client: the menu you are in, singleplayer or the server you play on, and how long you have been playing. It works on Minecraft 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3.

## Added
- **In game (DiscordRPC module, on by default).** Every screen has its own line on Discord: the main menu, the server list, world selection, every options page, the Lads menu, the HUD editor, Screenshots, the pause menu, your inventory, chests, crafting, furnaces, enchanting, anvils, trading, books, signs, the death screen, loading and connecting screens, and more. Screens from other mods show by their own name. In a world, Discord shows "Playing singleplayer" or "Playing on mc.hypixel.net", with the open screen underneath.
- **In the launcher.** Discord shows the page you are on (Home, Worlds, Servers, Modpacks, Profiles, Accounts, Skins, Settings, Mods and each Mods and Packs tab, Files, Gallery, Logs), any open dialog by its title, and "Launching Minecraft 26.2" while a game starts. Once the game is running the launcher steps aside and the game's own presence takes over.
- A **Get The Lads Client** button on your Discord activity links to ladsclient.arashyt.ca.
- **Settings → Behavior → "Show what I'm doing on Discord"** turns the launcher's presence off. In game, switch off the DiscordRPC module in the Lads menu (or on the launcher's Mods page).

## Changed
- **Privacy.** Server addresses are only shared as host names (mc.hypixel.net); IP addresses, localhost and LAN names show as "Playing multiplayer". World names and dimensions stay hidden unless you turn on Share world name or Show dimension. Detail Level Simple shows only menus, singleplayer or multiplayer; Minimal shows only "Playing Minecraft". No usernames, coordinates or account details are ever sent.
- Discord presence talks to the Discord desktop app directly on your PC. It needs no account sign-in, and if Discord is closed nothing happens until you open it.
- Minecraft 1.21.11 (no longer updated) shows Discord presence as unavailable.

## Fixed
- The DiscordRPC module no longer shows "Coming soon" and can be switched on and off like any other module.
