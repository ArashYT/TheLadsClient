# Lads Client 1.13.21

Friend invites are easier to notice, ModSync keeps a friend's world from crashing on mismatched mods, and logs can go straight to the Lads team.

## Highlights

- **Invites**: a game, server or party invite plays a short, quiet chime and shows its whole message (long world names wrap instead of being cut off).
- **Joining after Join**: pressing Join on an invite shows "Joining: Waiting for <friend> to let you in..." until the connection starts or fails.
- **ModSync checks versions**: when the host has another version of one of your mods, the host's version is used (no more two copies of the same mod). Your own mods made for your old version (for example Better Render Distance with another Sodium) are left out of that world's modpack instead of crashing the game. The Logs page lists every upgraded, downgraded, new, removed and left-out mod.
- **Send logs to Lads**: a "Send to Lads" button on the Logs page and on every modpack's Logs tab sends the game log and launcher log to the Lads team.

## Fixed

- **Modpacks show "Running" correctly**: with "Allow multiple instances" on, closing one copy of a modpack no longer marks it closed while another copy still runs.
