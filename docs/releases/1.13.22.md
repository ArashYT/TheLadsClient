# Lads Client 1.13.22

The launcher gets a Friends tab, and ModSync no longer crashes friends whose modpack uses its own version of a Lads mod.

## Highlights

- **Friends in the launcher**: a new Friends tab in the launcher sidebar shows what your friends are playing, with a badge for pending invites.

## Added

- New Friends tab in the launcher: see what your friends are playing, join their world or server, accept invites, and invite them while you're playing.

## Fixed

- ModSync: a friend's modpack that uses its own version of a Lads mod (like Sodium) now syncs that version too, so joining no longer crashes.
