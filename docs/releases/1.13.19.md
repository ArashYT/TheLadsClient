# Lads Client 1.13.19

A friend's modpack now drops the mods you removed, and Multiplayer Options can invite friends.

## Highlights

- **Removed mods leave the friend's pack**: when you remove a mod and a friend rejoins, their game restarts once, the mod is gone from their "<world> - <host>" modpack, and they join your world.
- **Invite friends from Multiplayer Options**: the Players tab lists your online friends with an Invite button.

## Fixed

- The 1.13.18 removal never ran in a game that was already running the friend's modpack, and not in packs made before 1.13.18. Both now work.
