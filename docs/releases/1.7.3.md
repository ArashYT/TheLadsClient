# The Lads Client 1.7.3

1.7.3 brings together two updates: a big speed-up for Minecraft 1.8.9 and everything planned for 1.7.2 (animated kill banners, Async, HUD FPS cap fixes, key bind fixes and more). There was no separate 1.7.2 release.

## Highlights
- **1.8.9 runs much faster, and looks exactly the same.** Players, mobs and chests hidden behind walls are no longer drawn (Entity Culling), armour, signs and name tags are no longer rebuilt every frame, the HUD FPS cap now really saves time, kill banners no longer cost frames, and a frame-pacing wait is gone. In our test scenes this was up to about 30% more FPS in crowds and sign-heavy lobbies, and half the work per frame with a crowd behind a wall. Screenshots before and after match pixel for pixel.
- **Unfocused FPS cap (1.8.9):** while you're tabbed out, the game drops to 60 FPS to save power.
- **Every kill banner is animated** like Reaver and Rogue, on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3, and quick multi-kills on servers are no longer lost (new Streak Reset timer, default 45 s, or Unlimited).
- **No more HUD flicker with the HUD FPS cap**, and HUD animations play at 60 FPS while they move.
- **Async is back** (26.2 and 26.3, Experimental, on by default) and now switches itself off whenever it isn't faster.
- **1.8.9 key binds no longer reset**, and Mouse 4/5 work for every key bind.
- **Directional damage tilt**, swinging while eating, drinking, drawing a bow or blocking, reset confirmations, and a live Fullbright slider in 5% steps.

## Added
- **Entity Culling (1.8.9, on by default, under Performance in the Lads menu).** Players, mobs, armour stands, dropped items, chests, signs, skulls and banners hidden behind solid blocks are no longer drawn. A background thread works out what is hidden, so the game itself does almost no extra work. In our test (155 players, armour stands and mobs and 25 chests behind a wall) the game's work per frame halved, from 4.4 ms to 2.1 ms, and the GPU's from 2.6 ms to 0.6 ms. With the crowd in plain view nothing is skipped and the cost stays the same (3.7 ms against 4.0 ms).
- Nothing you can see changes. Name tags still show through walls exactly as before. Anything that might be visible, even a sliver round a corner, or while you strafe, peek or flick your view, is drawn. Screenshots of the same scenes with Entity Culling off and on are identical, also with shaders on.
- Always drawn: beacons, leads, fishing lines, guardian beams, bosses, very large mobs, anything within 2 blocks, and anything you ride. It works with OptiFine, shaders (shadows are never culled), 3D Skin Layers, Essential cosmetics, 1.7 Animations, Item Physics and Dynamic Lights.
- **ParticleBudget now works on 1.8.9** (off by default, as on 26.2 and 26.3). It limits only decorative particles (smoke, mycelium spores, and void and underwater specks) to "Particles per tick" within "Distance". Hits, crits, potions and every other gameplay particle are never limited.
- **Unfocused FPS cap (1.8.9, on by default).** While the game window is not in front, the frame rate limit drops to 60 FPS, which saves power and leaves the CPU and GPU to the program you are using. It only ever lowers your own limit, never raises it. The title screen is unchanged (1.8.9 already limits a menu without a world to 30). You can turn it off or change the limit (10 to 240) in the Lads menu, Performance, "UnfocusedFpsCap". On 26.2 and 26.3 Dynamic FPS does this job.
- **Async (26.2 and 26.3, Experimental, on by default):** in singleplayer and in a world you open to LAN, mobs, dropped items and experience orbs that are far apart now tick on several CPU cores at once, so big farms and mob-heavy bases lag less. In our stress test (about 2,300 mobs and items on one platform) a server tick took about 15 to 55% less time with Async on. Mobs behave exactly as before: they wander and find paths, villagers work, pick up food and claim job sites, hostile mobs hunt and attack, skeletons shoot, items merge, animals breed, far-away mobs despawn, pressure plates and redstone react to them, and leashed animals and riders stay attached. Players, riders and their mounts, leashed animals, projectiles, mobs in portals, bosses, raids and a few special mobs (Endermen, Shulkers, Bees, Allays, Dolphins, Creakings, Copper Golems, Happy Ghasts) always tick the normal way. Async checks itself every 30 seconds: whenever ticking normally is as fast or faster on your PC (for example while other programs keep the CPU busy), it switches to normal ticking, writes one line to the log, and tries again a few minutes later. If anything ever goes wrong, it also writes one line to the log and goes back to normal ticking until you rejoin the world. Settings: Min entities (below this many entities in a dimension it does nothing, 300 by default) and Threads (Auto uses the cores the game may run on, minus two for the game's own main threads). It never runs on a server you join, and it steps aside if the separate Async mod is installed. Not on 1.8.9, where the Lads menu lists it as unavailable with the reason.
- **Every reset asks first.** The HUD editor's **Reset** button, a module's **Reset options** button and Flashback's **Reset** (replay folder) now open a small box, "Are you sure you want to reset?", that says what will be reset. **Confirm** (or Enter) resets; **Cancel** (or Esc, or a click outside the box) changes nothing. The same box is used everywhere, on Minecraft 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3.
- **Type an exact number on a slider.** Percentage sliders now have their value in a small field beside the slider: click it, type a whole number, press Enter (a number out of range or anything that is not a whole number is refused and the slider keeps its value). Typed numbers land on the nearest step, like the slider does. It is on Fullbright's Gamma, AppleSkin's Overlay Opacity, the Paper Doll's Model Opacity and the four Crosshair opacity sliders.
- **Fullbright shows the world.** Its settings preview is now the live game view, so you see the brightness change as you drag.
- **Directional damage tilt (Old Damage Tilt module), on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3.** When a mob, a player or an arrow hits you, the camera can now tilt towards the side the hit came from: a hit from the left or right rolls the view that way, a hit from the front or behind tips it forward or back. Minecraft 1.8.9 never tells your game where a hit came from, so Lads works it out from the knockback the server sends with the hit. Damage with no direction (falling, fire, poison) gets the old fixed tilt on every version.
  - **Directional**: off gives the old tilt that always leans the same way. New installs start with it on; see Changed for existing setups.
  - **Intensity** 0 to 100%: how far the camera tilts. 0 turns the tilt off. On 26.2 and 26.3 it scales Minecraft's own Damage Tilt setting (Accessibility), so 100% is exactly what that setting gives and Minecraft's 0% still means no tilt.
- **1.7 Animations: Swing while using items.** Attacking while you eat, drink, draw a bow or block now swings your arm over the use, like 1.7's blockhitting, on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3. It is only drawn on your screen: the eating, drawing or blocking carries on as before, food is eaten on time, the bow shoots when you let go, and nothing extra is sent to the server. On by default with 1.7 Animations (option "Swing while using items"; blocking follows "Blockhitting").
- **Kill streak timer.** Kill Banner has a new "Streak Reset (seconds)" setting: how long your streak waits for the next kill before it starts again at 1. It is 45 seconds by default (it used to be a fixed 8 seconds). Type any whole number from 1 to 600; anything else keeps the value you had, and the settings page tells you so.
- **Unlimited Streak** (off by default): your streak never times out. It only starts again when you die, when you join another world (a new dimension, or the next game on Hypixel) or when you leave the server.

## Changed
- **1.8.9: faster rendering of armour, signs and name tags, with no change to how anything looks.**
  - Armour: the game no longer rebuilds each armour piece's texture name every frame (with OptiFine this also went through a slow
    Forge lookup). In a test crowd of 85 armoured players and armour stands the FPS went up by about 28 % (143 to 183 FPS) and each
    frame took about 1.3 ms less to draw.
  - Signs: sign text is no longer re-wrapped and re-formatted every frame. In a test wall of 200 signs the FPS went up by about 30 %
    (342 to 447 FPS) and the slowest frames got much quicker. Edited signs update straight away.
  - Name tags on players and holograms no longer build hidden chat data every frame just to draw the name.
  - Works with and without OptiFine, and with Essential, Resourcify and 3D Skin Layers. Screenshots taken with the change off
    and on match pixel for pixel.
- **1.8.9: particles outside your view are not drawn** while Entity Culling is on. The check runs every frame, so nothing on screen changes, even when you turn quickly.
- **1.8.9: the HUD FPS cap now really saves time.** With the cap on, the Lads HUD is drawn into an offscreen image at the cap's rate and shown as one picture on every frame in between, instead of being drawn again piece by piece every frame. In our test scene (every Lads HUD element, a 15-line scoreboard and chat, cap at 10 FPS, no FPS limit) the Lads HUD took about 80 microseconds per frame instead of about 250 with the old cap, and the game ran about 12% faster (899 instead of 800 FPS on average). The paper doll and armour items (with their enchantment shine) are still drawn live every frame, so they move as smoothly as before. The HUD looks the same: text, shadows and see-through backgrounds blend as before, with no darker edges or halos. The cap stays off by default. With OptiFine's Fast Render or antialiasing on, which turn off the offscreen images, the cap works the old way.
- **1.8.9: text is measured faster.** Text that stays the same from frame to frame (scoreboard, tab list, nametags, chat, HUD labels) is measured once instead of every frame, which in our test made measuring a line 3-12 times faster. It works with the normal font, the Unicode font and OptiFine HD and custom fonts, and changing resource packs, language or the Unicode setting refreshes it.
- **1.8.9: the Lads Scoreboard is read once per game tick** instead of three or more times every frame, and its lines are only measured again when they change. It looks the same.
- **1.8.9: Autohide gives back its offscreen image** 10 seconds after the last fade and when you leave a world, instead of keeping a window-sized image for the rest of the session.
- **1.8.9: smoother frames, less waiting.** The game no longer waits for the graphics card to finish the previous frame before it reads your mouse (the "frame pacing" step). The frame rate goes up by roughly 10 % in a CPU-bound scene; the mouse still turns the camera at the very end of the frame's preparation, as before.
- **1.8.9: Minecraft's own graphics-error checking is off after the game has started.** Vanilla asked the driver for errors after most render steps, which costs a little on every frame. Nothing about how the game looks changes; OptiFine and the other mods in the pack do not turn it back on.
- **1.8.9: lighter Lads HUD.** The HUD, Autohide's fade, Better Resolution, AppleSkin's icons, Signal Loss, the crosshair and the kill banner no longer ask the driver for its state every frame, and the HUD no longer builds new objects, text and lookups for every frame. It looks exactly the same. (Numbers for how much lighter it is come from the final benchmark of the merged build.)
- **Toggle Sprint and Toggle Sneak no longer hitch the game.** Pressing the toggle key used to write the whole config file to disk on the game thread (Windows could stall for a moment). The write now happens a moment later in the background, one write for a burst of presses, and is always finished when you quit the game. Lads' module list for the launcher (the Mods page) is written the same way. (1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3.)
- Better Resolution does not look for OptiFine's shader state while it is off, and its upscale shaders look up their settings once instead of every frame.
- **Kill banners no longer cost frames while they play (1.8.9).** Reaver's and Rogue's banners used to unpack, recolour and re-upload a whole picture every time their animation moved on. That work now happens ahead of time on a background thread, so drawing a banner frame is a plain copy. In our test the time the banner takes out of each frame dropped from a worst case of 8 ms to 1.3 ms for a Reaver 4-kill banner, from 4.9 ms to 0.2 ms for a 5-kill Reaver, and from 1.6 ms to 0.1 ms for a 4-kill Rogue. Across a whole 5-kill Reaver banner the drawing cost fell from 279 ms to 24 ms, and a whole 4-kill Rogue banner from 101 ms to 9 ms. The banners look exactly the same: 568 test frames across 11 skins were compared pixel for pixel with the old version and none differ.
- The skin you pick now has all its pictures ready before your first kill: its sprites, Reaver's way-out pictures and the settings thumbnail are loaded in the background while you play, instead of on the first kill.
- Kill banner memory is freed completely after a minute without a banner: the frame picture, the pre-decoded frames, the thumbnails and Reaver's and Rogue's packed frames (about 10 MB for Reaver) all go, not only the sprites. They load again in the background the next time you pick the skin.
- **Every kill banner is animated now.** The 88 skins added in 1.7.0 (Aemondir to Xerøfang) and the Base banner play a Valorant-style animation like Reaver and Rogue, instead of only scaling in and fading out: the emblem drops into place, the frame and ring land around it, the kill pips fly in and flare in the skin's own colour as the kill mark hits, and the banner leaves the way Valorant's does (the frame goes, the emblem shrinks and darkens, then the ring and the pips).
- **More kills, more show:** from 2 kills a spray of sparks in the skin's colour bursts out to the sides and a glint runs around the ring. The 5-kill banner plays longer, with the glint circling three times and the pips turning once around the ring.
- **Champions 2024 and 2025** start on the previous kill's banner and turn into the new one as the kill mark lands.
- The **Kill Banner settings preview plays** the chosen banner, 1 to 5 kills in turn, instead of showing a still picture.
- The skin you pick (and Reaver's and Rogue's frames for every kill count) is loaded as soon as you choose it, so your first kill shows its banner without a stutter.
- Kill banner art that has not been shown for a minute is freed, so scrolling through every skin in the settings no longer keeps all of it in memory.
- **HUD FPS cap: HUD animations play at 60 FPS.** While something on the HUD moves, fades, grows or turns (Jade's tooltip fading in and out, a kill banner, the spyglass zoom, the night vignette, a sound subtitle fading), the HUD is redrawn at least 60 times a second even with the cap at 25 or 30. Numbers and text that just change (the FPS counter, effect timers, coordinates, the clock, new chat lines) still update at your cap, so the cap keeps its saving with those on screen.
- **Fullbright's Gamma is a percentage in steps of 5%** (0% is Minecraft's own brightest setting, 100% the brightest Fullbright goes, 65% is what the old default of 10 was). Dragging, the arrow keys and typing all stop on 5% steps and there are no raw decimals like 67.43%. Settings you saved before carry over (an old Gamma of 10 is 65%, anything else rounds to the nearest 5%).
- **1.8.9 with Lunar Client installed:** your key binds, field of view, sensitivity, GUI scale and sounds no longer go back to Lunar's 1.8 settings at every launch. A launch only brings in what you changed in Lunar (or in another Lads version) since the last one, and keeps what you set in 1.8.9. The Controls tab's note about Lunar says so now.
- **Old Damage Tilt is on by default** on every version.
- **Updating keeps your tilt's direction:** existing setups get Directional switched off, so the tilt keeps leaning the fixed way it did before. Switch Directional on in the Old Damage Tilt settings to try the new directional tilt. Old Damage Tilt itself is switched on for you unless you had changed it before (if you had switched it off, it stays off).
- **Old Damage Tilt's Intensity is a slider** (0 to 100%) instead of Subtle, Normal and Strong. Your choice carries over: Subtle becomes 50%, Normal and Strong become 100% (Minecraft's full tilt).
- Eating and drinking still never swing by themselves: a swing left over from mining or an earlier hit does not show on the food, only an attack you make while eating.
- **Every kill shows its own banner.** Kills that land at the same moment, or while a banner is still playing, now show 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 in turn, each with its own sound, instead of jumping straight to the last one. As in Valorant, the next kill's banner replaces the one playing, a quarter of a second after it started at the earliest.
- **Your death ends your streak on servers that never really kill you** (Hypixel and other minigames): a server message that you died ("You were killed by...", "YourName fell into the void.", The Pit's "DEATH!") starts your next streak at 1, as the death screen already did.
- Vanilla death messages are read in any game language, so kills count with the game set to German, Spanish, Japanese and the rest.
- On 1.8.9, a player you shoot, hook or knock into the void now counts when the server reports your kill. Mobs and bosses on 1.8.9 still need your own blow, because 1.8.9 servers don't tell the client who hit what.

## Fixed
- **HUD FPS cap: nothing on the HUD flickers any more (26.2, 26.3).** With the cap on, Jade's block tooltip, the sound subtitles, Item Physics' throw bar and every other mod HUD that Fabric draws last were only on screen on the frames the HUD was redrawn, so they flickered. They now stay on screen between redraws like the rest of the HUD. This also covers mods you add yourself.
- **HUD FPS cap: HUD animations keep their speed.** With the cap on, Jade's fade-in and fade-out, the spyglass zoom and the Lads FPS counter's smoothing ran slower the lower the cap (several times slower at 25 or 30). They now take as long as with the cap off, on 26.2, 26.3 and 1.8.9.
- **HUD FPS cap: pictures in the HUD** (Xaero's minimap, the paper doll, and any mod that draws its own picture into the HUD) are kept on screen between redraws in one shared way, so a mod that draws its picture only once per redraw no longer blinks, and the cap still saves redrawing them every frame.
- **1.8.9: with the HUD FPS cap on, the Armor HUD now follows Raised's lifted hotbar** as it does with the cap off (it sat 2 pixels too low).
- **Fullbright did not change the world until you closed the menu** on 26.2 and 26.3: the game rebuilds its light map only on its own tick, which does not run while a menu pauses the world, and BadOptimizations (in the pack) skips that rebuild until something in vanilla changes. Changing Fullbright (on or off, or Gamma) now rebuilds it in the same frame, menu open or not.
- **1.8.9: Fullbright's Gamma and Brightness Multiplier did nothing** (it was always at its maximum) and the game's own Brightness stayed raised after you switched Fullbright off. They now work, change live, and your Brightness comes back when Fullbright goes off.
- **1.8.9 key binds no longer reset.** Besides Lunar (above), the same happened without Lunar whenever the launcher was closed, or crashed, while you played: your 1.8.9 binds were replaced at the next launch. Your own binds now stay.
- **Mouse 4 and Mouse 5 on 1.8.9.** Pressing Mouse 4 while holding Shift, Ctrl or another mouse button (sneaking, sprinting, attacking, using an item) counted as Mouse 5, and Mouse 5 then stayed held down. This was a bug in the LWJGL version Minecraft 1.8.9 uses. Side buttons now work for every key bind (Drop, Lads Zoom, Toggle Sprint, OptiFine's zoom, Essential's keys, other mods' keys) and show as Button 4 and Button 5 in Controls.
- 1.8.9 saves options.txt in one go, so a crash, or the game closed from Task Manager while it saves, can no longer leave a cut-off file that resets the binds after the cut. Reset Keys in Controls is saved right away.
- Key binds of a mod that did not load for a session (for example Essential when it could not be downloaded, or a mod you turned off for a while) stay in 1.8.9's options.txt instead of being dropped, so they are still there when the mod is back.
- A key that one version has no name for (such as a 1.8.9 key typed on a non-English layout, or Mouse 6 and up) no longer breaks key binds when the launcher shares them: the other version keeps its own bind. Before, 1.8.9 went back to the default for that key, and on 26.2 and 26.3 it stopped the rest of the key binds and the sound settings from loading.
- F19 is shared between 26.x and 1.8.9 correctly.
- Opening Lads Screenshots on 1.8.9 with no screenshots no longer crashes the game when Take Screenshot is on a mouse button.
- **Old Damage Tilt did nothing on 1.8.9.** It now has the same Directional and Intensity settings as on 26.2 and 26.3.
- **26.2 and 26.3, with Old Damage Tilt and Directional on:** falling or burning right after a hit no longer tilts the camera towards that earlier hit (Minecraft reuses the last direction); it gets the fixed tilt, as on 1.8.9.
- **Quick kills on servers no longer go missing.** Killing the same player again within 5 seconds (The Bridge, The Pit, Sumo and other instant-respawn games) used to be ignored as a duplicate. Now every death counts once, and only real duplicates of the same death (its death animation, its health update and its kill message) are ignored.
- A kill message about a player who had just respawned could be ignored because of their previous death.
- More server kill messages are recognised: vanilla's "didn't want to live in the same world as", "walked into the danger zone due to", "went off with a bang due to a firework fired from... by", thorns kills, Bed Wars kill effects that end in "from" (such as "hit off by a love bomb from"), long item names and health numbers next to names.
- A missing or damaged kill banner picture no longer crashes the game in the middle of a fight. The banner is skipped and the problem is written to the log once.
- Reaver's and Rogue's frame decoders (native memory) are now released instead of staying until the game closes.
- **1.8.9: the game could crash at the loading screen** in rare cases while Forge measured text before the mods had loaded.

## Known limitations
- Reaver's purple smoke and Rogue's blood splash are part of their own recorded animations. Kingdom Archives only has still images of the other skins, so they use the shared animation (spray, glint and flares in each skin's colour) rather than effects of their own.
- With Randomize set to "Skin and variant" or "Chosen", a skin that has not been shown yet loads its art at the kill (in testing this took under 12 ms).
- On Minecraft 1.21.11, which no longer gets updates, these skins still only scale in and fade out.
- **Async:** right after the load on your PC changes, it can take up to about half a minute before Async notices that normal ticking is faster. With the /debug profiler or a metrics recording running, ticking stays normal while it records. If a mob ever needs a part of the world that is not loaded yet, Async switches itself off for the rest of that session (the game keeps running normally).
- Typed numbers are whole numbers only and are rounded to the slider's step (typing 67 on a 5% slider gives 65%).
- Stopwatch's "Reset timer" button and the Installed mods list's "Reset" (which only clears its search and filters) do not ask: they do not reset settings.
- On Minecraft 1.21.11, which no longer gets updates, none of this applies.
- The first launch after updating keeps whatever 1.8.9 has right now; changes made in Lunar Client or another version after that come in at the next launch.
- OptiFine's zoom key only zooms while Lads Zoom is off (Lads Zoom is the only zoom, as since 1.6.0); bind Lads Zoom to a side button instead.
- The side-button fix is for Windows, where the bug is. Minecraft 1.8.9 sees at most five mouse buttons on Windows.
- On 1.8.9 the direction comes from the knockback, so a hit that does not knock you back (Knockback Resistance, or a server that removes knockback) tilts the old fixed way.
- On 26.2 and 26.3, Minecraft's own tilt already follows the hit; with Old Damage Tilt now on and Directional off after updating, the tilt leans the fixed way until you switch Directional on.
- The swing while using items is only on your own screen; other players see what Minecraft shows them.
- Kill streaks and banners are cosmetic and only count what your game sees. A kill message in a server's own wording that names you as the killer without the usual words ("by", "killed", "slain", "fighting", "whilst"...) is not recognised.
- A very fast run of more than five kills shows five banners in a row; the streak still counts every kill.
- Only full solid blocks hide things. Glass, leaves, slabs, fences and other see-through or partial blocks never hide anything.
- A block that was just placed or broken, and a chunk that has just loaded, hides nothing for a second or two, until the game has drawn it.
- On 26.2 and 26.3 the client's built-in entity culling is unchanged and always on. The new Entity Culling switch is for 1.8.9 only.
- The gains show where there are many armoured players, signs or holograms (lobbies, PvP); an empty world runs as before.
- Measured on one PC (i7-13700KF, RTX 4060 Ti) with OptiFine shaders off.
- With the HUD FPS cap on, the paper doll and armour items are drawn on top of the other Lads HUD elements. This only shows if you move another element over them.
- With the HUD FPS cap on, see-through backgrounds can differ by 1 brightness level out of 255 (rounding in the offscreen image). This cannot be seen.
- The unfocused FPS cap is 1.8.9 only; 26.2 and 26.3 have Dynamic FPS.
- The speed-ups are measured on one PC in a flat test world; a busy world, a slower PC or a GPU-bound setup will see different numbers.
- When a kill banner is shown for the first time after you change skins, its first frame may be drawn one frame later than before while the background thread finishes decoding it. Nothing is waited for on the game thread.
- Occasional single frames of 4 to 10 ms still show up in the first half-second of a Reaver 1-kill banner in our measurements; the old version's numbers show a similar one in the same banner, and it is not a garbage collection pause. Its cause is not known yet.
