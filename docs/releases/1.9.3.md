# Lads Client 1.9.3

Lads Client 1.9.3 restores responsive vanilla swinging, brings true fullbright to pitch-black caves across every version without touching your brightness slider, adds folders and search to the resource pack menu, enhances Hypixel Auto Play Again with party awareness, expands module settings layouts, and includes small 1.8.9 tick cleanups.

## Highlights

- **Fullbright in pitch darkness across all versions.** Fullbright now overrides the lighting texture directly, illuminating pitch-black caves and light-level 0 areas to daylight brightness on 1.8.9, 1.20.1, and 26.3 while keeping your personal Brightness setting untouched.
- **Faster, responsive arm swinging.** Restored vanilla's halfway animation cut-off rule and removed 1.9.2's pending swing queue, allowing spam clicking to restart swings at full vanilla speed.
- **Transparent resource pack menu with search and folders.** The pack menu displays a clean transparent view over the game world or title screen, live search filtering by name and description, and subfolder navigation with persistence.
- **Party-aware Auto Play Again.** Auto Play Again now waits for your party members by default, adds configurable replay triggers (after death or when the game ends), and ignores Bed Wars respawn deaths.
- **Two-column module settings and scrollbar controls.** Settings screens without a visual preview now arrange options into two clean columns, and all settings menus support scrollbar thumb dragging, track click paging, and arrow key navigation.

## Added

- **Resource pack search bar**: filter available and selected packs simultaneously in real time by pack name and description.
- **Resource pack folder navigation**: organize packs into subfolders in the resource packs directory with in-game folder browsing and back navigation; folder selections save cleanly to your config.
- **Auto Play Again triggers**: choose between Replay when "After death" (default) or "When game ends".
- **Wait for party**: Auto Play Again option (on by default) ensures the client waits for your party before sending `/play`.
- **Two-column module settings layout**: modules without a visual preview display options across two columns.
- **Scrollbar and keyboard controls**: drag the scrollbar thumb, click the scrollbar track to page up or down, or use Up and Down arrow keys and Page Up and Down to scroll settings menus.

## Changed

- **Swing animation timing**: arm swing cut-off returned to vanilla's halfway mark (1.9.2's pending swing queue removed) so rapid attack clicking swings at vanilla rate.
- **Transparent pack menu default**: resource pack screen renders transparent by default with an option to restore the classic opaque background in the ResourcePacks module.
- **Fullbright lighting pipeline**: implemented via lightmap texture overrides rather than gamma increases, ensuring daylight brightness in zero-light caves without altering `options.txt` brightness.

## Fixed

- **Fullbright in pitch darkness**: caves and enclosed light-0 areas no longer stay dark or grey when Fullbright is enabled.
- **Auto Play Again on Bed Wars respawns**: temporary respawn deaths in Bed Wars no longer trigger an early play-again command while you are still in the game.
- **1.8.9 client tick overhead**: window title polling via LWJGL is throttled to once every 2 seconds, and OpenGL error check queries are latched after startup initialization.
