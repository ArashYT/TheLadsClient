# Lads Client 1.13.6

A small fix for your own skin.

## Highlights

- Your own slim skin and cape show properly again.

## Fixed

- **Own skin after a restart**: a skin picked in the Skin changer came back as the classic (wide-arm) model after restarting the game, even when it was set to Slim. The saved model is now kept (1.8.9, 1.20.1, 26.3).
- **Own cape**: with a Skin changer skin on 26.2 and 26.3, your cape disappeared on your side. It now stays.
