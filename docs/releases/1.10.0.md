# Lads Client 1.10.0

Lads Client 1.10.0 adds many features players know from Lunar, Badlion, Feather and OneClient: PvP visuals, a big set of HUD widgets, chat and inventory tools, and launcher tools for fixing problems. It also puts The Lads Network (`mc.arashyt.ca`) in every server list and keeps your launcher settings across restarts. "All versions" below means 1.8.9, 1.20.1 and 26.3.

## Highlights

- **Item textures on 1.8.9 without OptiFine** (1.8.9): resource-pack item, armor and enchantment-glint textures now show on the fast renderer.
- **PvP visuals** (all versions): Block Overlay, Hitboxes, Hit Color, Damage Tint, Freelook, ViewModel, Motion Blur, Menu Blur, Dark Mode and more.
- **30 new HUD widgets** (all versions): Combo Counter, PvP Info, TPS, Compass Strip, Media Player, Hearts, Inventory HUD and more.
- **Chat and inventory tools** (all versions): Compact Chat, chat tabs and search, Slot Lock, Inventory Search, Pack Profiles and a draggable server list.
- **Launcher tools**: Mod Bisect, one-click log sharing to mclo.gs, Game Repair, CurseForge modpack import and `lads://` join links.
- **The Lads Network and saved settings**: `mc.arashyt.ca` is pinned in every server list, and launcher settings no longer reset after a restart.
- **Server rules**: features that show extra info or help input are off on servers that ban them and on unknown servers; you can switch one on per server after a red warning.

## Added

- **Custom Item Textures** (1.8.9): shows OptiFine-format item packs without OptiFine: textures by item, damage or enchantment, armor textures and enchantment glints. On by default; custom models and animated clocks are not supported yet.
- **Custom Skies** (all versions): draws the skies a resource pack includes (OptiFine format), on by default. On 1.8.9 it works without OptiFine.
- **Block Overlay** (all versions): outline color and width, and an optional fill, for the block you look at.
- **Hitboxes** (all versions): entity hitboxes in your colors with eye height, look line, a hurt color and per-type filters. Through Walls follows server rules.
- **Hit Color** (all versions): the color and strength of the flash on hurt mobs and players.
- **Damage Tint** (all versions): red screen edges and a heartbeat sound while your health is low.
- **Glint Colorizer** (all versions): enchantment glint color and speed; Shiny Potions makes potions with effects glint.
- **Hide Mob Deaths** (all versions): dying mobs vanish at once; players still show their death.
- **Mount Opacity** (all versions): the mob you ride is drawn see-through.
- **Freelook and Snaplook** (all versions): hold a key to look around your player in third person, or look behind you. Follows server rules.
- **ViewModel** (all versions): move, rotate and resize your hand and held item, and set the hand FOV.
- **Time Changer and Weather Changer** (all versions): pick the time of day and clear, rain, thunder or snow; only you see it.
- **Fog Customizer** (all versions): move or remove distance fog. Clear Water, Clear Lava and Clear Blindness follow server rules.
- **Dark Mode** (all versions): darkens chest and inventory screens and lightens their titles.
- **Render Tweaks and Overlay Opacity** (all versions): hide entity shadows, lightning, fishing lines or the Elder Guardian curse; stop the world bobbing while your hand bobs; set fire, pumpkin and portal overlay strength.
- **Totem Animation** (1.20.1, 26.3): hide or resize the totem pop.
- **1.7 Animations extras** (all versions): Constant swing speed and No re-equip on item changes, both off by default.
- **Elytra Flight Indicator** (1.20.1, 26.3): a Crosshair Tweaks option showing the worn elytra's durability and your speed while gliding.
- **Nametag options and Nametag Health** (all versions): scale, background opacity, distance fade, ping and show in F1. Nametag Health follows server rules.
- **Particle Core styling** (all versions): size, color and fade-out per particle type.
- **Oof Mod** (all versions): players' hurt and death sounds play the classic oof or a sound file you pick; only you hear it.
- **Light Overlay** (all versions): marks block tops where hostile mobs can spawn, red at any time and yellow at night. Follows server rules.
- **Loot Beams** (all versions): a beam of light over dropped items, colored by rarity. Follows server rules.
- **Mob Overlay and Mob Size** (all versions): colored outlines through walls on mobs (and shulker bullets on 1.20.1, 26.3), and mobs drawn bigger or smaller. Follow server rules.
- **Item Despawn** (all versions): dropped items blink before they despawn.
- **UHC Overlay** (all versions): golden apples, heads and ores drawn bigger in the hotbar and inventories.
- **Color Saturation, Motion Blur and Menu Blur** (all versions): hue, saturation, brightness and contrast; frame-blend motion blur; blur behind inventories and menus (on by default). They pause while a shader pack renders the world (1.20.1, 26.3).
- **Hide Foliage and Clear Glass** (all versions): stop drawing grass, ferns, bushes, seagrass, Nether plants and leaf litter; remove the streaks inside glass. Hiding tall plants follows server rules.
- **Chunk Animator** (1.8.9 with OptiFine): chunks rise or drop into place. Not available with Ionium.
- **Combat widgets** (all versions): Combo Counter, PvP Info (accuracy, hits landed and taken, golden apples), Potion Counter, Directional Hit Indicator and a Jump Reset trainer.
- **Info widgets** (all versions): TPS, System Resources (CPU and RAM), Session Playtime, View Angles, Compass Strip, Mouse Trail, Block Counter, Protection, Death Info and Item Tracker.
- **Hearts** (all versions): vanilla heart, absorption and armor icons, extra absorption in one row (x2, x3) and armor toughness as a number.
- **Cooldowns** (1.20.1, 26.3): timers for item cooldowns such as ender pearls.
- **Media Player** (all versions, Windows only): the track, artist and progress of what Windows is playing, such as Spotify.
- **Damage Indicator, Horse Stats and Player Counter** (all versions): target health and floating damage numbers; a horse's jump, speed and health; players nearby and on the server. Follow server rules.
- **Block Info** (all versions) and **Totem Pop Counter** (1.20.1, 26.3): the block you look at (tool, harvestable, mining progress, light), and totem pops per player. Follow server rules.
- **Custom widgets** (all versions): Custom Text with placeholders such as {fps} and {ping}, Custom Shape and Custom Image.
- **Small widgets** (all versions): Player Head, Block Above, Entity Count, Shader Pack and Held Item Lore.
- **Inventory HUD** (all versions): your inventory on screen, hidden while it is empty.
- **Subtitles** (1.20.1, 26.3): move, scale and recolor subtitles.
- **Title Tweaker and Toast Control** (all versions): move, scale, copy to chat or hide titles; hide or move pop-ups (1.8.9 only has achievement and tutorial pop-ups).
- **Vanilla HUD Controls** (all versions): always-hardcore hearts, hide the XP bar or level, keep the item name on screen, hide or move effect icons (no effect icons on 1.8.9).
- **Tab list extras** (all versions): your own row first, animated open and close, and a toggle key mode.
- **Chat tools** (all versions): Compact Chat stacks repeats with a count, Chat Search (Ctrl+F), Copy and Delete buttons on hover, Shift+Enter sends and keeps chat open, Chat Peek and Toggle Chat keys.
- **Chat Tabs** (all versions): tabs from your own patterns, plus Party, Guild and PM tabs on Hypixel. Off by default.
- **History per Server** (all versions): each server's last 1,000 chat lines (up to 16 servers) come back when you rejoin in the same session.
- **Translate Chat** (all versions): chat in another language gets a translation through Google Translate. Off by default; the message text is sent to Google.
- **Mentions and Profanity Filter** (all versions): highlight your name with an optional sound, and an optional word filter.
- **Chat Shortcuts** (all versions): your own abbreviations, such as /l for /lobby.
- **Fuzzy Command Suggestions** (1.20.1, 26.3): "gmd" suggests /gamemode.
- **Unlimited Command Length** (all versions): commands past the normal limit, in singleplayer only. Follows server rules.
- **Auto Text** (all versions): six keys that each send a chat message or command, at most one per second. Follows server rules.
- **Slot Lock** (all versions): lock slots so their items can't be dropped, moved or shift-clicked out.
- **Inventory Search** (all versions): a search box on chests and inventories dims stacks that don't match.
- **Inventory Move and Armor Swap** (all versions): walk while a container is open; middle-click armor to wear it. Follow server rules.
- **Nick Hider** (all versions): shows a nickname instead of your name and a default skin instead of yours; can hide other skins too.
- **Click Outside to Close** (1.8.9): click outside a chest or inventory to close it.
- **Drop Confirmation, Confirm Disconnect and Scroll Tweaks** (all versions): press drop twice for valuable items, confirm before disconnecting, invert the hotbar wheel or stop it wrapping.
- **No double-tap sprint** (all versions): a Toggle Sprint option.
- **Auto Perspective** (all versions): third person while riding or gliding, with an optional smooth camera.
- **Auto World Backup** (all versions): zips a singleplayer world when you close it and keeps the newest backups.
- **Log Pruner and Numerical Enchantments** (all versions): delete old .log.gz logs; show enchantment and potion levels as 1, 5, 10.
- **Survival Guards** (all versions; only Anti Tool Break on 1.8.9): stops a click that would break a nearly broken tool, strip a log or mine budding amethyst. Sneak to do it anyway.
- **Breeding Timer** (all versions): the time left before an animal you saw breed can breed again. Follows server rules.
- **Ender Chest Peek** (all versions): hover an ender chest item to see what your ender chest held when you last closed it. Follows server rules.
- **Pack Profiles** (all versions): save and switch named sets of resource packs in the pack screen.
- **Draggable Server List** (all versions): drag servers up and down the multiplayer list; on by default.
- **Scrollable Tooltips** (all versions): scroll tooltips that don't fit on screen with the mouse wheel.
- **Crash Catcher** (all versions): most crashes while playing take you back to the title screen instead of closing the game; the crash report is still saved and sent like any other. On by default.
- **Quick Reconnect** (all versions): a Reconnect button on the disconnected screen, and Direct Connect shows the server's MOTD, players and ping while you type.
- **Keep Cursor Position** (all versions): the mouse pointer stays put when one menu opens straight after another, such as server chest menus.
- **Sound Volumes** (all versions): 30 separate volume sliders (0-200%) for sound groups such as rain, footsteps or explosions; searchable in Lads Settings.
- **GUI Scale** (1.8.9, 1.20.1): in-between GUI sizes such as 2.5x. Off by default.
- **Chunk Borders** (all versions): chunk edges with an optional grid and section lines; new on 1.8.9.
- **Hypixel helpers** (all versions, on Hypixel only): Auto GL (glhf at game start), Pit Lag Reducer (hides other players at The Pit's spawn), Low Block Warning (Bed Wars and The Bridge), Silent Lobby and Bed Wars bed alerts.
- **Bed Wars hardcore hearts** (1.8.9) and **Opened Chest Highlight** (1.8.9, 1.20.1): hardcore hearts after your bed breaks; chests you opened turn green. Hypixel only; Opened Chest Highlight follows server rules.
- **Emotes and AFK emote** (all versions): new Dab and Taunt emotes, and an optional AFK emote after you stand idle.
- **Mod Bisect** (launcher): Mods > Installed Mods > Bisect mods finds the mod behind a crash or low FPS by turning off half your mods at a time. Your mods are backed up first and Cancel restores them.
- **Share Log** (launcher): removes emails, IP addresses, access tokens and your user folder name from the log, shows a preview, uploads it to mclo.gs and copies the link.
- **Game Repair** (launcher): Mods > Installed Mods > Repair game files re-checks libraries, assets and game files and restores bundled core mods; worlds are never touched.
- **CurseForge modpacks** (launcher): Modpacks > Import also takes CurseForge .zip packs. Mods download through CurseForge, which needs the launcher's key or yours in Mod Manager Settings.
- **`lads://` links** (launcher): `lads://join/<server>` opens the launcher and asks before joining; `lads://launch/<profile>` selects that profile.
- **Profile icons** (launcher): pick one of 17 symbols and 8 color gradients, your own hex colors, or randomize.
- **Loading screen colors** (launcher): set the background, progress bar and glow colors of the game's loading screen in Settings.
- **Home news card and play time** (launcher): Home shows the latest release's highlights, and a modpack's page shows its total play time.
- **OptiFine for packs that need it** (launcher, 1.8.9): if a resource pack uses OptiFine features Ionium can't draw (connected textures, custom colors, emissive, natural textures, better grass), that launch uses OptiFine and says so. Settings > General > Graphics can force Ionium or OptiFine.
- Play > Experiences: Zero SMP shows the players online next to the server address, updated each time you open the tab.
- The Lads Network (`mc.arashyt.ca`) is pinned first in the server list every Lads version shares. It is put back at the top at each launch, and the Servers tab marks it as pinned.
- Title screen: a **Network** button joins The Lads Network in one click (26.3, 1.20.1 and 1.8.9; not in modpacks).
- **Lads Client on The Lads Network**: the network now knows who plays on the Lads Client. Lads players see a Lads badge next to each other's names, get a welcome message, the Lads Client trail in the lobby cosmetics, +25% coins from `/daily`, and `/lads` lists who is on the Lads Client.
- Servers can now adjust Lads Client modules for their players (LadsClientAPI, like Lunar's Apollo). You can refuse forced changes in Lads Settings; blocks always apply.
- Discord shows "Playing on The Lads Network" while you are on it.

## Changed

- **Launcher updates**: updates now download through GitHub's direct download links, so "403 rate limit exceeded" no longer stops them; if GitHub still refuses, the launcher waits an hour before asking again. From the next update on, the launcher downloads only what changed, and every update is checked against its signed contents before it installs.
- **Updates and games**: the launcher only raises its own priority while installing an update, and running games keep their priority while an update downloads.
- **Raised** (all versions) can now move and scale the hotbar, hearts, hunger, armor, air and mount hearts one by one.
- **FPS widget** (all versions): new Stats option with Min / Max, Average, 1% Low or Full.
- **Item Counter** (all versions) also counts golden apples, ender pearls, potions and soups.
- **Share codes** are much shorter (settings that match the defaults are left out); old codes still import.
- **F3 screen** (all versions): Better F3 has an Update Interval option (100 ms by default) so its metrics refresh less often.
- **1.8.9 startup and pack reloads**: mods are scanned in parallel, zip resource packs are indexed once, and caches are cleared when you switch worlds. Particle Core skips rain splash particles (on by default; the rain sound stays).
- **Text drawing** (1.20.1, 26.3): text widths are cached to make busy screens cheaper.
- **Launcher**: the game's JVM flags now include string deduplication to save some memory in long sessions.
- Zero SMP and The Lads SMP Reborn installed from Experiences now only show under Experiences, not in Modpacks > My Modpacks.

## Fixed

- **Server rules gaps**: Auto Reconnect, Mouse Tweaks, Reach Display, Jade, Shulker Box Peek, Fullbright and TNT Timer's see-through option were only blocked on Hypixel, or not at all. They are now off on servers that ban them and on unknown servers, and on in singleplayer, LAN and on our servers. Fullbright stays allowed on Hypixel, CubeCraft, MCC Island and Pika.
- 1.8.9: a resource pack that was selected when the pack screen opened vanished from Available after you deselected it (since 1.9.3); it now comes back, also after a resize.
- 1.8.9: OptiFine-style Fog: OFF removed every fog, including underwater, lava and blindness; it now removes only the distance fog, like OptiFine.
- 1.20.1: opening your singleplayer world to LAN switched off server-rule features such as Minimap and Stack Refill for the host; the host now keeps singleplayer rules.
- 26.3: the fallback elytra for a player with a custom cape had an empty texture path; it now uses vanilla's elytra.
- Launcher settings could all reset to their defaults after a restart. An unreadable settings file is now read again or restored from its backup copy instead of being replaced by defaults.
- "Send crash reports" and "Send anonymous usage stats" were only saved when another setting changed too.
- After applying a skin, the account head in the sidebar, the "Playing as" pickers and the Accounts list kept showing the old skin.
- 1.8.9 players could not join The Lads Network ("only compatible with Minecraft 1.13 and above"); the network now lets every Lads version in.
