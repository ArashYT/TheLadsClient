# Lads Client 1.13.12

Joining a friend's modded world now opens it as its own modpack, and the Modpacks page looks and works like the rest of the launcher.

## Highlights

- **A friend's mods get their own modpack**: when you join a friend's world that runs mods you don't have, the launcher makes a modpack named after the world and your friend (for example "Survival - Alex") with your settings and your mods plus theirs, starts it, and you rejoin them. Your own profile is never changed.
- **Modpacks page rework**: the same header, search bar and buttons as Profiles and Servers, and each modpack is a row with Play and Manage.

## Added

- **ModSync modpacks**: the modpack gets a copy of your options.txt, servers.dat and config folder the first time; joining the same world again reuses it and refreshes its mods. It shows a "Friend's world" tag on the Modpacks page.

## Changed

- **Modpacks page**: modpacks are listed as rows with their Minecraft version, loader, mod count and when you last played; Play starts one straight from the list. New Modpack and Import sit next to the search bar. "External Instances" is now "Other Launchers".
- **Manage page**: Play and the switches use the launcher's red, and the version and loader show as tags.

## Fixed

- **Launcher after a friend's world**: the launcher comes back when that game closes.
