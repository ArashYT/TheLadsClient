# Lads Client 1.9.0

Lads Client 1.9.0 brings full Minecraft 1.20.1 support to both plain Fabric and Forge modpacks, introduces the Experiences tab with Zero SMP, builds seven performance features into the client, and gives Zero SMP a large performance and stability pass. It also makes your account and launcher updates safer.

## Highlights

- **Minecraft 1.20.1, on par with 26.3.** The complete Lads Client experience runs on 1.20.1 with Fabric, and inside Forge modpacks through Sinytra Connector: title and pause screens, the Lads settings and HUD editor, every HUD module, the visual and gameplay modules, the screenshots viewer, capes and server discovery. Vanilla dirt backgrounds are replaced across all menus by dark Lads panels.
- **Experiences tab and Zero SMP.** A new Play sub-tab offers curated server experiences. Zero SMP launches as an official Experience, always runs the newest Lads Core, and has an Auto-join toggle to connect to the server on launch.
- **Zero SMP is much smoother.** Distant terrain is back (Distant Horizons with low-cost settings), F3+T is more than twice as fast, menus and inventories run about 25% faster, flying into new chunks stutters far less, and the Lads loading screen shows from the first seconds instead of a greyed "(Not Responding)" window. Flying over new terrain: 125.7 FPS with a 1% low of 28.3, against 79.5 FPS and 5.9 for the previous pack (test laptop).
- **Seven remade performance features** on 1.8.9, 1.20.1 and 26.3: Particle Core (module, on by default) and six built-ins with no settings: batched raw mouse input (Windows), input-latency frame pacing, faster item and entity rendering, faster animated textures, instant Save and Quit, and baked block entities.
- **Combo Sounds and your own kill sounds.** A new Combo Sounds module plays a rising sound for every hit in a row, and you can upload your own MP3 or WAV sounds for combos and kill banners.
- **Controls synced across versions.** Key binds, FOV, sensitivity, render and simulation distance, max FPS, GUI scale and VSync are shared across 1.8.9, 1.20.1 and 26.3; only controls you change are written back, so version-specific and mod binds are never lost.
- **Safer account and updates.** Your Microsoft login token no longer appears on the game's command line, and the launcher only installs updates signed by The Lads.
- **Minecraft 26.2 is paused** in favour of 26.3. Existing 26.2 profiles, worlds and settings are left untouched; 26.2 and 1.21.11 are hidden from the version selector.

## Added

- **Minecraft 1.20.1** with Java 17, including the Lads settings screen, HUD editor, crosshair editor, paper doll, kill banners with sounds and counters, AppleSkin-style food preview, chat heads, look-at info overlay, tab list, nametags, signal loss toast, Voice Chat HUD, low fire, custom FOV, vertical bobbing, dynamic lights, item physics, Mouse Tweaks, Controlling and Searchables key bind screen, capes selector, World Play Time, Hovering Hotbar and the 1.7 combat animations.
- **Entity Texture Features, Entity Model Features, ImmediatelyFast and NBT Autocomplete built into 1.20.1**: Fresh Animations and other ETF/EMF packs work without installing those mods, on Fabric and in Forge modpacks.
- **Native 3D Skin Layers and Not Enough Animations** for 1.20.1.
- **Experiences tab** with Zero SMP, status and direct launch. The pack is stored in the Lads data folder.
- **Zero SMP additions**: c2mef faster chunk loading, Crawl on Demand (hotkey J, works once the server has it) and an Auto-join server toggle.
- **Zero SMP update 1.1.2**, shipped inside the launcher and applied once when you press Play (existing installs too): Distant Horizons renderer on with low-cost settings (32-chunk radius, one background thread, surface-only distant generation), ModernFix dynamic resources, ImmediatelyFast screen batching and one-at-a-time mod loading. Client settings only; the server needs nothing.
- **Lads loading window for Forge 1.20.1 packs**, with Forge's own window as the fallback.
- **One automatic relaunch** if Zero SMP hits a known mod-loading crash.
- **Modpack tools**: CurseForge-style modpack view, modpack update patcher, and automatic Lads Core sync into every modpack that contains it.
- **Combo Sounds** module (off by default): every hit you land in a row without taking damage plays a sound that rises with the combo; upload your own MP3 or WAV for hits 1-5 (empty slots use a built-in rising note; Combo Reset 2 s by default).
- **Your own kill banner sounds**: Kill Banner > Custom > Custom Sound > "Your sounds" takes MP3 or WAV uploads for kills 1-5 (aces use Kill 5). Uploaded sounds are shared across 1.8.9, 1.20.1, Zero SMP and 26.3 (files up to 20 MB, cut at 12 s).
- **Live crosshair preview** in the Crosshair module settings on 1.8.9, 1.20.1 and 26.3.
- **1.20.1 in Switch Versions**, alongside 26.3 and 1.8.9.
- **F3 hides the Lads HUD** (Better F3 option, on by default). Auto Hide gets a faded opacity, an option to reappear when you swing, place or use items, and a choice of what fades.
- **HUD scale** setting (Auto follows GUI scale) and a **hotbar snap target** in the HUD editor.
- **Pack HUD icons** ("Show Icons", on by default); the Pack HUD lists only the packs you picked.
- **Signed launcher updates**: every update carries a signature from The Lads' release key, and the launcher refuses and deletes any update without a valid one, so nobody else can push a launcher update to you.

## Changed

- **Your login token stays off the command line.** The launcher hands the Microsoft access token to the game privately instead of passing it as a launch argument, where other programs and crash dumps could read it. Old launchers (1.0.x) saved tokens in `lads_accounts.json`; 1.9.0 removes any left in that file.
- **Zero SMP duplicates retired.** Particle Core, Dynamic FPS, BorderlessWindow, Just Zoom, AppleSkin, Clumps, Chat Heads, Controlling, Searchables, Entity Culling, Mouse Tweaks, Not Enough Animations, 3D Skin Layers, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Texture Features and Entity Model Features are replaced by the built-in Lads versions. Jade stays installed.
- **Zero SMP tuning.** Grassier Grass radius 80 instead of 160, duplicate rain ripple and splash particles off, Cull Leaves and MidnightLib removed. Corgilib, Data Anchor and Player Animation Lib stay, because the server uses them.
- **Forge 1.20.1 packs** start with background work capped at half your CPU threads (minimum 2), doubling the flight 1% low without Distant Horizons.
- **Sky and fog colours** are worked out once per game tick instead of every frame, and the frame limiter wastes less CPU on loading screens.
- **Holding Tab** covers the HUD widgets under the player list (including Xaero's minimap); **F3** hides the widgets its text covers, and vanilla boss bars, effect icons and the sidebar step aside.
- **Chat Heads** now live in the Chat module's settings; your settings carry over and heads keep working with the rest of Chat off.
- **Fullscreen button** on the title and pause screens shows a "Fullscreen" / "Windowed" label.
- **Optimized Block Entities** is retired from the 1.20.1 and 26.3 packs, and the standalone Not Enough Animations and 3D Skin Layers from the 1.20.1 pack.
- **Dynamic FPS** is off by default; its Balanced mode caps the frame rate after 3 seconds unfocused.
- **Auto Hide HUD** is off by default; default configurations are migrated to off, custom ones are kept.
- **Particle Budget** settings migrate automatically to Particle Core.
- **26.2 paused**: hidden from the profile filter, version selector and in-game Switch Versions menu; 26.2 preset import is refused with a message.
- **Modpack archives and caches** are stored inside the Lads Client data folder; modpack loader installers are checked against their published checksums before they run.
- **Lads popups** on 1.20.1 are real modal layers, and the Lads HUD and kill banners draw in front of vanilla's HUD.
- **Microsoft sign-in popup** simplified to a single "Open Link & Copy Code" action.
- **HUD positions keep their place** across GUI scale and window size changes, and snapped widgets push instead of overlapping.

## Fixed

- **Zero SMP startup crash** (about 1 launch in 15-20): Create Deco and Create, or Supplementaries and Quark, could collide while Forge loaded mods in parallel. Forge now loads mods one at a time (about 1 s slower startup), and the launcher relaunches once if it still happens.
- **Baked block entities** work with ModernFix dynamic resources, and F3+T no longer copies the whole block-model map.
- **In-game Switch Versions** reliably relaunches the chosen version (including from Zero SMP and modpacks), with no update prompt or false crash report; "Relaunch Now" warns if the switch request could not be saved.
- **In-game account switch** also works from Zero SMP and modpacks.
- **Kill banner headshots** are judged by where the arrow or hit enters the hitbox: no more false headshots on wide mobs, and heads are recognised on upright, quadruped, modded and multipart mobs.
- **1.20.1 hooks missing**: mixin refmaps were not generated, which silently disabled many gameplay and visual hooks on Fabric and Forge.
- **1.8.9 key bindings**: added the Windows key, Menu key and keypad equals, and fixed key names when syncing with newer versions.
- **Crosshair settings preview** shows the active crosshair, and the drawing editor preview renders at standard GUI scales.
- **Raw Input** now actually switches raw mouse input on.
- **Installed-mod cards**: Better Statistics Screen, Resourcify, Modern Advancements, ScalableLux and Lithium show as installed-mod cards instead of switches that did nothing.
- **Choose cape** is back in the skin screen, and tooltip burn times work in Zero SMP.
- **Zero SMP could not join the server**: Corgilib and Data Anchor are no longer removed.
- **Toggle Sprint crash** when loading into a world on 1.20.1.
- **Item Physics** charge bar no longer renders behind the interface and chat.
- **Damage tilt and fullbright** no longer conflict with other Fabric and Forge mods.
- **Borderless fullscreen** on 1.20.1 goes straight to the borderless window and captures correctly in OBS and screen sharing.
- **Zero SMP screens** no longer sit 2 to 14 pixels too high after Raised or Auto Hide HUD parts.
- **Voice Chat HUD** works with Simple Voice Chat 2.6.
- **FastQuit on 1.8.9**: no more crash on singleplayer shutdown, and leaving a world no longer waits for its own save.
- **Kill banner** fade-out blending, and the Lads settings header no longer overlaps the HUD FPS button.
- **LAN worlds** keep the host's cheat permissions.
- **Chests, beds and shulker boxes with shader packs** are lit exactly like vanilla's.
- **Zero SMP Save and Quit with Distant Horizons** no longer freezes for 5 to 8 seconds.
- **Zero SMP crash after several resource reloads** (F3+T, resource pack changes), caused by a shader being freed twice in Mahou Tsukai.
- **Euphoria Patches** no longer replaces your clipboard with a download link on every launch.
- **1.8.9 title screen** no longer cuts off the account card on small windows, and Essential's row no longer overlaps the Switch button.
- **1.8.9 screenshot preview** no longer disappears while the file is still being written.
