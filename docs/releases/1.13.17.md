# Lads Client 1.13.17

The Lads SMP Reborn modpack 0.5.0: new mods, updates and stability fixes.

## Highlights

- **The Lads SMP Reborn 0.5.0**: 18 new mods, 74 mod updates and Fabric Loader 0.20.1. Installed packs update by themselves the next time you press Play.

## Added

- **New mods in The Lads SMP Reborn**: Rustic Delight, Display Delight, Friends&Foes Flowery Mooblooms and Beekeeper Hut, PatPat, Reconnectible Chains, Chalk, Kaffee's Dual Ride, Spyglass Astronomy, Explorer's Compass, Easy Shulker Boxes, Horse Expert, Leave My Bars Alone, Healing Campfire, Followers Teleport Too and Neruina (with Collective and Configurable).

## Changed

- **74 mods updated** in The Lads SMP Reborn (Fabric API, Alex's Mobs, Alex's Caves, Farmer's Delight, Waystones, Simple Voice Chat, JEI, Xaero's maps and more), on **Fabric Loader 0.20.1**.

## Fixed

- **Joining a world crashed the game** when a mod's key was saved in a format 26.3 does not use (Fabrishot's screenshot key). Such keys now count as not pressed; the update also resets that key to F9.
- **Parkour keys** (dodge, ledge grab, slide) were bound to the wrong keys on 26.3; they are Left Alt, G and C again.
- **The world froze** when a Backpacker Camp generated nearby.
- **Explorer maps from Dungeons & Taverns charts** could freeze the world for minutes while looking for their structure; the search now stops after 3 seconds (the map stays blank if nothing was found).
- **Lads SMP settings files** could not be saved on Windows.

## Known limitations

- Two Better End music discs were removed by its update; copies already in a world disappear.
- A few chart maps (Illager Manor, Toxic Lair) can still pause the world for up to about 30 seconds.
