# Lads Client 1.13.24

Experimental Lads Horizon (26.3) moves to the newest Horizon and is now plain Horizon with the Lads title screen.

## Highlights

- **Lads Horizon on the newest Horizon**: the experimental 26.3 build now comes from Horizon Nightly 2026-11-13 (was v0.2.97 from October), with everything Horizon added and fixed since. It downloads once on the next Play, then prepares itself again.
- **Lads SMP Reborn 0.5.3: new worlds no longer freeze**: in about half of all new singleplayer worlds the game showed the first chunks and then got nothing more (you could walk, but no chunks, no blocks, nothing worked). The AntiXray mod was holding every packet behind one chunk; it can no longer do that. Your installed pack updates itself on the next Play.
- **Lads SMP Reborn loads without the long freezes**: creating and joining a world froze the window for up to a minute or more; the longest hitch is now about 1.5 seconds, and from Create World to standing in the world takes about a third of the time. Short hitches are still there, and the first step into the Nether or the End of a new world can still stall for some seconds.
- **HUD editor: resize with the wheel**: select a HUD and scroll to change its size in 25% steps, or hold Control for 5% steps. The size shows next to the HUD while you scroll. Over the list the wheel still scrolls the list.
- **HUD editor: every HUD reaches every edge**: a HUD that touched another one could not be dropped on the screen edge (it stopped short and landed on its neighbour). Fixed on every version and window size.
- **New Play page**: the profile, account and Play button sit at the bottom, the artwork fills the page, and the Play The Lads Network button is red. The Version profiles / Mods / Screenshots shortcuts and the What's new box are gone (the pages are in the side menu, release notes under your name).
- **Skins**: the tab opens at the top, shows the skin your account wears right now and saves it to your library by itself.
- **Smooth scrolling** everywhere in the launcher.
- **The old logo is back** on the launcher and the game window, and the game window icon is sharp at every size (1.8.9 now has it too).

## Changed

- Vignette and Chunk Fade are off by default. Existing profiles get this once; turn them back on and they stay on.
- A profile with no settings of its own starts from your `.minecraft` options (keys, video, sound).
- The launcher no longer asks to import from other clients at startup. Use Settings > General > "Import from another client…".
- The built-in 26.2 and 1.21.11 profiles are gone from Profiles and the Play picker; if one was selected you land on 26.3. Their game folders and worlds are not touched, and profiles you made yourself stay.
- Lads Horizon no longer has the Lads HUD and Lads Zoom: it is plain Horizon with the Lads title screen, window name and icon. The old Lads HUD mod is removed from its folder by itself; your Horizon worlds and options stay.
- Lads Horizon still turns risky features (agent control, auto-reconnect, minimap, mouse tweaks, inventory sort, fullbright, mod chat and packets) off on servers that ban them or whose rules are not known. They cannot be turned back on per server in this build.
- The earlier Lads Horizon build is deleted from disk once the new one is installed.
