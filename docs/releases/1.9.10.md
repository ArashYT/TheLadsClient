# Lads Client 1.9.10

Lads Client 1.9.10 introduces **The Lads SMP Reborn** (v1.0.0) in the launcher's **Experiences** tab — an audited, optimized, and balanced 225-mod Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric 0.19.5) experience powered by our custom **Lads SMP** (`ladssmp-1.0.0.jar`) core mod.

## Highlights

- **New Experience: The Lads SMP Reborn (MC 26.3, Fabric 0.19.5).** One-click install, update, and launch from the **Experiences** tab with 225 curated mods including Alex's Caves & Mobs, BetterEnd, Biomes O' Plenty, Dungeons & Taverns, Farmer's Delight, Simple Voice Chat, and Sodium & Iris.
- **Custom `Lads SMP` (`ladssmp-1.0.0.jar`) Core Mod.** Consolidates 13 standalone QoL and server performance mods into a single zero-dependency jar, cutting JAR count from 256 to 225 (`-31` JARs) and eliminating redundant event/tick hooks.
- **11-Layer Item Disabler & Progression Rebalance.** Blocks overpowered relics, duplicate backpacks, and early-game Netherite/Enchanted Golden Apple dungeon loot while adding `/ladssmp` live admin controls and `/back` death recovery.
- **Zero-Crash 26.3 Registry & Loot Upgrader.** Automatically upgrades 1,714 legacy 1.20.x loot tables and 160 recipe arrays in memory before Minecraft 26.3's strict `RegistryDataLoader` parses them.

## Added

- **The Lads SMP Reborn Experience Card (`ExperiencesView`)**: Added one-click install/launch, per-instance RAM allocation (`8192 MB` default), account switcher, multi-instance management, folder shortcut, and non-destructive reinstall for `lads-smp-reborn-26.3`.
- **`Lads SMP` (`ladssmp-1.0.0.jar`) Custom Mod**:
  - **11-Layer Item Disabler (`ItemDisabler`)**: Prevents disabled items from being picked up, held, equipped, crafted, looted, dropped, traded, or moved in containers.
  - **13 Consolidated Features**: Always Eat, No Animal Tempt Delay, Spawner Control, Crying Portals, Despawning Eggs Hatch, Double Doors, Stack Refill, Sticky Enchanting Lapis, Villager Names, Death Backups (`/back`), Friendly Fire Protection, Attribute Clamping, and Fletching Table Arrows.
  - **`/ladssmp` Admin Command Suite**: Live config reload, item disable/enable/check, loot & consolidated feature status, and death backup recovery.

## Changed

- **Modpack Optimization & Redundancy Cleanup (`256 -> 225` Mods)**: Removed 32 redundant, broken, or competing mod JARs (including duplicate `alexsmobsfabric`, `ksyxis`, `gpumemleakfix`, `euphoria_patcher`, `chisel`, `signpost`, `deathbackup`, and `vc-audio-distance`) and tuned `c2me`, `entityculling`, `immediatelyfast`, `modernfix`, `sound_physics_remastered`, and `lootr` configs for high-TPS SMP play.
- **SMP Loot & Exploit Hardening**: Capped early-game Netherite and Enchanted Golden Apple generation across structure loot tables, blacklisted spawners and Lootr chests from `Carry On`, disabled `Universal Enchants` Infinity+Mending stacking, and disabled `Universal Bone Meal` rare block duplication.

## Fixed

- **Minecraft 26.3 World Creation & Datapack Registry Crash**: Fixed `RegistryDataLoader` failing on 1,714 legacy `loot_table` files (`bitsandbalance`, `rechiseled`, `stoneworks`, `betterend`) and 160 `justenoughbreeding` recipe ingredient arrays on Minecraft 26.3 via `MixinRegistryLoadTask` + `DataFormatUpgrader`.
- **`Citadel` / `Alex's Mobs` 26.3 Model Warning & Crash**: Removed the conflicting duplicate `alexsmobsfabric-26.3-1.0.0.jar` and suppressed legacy `citadel:fancy_item` model errors.
