# Lads Client 1.14.0

Servers can now build proper minigames with the Lads API: cinematic cameras, screen effects, waypoints, boss attack warnings, custom HUD pieces, run timers with a ghost, server keybinds and vote pop-ups. Works on 1.8.9, 1.20.1, 1.21.11, 26.2 and 26.3.

## Added

- Cinematic camera shots a server can play for you: fly-throughs, orbits and fixed angles. They always end by themselves.
- Screen effects from the server: blur, grayscale, sepia, invert, vignette, colour tint and flash.
- Waypoints and team markers in the world, and ground warnings for boss attacks (circle, ring, cone, line, rectangle).
- Server HUD pieces: cooldowns, lives, meters, text lines and timers. You can move them in the HUD editor like any other piece.
- Parkour and speedrun timer with green or red split times against your best, a finish screen, and a see-through ghost of the best run.
- Server keybinds: a game can add its own keys (for example "Dash" or "Ultimate") under a "Server" category in Controls. You can rebind them, and they disappear when you leave the game.
- Toasts, votes and yes/no pop-ups from the server. They never cover the pause menu.
- Game sounds and music from the server, never louder than your own volume sliders.
- Games can switch you to a fitting profile while you play and give your own settings back when you leave.
- Servers can see whether you play on keyboard and mouse or a controller, so they can show the right button prompts.
- Short input locks for cutscenes and countdowns. They only pause movement, never press keys for you, never block Esc or chat, and end after 15 seconds at most.

## Fixed

- A launcher update that was interrupted halfway no longer fails on the next try because of the leftover download.

## Notes

- Everything a server starts ends when you leave it. Nothing is saved to your settings.
- Controller rumble from the server works on 26.3 only for now. It has not been tried on a real controller yet.
