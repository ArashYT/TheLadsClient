# The Lads Client 1.4.8

## Highlights
- **Kill banners show the moment you get the kill**, and they now work on servers whose games never really kill anyone, such as Hypixel.
- **New Kill Banner settings.** Pick a skin and its variant from pictures of the banners, mix one skin's banner with another's sound, randomize your banner on every kill, and choose whether players, mobs or bosses get a banner.
- **1.8.9 shares your worlds, resource packs and settings** with the other versions and your .minecraft folder. Lunar Client's 1.8 settings and resource packs are used when Lunar is installed. Before 1.8.9 opens a world a newer version saved, it offers to back it up first.
- **Updates bring every new mod.** The launcher now recognises every mod file it ever shipped, so old copies are replaced or removed even when its records are missing. A new **Reset mods folder** button starts a profile's mods from scratch.

## Added
- **Kill Banner picker.** Skin tiles (Base, Reaver, Rogue and Custom) show each banner's one-kill art; choosing Reaver or Rogue shows its four variants the same way.
- **Custom kill banner:** the banner of one skin with the sound of another. Clicking a sound plays it.
- **Randomize:** a different variant of your skin on every kill, a random skin and variant on every kill, or a random pick from the banners you tick. The same banner never shows twice in a row.
- **Show a banner for:** Players, Mobs and Bosses checkboxes (Players and Bosses are on).
- **HEADSHOT text** can be turned on or off. It is off by default.
- **Reset mods folder** on the Mods page: every file in the profile's mods folder goes to the Recycle Bin (including mods you added), your mod on/off choices are cleared and the Lads pack is installed fresh.
- **Update before playing.** When a launcher update is already downloaded, pressing Play asks whether to restart and update first.
- **1.8.9 world backups.** Opening a world a newer version saved asks first. Yes copies it to "<name> - 1.8.9" and opens the copy, leaving the original untouched. No shows a large red warning that the world might be corrupted before it opens.

## Changed
- **Kill detection** no longer waits for the server's kill statistic (up to 2 seconds late). A kill counts when something you hit last dies within 5 seconds, as Minecraft credits kills, or when the server's kill message names you and a player you hit in the last 10 seconds.
- **Quick kills** each play their own sound, so the sounds stack, while the banner restarts its animation on the newest kill.
- **Raw Input** now switches Minecraft's own Raw Input setting on 1.21.1, 1.21.11 and 26.2, and changing it in Mouse Settings updates the module. Minecraft 26.3 removed that setting because its mouse input is always raw; there, turning the module off applies Windows' pointer speed and acceleration.
- **1.8.9 is no longer a separate instance** for worlds, resource packs and shader packs. Its existing worlds and packs move into the shared folders (nothing is overwritten). Its game settings (keybinds, FOV, sensitivity, sounds and the other shared settings) follow your other versions, or Lunar Client's 1.8 profile when Lunar is installed, and its Lads settings (HUD layout and modules) are shared with the other versions.
- **Mod updates and removals** apply to every copy the launcher shipped, whatever its file name, including old separate copies of mods that are now built into Lads Core (those copies turned the built-in version off).

## Fixed
- **Kill banners appeared up to 2 seconds after the kill.**
- **Kill banners never appeared on Hypixel** and other servers whose minigames don't use Minecraft's deaths.
- **Several quick kills played only one sound.**
- **Some players didn't get new mods or features after updating**, because an old copy was kept when the launcher's records of it were missing.
- The old launcher pack link (Packwiz) from before 1.2 is cleared from profiles that still had it; it installed outdated copies of mods that are now part of Lads Core.

## Known limitations
- Kill messages are recognised by their wording: the message must name a player you hit in the last 10 seconds and then you ("Bob was killed by You"), or say you killed them ("You killed Bob"). Unusual wordings are not detected; deaths that happen in the world are always detected.
- Lunar Client's files are only read, never changed. With Lunar installed, 1.8.9's shared settings follow Lunar's 1.8 profile each launch.
- Opening a world from a newer version in 1.8.9 without a backup can permanently damage it, also for the newer versions.
