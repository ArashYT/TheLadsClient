# Lads Client 1.11.0

Lads Client 1.11.0 adds Friends and world hosting: add friends by Minecraft name, see what they are playing, and join each other's singleplayer worlds with no port forwarding and no server. It also makes launcher updates smoother and smaller, brings ReplayMod to 1.8.9, and adds more tools players know from Lunar, Badlion and Feather. "All versions" below means 1.8.9, 1.20.1 and 26.3.

## Highlights

- **Friends** (all versions): add friends by Minecraft name, see who is online and what they play, message them and send invites. Press O, or use the Friends button on the title and pause screens.
- **Host your world for friends** (all versions): Multiplayer Options replaces Open to LAN. A friend clicks Join, you accept, and they play in your world: directly, or through a Cloudflare relay when a direct connection fails. Only your Lads Client friends can join, on the same Minecraft version.
- **Smoother launcher updates** (launcher): one Lads install screen until the new launcher opens, and smaller updates from now on.
- **ReplayMod and build tools**: ReplayMod comes to 1.8.9; a Schematic Visualizer and WorldEdit CUI draw blueprints and selections.
- **More PvP, chat and inventory tools** (all versions): Hit Color per damage type, an MLG helper, Save chat as image, Link Preview, Container Sorting and more.
- **Server rules**: a friend's world counts as an unknown server for features that follow server rules, so they stay off there until you allow them.

## Added

- **Friends screen** (all versions): tabs for Friends, Requests, Blocked and Add Friend (by Minecraft name); give friends nicknames, remove or block them. Open it with O or the Friends button on the title and pause screens, which shows how many friends are online.
- **Friend status** (all versions): friends see whether you are in menus, in singleplayer, hosting a world or on a server. Server: Hidden keeps your server address private and Appear Offline shows you as offline.
- **Direct messages** (all versions): the Chat button on a friend opens a private chat that never goes through Minecraft chat.
- **Invites and join requests** (all versions): invite a friend to your world or to the server you are on. Invites and join requests show as pop-ups with buttons and a clickable chat line; two unbound keys accept or decline the newest pop-up.
- **Multiplayer Options** (all versions): the pause screen's Open to LAN becomes Multiplayer Options. Pick a scope (Off, LAN, Friends or Invite only), Join Approval (Auto Friends, Ask Me or Invite Only), max guests (1-16) and whether friends see your world as joinable.
- **Guest rules** (all versions): default game mode, permission level (0-4), PvP, difficulty, common game rules (keepInventory, daylight and weather cycle, mob griefing, fire spread, mob spawning, natural regeneration, sleeping percentage where the version has it) and a guest view distance cap. Saved per world.
- **Guest list** (all versions): everyone in your world with their ping and buttons for Kick, Ban, Mute, Op or Deop, game mode and TP (teleport to you). Bans are per world and can be undone in Multiplayer Options.
- **World backup** (all versions): Backup World Before Opening is on by default and zips the world into lads-backups, keeping the newest 5 per world.
- **Direct or relayed** (all versions): friends connect straight to your PC when they can and through a Cloudflare relay when they can't. Always Use Relay (Advanced) hides your IP from guests. The relay has a monthly data limit.
- **Only the right friends, on the right version** (all versions): only your accepted Lads Client friends can join, there is no public address, and vanilla players can't join. Join stays greyed out, with the reason on hover (for example "Needs Minecraft 1.20.1"), when the Minecraft version, loader or modpack version differs. The world closes for everyone when you leave.
- **Mojang friends** (26.3): a view-only Mojang friends page in the Friends screen lists your Mojang friends with an Add on Lads button, and Manage Mojang friends opens the game's own screen.
- **Friends & Social** (all versions): a module under Server in Lads Settings; turn it off and the game never connects to the Lads social server. While it is on, your Minecraft name and UUID go to that server.
- **ReplayMod** (launcher, 1.8.9): ReplayMod 2.6.20 downloads with the other 1.8.9 client mods; switch it off in the launcher's Mods tab if you don't want it.
- **Schematic Visualizer** (all versions): load a classic .schematic file and see it as a ghost blueprint in the world, with tint, render distance, bounding box only and origin settings plus Set Origin to Player. It only draws; it never places blocks. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **WorldEdit CUI** (all versions): draws your WorldEdit cuboid selection (corners and a grid) when the server has WorldEdit with CUI support. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **Sound Filters** (all versions): Underwater Muffle lowers volume and pitch underwater, with an intensity slider; menu sounds and music are not changed. Off by default.
- **Mumble Link** (all versions, Windows only): shares your position and look direction with the Mumble voice app for positional audio. Off by default.
- **Hit Color per damage type** (all versions): fire or lava, fall and drowning hits can flash their own colors; on 1.8.9 the type is a best guess on servers.
- **MLG Cobweb** (all versions): a timing reticle and a ghost box showing where to place water, a cobweb, a slime block or a hay bale to break a fall. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **1.7 Animations extras** (all versions): Cast rod as stick (off by default), 1.7 item lighting (on by default) and Swing speed vs haste, which sets how much Haste and Mining Fatigue change your swing speed (100% is vanilla).
- **Camera Parallax Fix** (1.8.9): a Performance Manager option that puts the first-person camera at your eyes instead of slightly behind your head. Off by default.
- **Save chat as image** (all versions): hover a chat line and click I next to Copy and Delete to save the line, in its colors, as a PNG in your screenshots folder.
- **Link Preview** (all versions): on a link prompt, Load preview shows the picture behind a small https image link. Nothing is loaded until you click. Off by default.
- **Container Sorting** (all versions): press R or click Sort in chests and other storage containers and in your inventory. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **Elytra Swap** (1.20.1, 26.3): a key (unbound) swaps your chestplate and elytra. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **Creative Fly Boost** (all versions): multiplies creative flying speed (1x to 5x). Off by default; follows server rules.
- **Nick Hider: Hide others' names** (all versions): shows a nickname you choose instead of other players' names. Only you see it.
- **SkyBlock Suite** (all versions): Terminal Solvers highlight the right slots in Dungeon terminals (click in order, same color, starts with a letter, correct panes). They only highlight; you click. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **Dungeon Map** (1.8.9, 1.20.1) and **Dark Auction timer** (all versions): HUD widgets for the dungeon map and a countdown to the next Dark Auction. Follow server rules.
- **Server Notification Actions** (all versions): pop-ups from servers that use the Lads Client API can carry up to four buttons that run a command, fill the chat box, open a link or dismiss. Off by default; follows server rules.
- **Opened Chest Highlight** (26.3): chests you opened turn green, now on 26.3 too. Hypixel only; follows server rules.

## Changed

- **Pause screen** (all versions): Open to LAN is now Multiplayer Options; LAN is one of its scopes.
- **Launcher updates**: the Lads "Installing update" screen stays up until the new launcher has drawn, and the launcher reopens at the same place and size. The separate installer window is gone. Going from 1.10.0 to 1.11.0 still shows the old install window once.
- **Update size** (launcher): updates download only what changed since your version, with the full download as the fallback. Every update is still checked against its signed contents.
- **1.7 Animations** (all versions): held items are drawn fully bright in first person, as in 1.7. Turn off 1.7 item lighting to go back.
- **Auto Reconnect** (all versions): never offers to reconnect to a friend's world.

## Fixed

- 26.3 no longer sticks on OpenGL after shaders were turned on once.
- **Video Settings crash** (1.8.9): changing Fullscreen Mode in OptiFine's Video Settings no longer crashes the game (OptiFine read the screen size before the window existed). F11 still gives borderless fullscreen.
- **`lads://` links** (launcher): they always open the installed launcher. A test or portable copy that ran first could take them over; the installed launcher now owns them and repairs a link that points elsewhere.
