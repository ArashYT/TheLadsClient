# The Lads Client 1.4.0

## Highlights
- Minecraft 1.8.9 joins the client: Forge with OptiFine, the Lads menu and the full Lads HUD, on a managed Java 8.
- 1.21.1 and 1.21.11 catch up with 26.x: the Lads title and pause screens, native Autohide, more HUD modules and one built-in controls search.
- A new launcher updating screen, a top-right **Update** button that asks before restarting, and the Lads artwork everywhere.

## Added
- **Minecraft 1.8.9 (Forge 11.15.1.2318).** A new "The Lads Client 1.8.9" profile. The launcher installs Forge, downloads and verifies a Java 8 runtime, and keeps 1.8.9 on exactly Java 8.
- **OptiFine 1.8.9 HD U M5** is downloaded from optifine.net at launch and checked against its known fingerprint. It is never shipped with the client; if the download fails, Minecraft starts without it and tries again next time. You can switch it off on the Mods page or use your own OptiFine jar.
- **Lads Core on 1.8.9:** the Lads menu on Right Shift (title screen, pause menu and in game), a **Lads Client** button in the pause menu, and 18 HUD modules with the full HUD editor (drag, snap, groups, On/Off switches, module settings). The launcher's Mods page lists the 1.8.9 modules.
- **1.21.1 and 1.21.11:** the Lads title screen and More menu, the redesigned pause menu with **Multiplayer** and **Replays**, Right Shift opening the Lads menu from the title and pause screens, the HUD editor gear, native **Autohide** fading the Lads HUD with Minecraft's hotbar, Smooth Hotbar, Boss Bar and mod icons in the HUD, Scoreboard and Armor HUD on 1.21.1, Zoom, Fullbright and Toggle Sprint/Sneak on 1.21.1, settings shortcuts for Resourcify and Modern Advancements, and smoothly scrolling lists.
- **Controls search** on 1.21.1 and 1.21.11: one built-in search by name, key, category or mod, with conflict and unbound filters.
- **Resource-pack filter** on 1.21.1 and 1.21.11: show all, compatible or incompatible packs, sorted by name if you like.
- **Updating screen:** larger text and progress bar, with a pickaxe mining a block while the update downloads.
- **Update button:** when a launcher update arrives while the launcher is open, a glowing red **Update** button appears in the title bar and installs when you click it, instead of restarting on its own.

## Changed
- The Lads artwork is now the launcher icon, the taskbar icon and the Minecraft window icon on every version. The Classic Minecraft Icon mod is no longer needed and is removed.
- The Auto Hide HUD mod is replaced by the built-in Autohide on 1.21.1 and 1.21.11; your "off" choice is kept.
- What's New shows formatted notes: headings, bold text, code and links.
- 1.8.9 keeps its own worlds, resource packs and shader packs. A world saved by a newer version cannot be opened in 1.8.9, so the shared folders are never used there. Servers and screenshots are still shared.

## Fixed
- Essential's buttons and side menu no longer overlap the title or pause screens on 1.21.1 and 1.21.11.
- The 1.21.11 default Zoom key is C, as on the other versions.

## Known limits
- On 1.8.9, Toggle Sprint/Sneak, Lads Zoom, Fullbright, the Lads crosshair, the Lads title screen theme, Discord presence, 1.7 Animations and Autohide are not available yet; the Lads menu lists them as pending. OptiFine's own zoom (C) works as usual.
- 1.21.1 and 1.21.11 still lack some 26.x features (tooltips, shulker previews, kill banner, paper doll, tab list, crosshair editor and others); they stay listed as pending until a later update.
