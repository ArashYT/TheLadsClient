# Lads Client 1.9.15

Lads Client 1.9.15 fixes **fullscreen on 1.8.9** for good, adds **Low Fire / Low Shield switches to the resource packs menu**, and fixes a **1.8.9 crash near signs and banners**.

## Highlights

- **1.8.9 fullscreen fixed**: leaving fullscreen (F11 or the Fullscreen/Windowed button) puts the window back exactly as it was, with the whole picture in it (no black band on top, no menu cut off at the bottom), and going fullscreen again covers the whole screen (no strip of desktop at the top, no taskbar on top of the game).
- **Low Fire and Low Shield in the resource packs menu**: some resource packs already lower the fire overlay or shields, and with Lads lowering them too they ended up off screen. Switch Lads' lowering ON or OFF right under the pack lists.

## Added

- **Low Fire** switch in the resource packs menu (all versions) and **Low Shield** (1.20.1 and 26.3; 1.8.9 has no shields). They are the same settings as in Lads Mods -> 1.7 Animations.

## Changed

- Nothing else.

## Fixed

- **1.8.9 crash "TileEntitySign cannot be cast to TileEntityBanner"** while rendering signs and banners (seen on Hypixel): a renderer lookup shortcut could hand a sign the banner renderer when chunks were being built at the same time. The shortcut is gone; vanilla's lookup is just as fast.
- **1.8.9 fullscreen**: leaving fullscreen left the window drawing at the old size in its bottom-left corner, and going fullscreen again placed the game 23 pixels down under the taskbar.
