# Lads Client 1.7.4

Kill banners only: every skin now moves the way its own Valorant preview shows, and every skin has a headshot banner.

## Highlights
- **Every kill banner animates like its own Valorant preview.** 83 of the 88 still skins had their motion measured frame by frame from the skin's preview video (the five kills at the start of Riot's level previews): when its emblem pops, how its ring and frame come in, where its kill pips sit and how they slide and turn, when the kill mark lands, how it leaves. 65 skins have all five kill counts from their preview; 18 older skins' previews are short clips with one to three kills, so their other counts play Rogue's measured motion. Reaver and Rogue keep their recorded frames. Five skins whose previews could not be read (Phaseguard, Champions 2022, Champions 2024, RES Knockout, RES Dance Fever) play Rogue's motion throughout, as 1.7.4's first cut did for everyone.
- **HEADSHOT banner for every skin.** Valorant's old headshot banner is back: a coloured box with HEADSHOT under the banner, in the colour the skin's preview showed where its demo made headshots (33 skins), and a best guess from the skin's accent colour for the rest. On by default.
- **Head hits count as headshots.** An arrow that hits the head and kills, or a kill whose last hit landed with the crosshair on the head. "Every kill" is there too.
- **The kill mark is optional.** The red X on the emblem and its red strobe can be turned off.
- **A tidier Kill Banner screen.** The picker's sections fold (skins open by default, customising closed) and a skin's variants sit under the big preview, so choosing a variant no longer means scrolling back up.

## Added
- Kill Banner options: **Headshots** (Head hits / Every kill), **Headshot Banner** (on), **Kill Mark** (on). The old "HEADSHOT Text" option is retired.
- Each still skin's motion file (`killbanner/motion/<skin>.properties`): per kill count, the emblem, ring, frame and pip channels frame by frame, the pips' settled angles and orbit, the mark frame, and the skin's HEADSHOT box colour where its preview showed one.
- `tools/killbanner/measure_video.py`, `map_videos.py` and `measure_all.py` (developer tools) measure a skin's preview video into its motion file: kills are dated by the mark's red strobe, the skin's own art is matched to each frame (emblem, frame, pips, ring), and check sheets show the measured layers next to the video.
- Headshots from arrows: the client follows its own arrows in flight and takes where one crosses a living entity's box as the hit; the top quarter is a head hit (1.8.9 and 26.x).

## Changed
- A banner the next kill cuts short now leaves for a moment before the next one starts, as in the game, instead of being replaced on the spot.
- Still skins' pips take their places from their own preview; skins whose pips the preview never showed clearly keep Rogue's pip motion on their own emblem, ring and frame.
- Champions 2024 and 2025 (one picture per kill count) are measured from their previews too, each count's picture in turn.
- The HEADSHOT label is drawn as a box with the word across it, on Reaver and Rogue as well, instead of bare white text.
- The Kill Banner picker: dropdown sections, variants under the preview.
- Still skins' art draws 4% larger (0.76 cell pixels an art pixel instead of 0.73), the median size the 83 previews showed.

## Fixed
- Still skins no longer all share one animation: each follows its own preview (1.7.4's first cut gave them all Rogue's).
- Headshot banners show again: the option that hid them is gone and the new one starts on.
- A kill while a banner is up no longer snaps the old banner away.

## Known limitations
- Some skins' kill pips could not be told from the skin's own effects in the preview (the same colour and shape); those banners play Rogue's pip motion on the skin's own measured emblem, ring and frame. The analysis folder (`artifacts/1.7.4/analysis`, check sheets per skin) shows exactly what was measured.
- The HEADSHOT box colour is measured only where a preview's demo made headshots; other skins' colours are a guess from their accent.
- Arrow headshots are the client's own reading of its arrow's flight, a tick or so behind the server; a very close shot may count as a body hit.
