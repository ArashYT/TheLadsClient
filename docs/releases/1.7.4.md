# Lads Client 1.7.4

Kill banners only: every skin now plays Valorant's own kill banner animation, with the game's own colours, and every skin has a headshot banner.

## Highlights
- **Every kill banner is imported from the game.** The banner's motion is no longer measured off preview videos: it is Valorant's own animation, read from the game files (the KillBanner widgets' sequencer curves and their Blueprint timers) and evaluated frame by frame. Every still skin plays exactly that, because in the game they all share it and differ only in their art, their colour and their pip radius; those come from each skin's own KillBannerData too. Reaver and Rogue keep their recorded frames.
- **The motion, as the game does it.** The emblem drops in from 30 px up; the wheel grows into place from a tenth large as it dissolves in over half a second; the frame dissolves in over 0.3 s. At 0.15 s every kill pip lights up a fifth large and 15 px out, its hover glowing to full, then slides into place by 0.75 s and settles at 0.6. At 0.75 s the wheel spins the way the game's `FInterpTo` does: an exponential turn (speed 8 a second) to a pip's spacing (half a turn for two kills, a third for three, a quarter for four), snapping to its goal; the ace turns two full turns the other way (speed 5). At 1.8 s the emblem shrinks to 0.6 and dissolves over 0.3 s, and at 1.9 s the whole banner fades as the wheel grows back out. The ace plays all of this at the game's 0.3 speed (7 seconds), with its pips and spin in real time.
- **The headshot flicker, as the game does it.** On a headshot the emblem flashes red four times, every 6 frames, easing in and out, and pulses larger with each flash (1.155, 1.106, 1.076, 1.049); the X over it flashes white against the red; the reticle lands at twice its size and shrinks onto the emblem over 0.25 s. As in the game, the X and the flicker belong to headshots: a body kill shows none.
- **HEADSHOT banner for every skin, in the skin's colour.** A box with HEADSHOT under the banner, tinted the skin's PrimaryColor at the game's 30% over the dark backdrop: the same colour its pips light up in. On by default. A skin with a headshot badge in the game (Holo Meridian) swaps it in for its emblem on a headshot.
- **Pips as the game builds them.** Each pip is the skin's Up texture under its coloured hover texture, the hover lit in the skin's colour as it arrives and settling at 60%.
- **Head hits count as headshots.** An arrow that hits the head and kills, or a kill whose last hit landed with the crosshair on the head. "Every kill" is there too.
- **The kill mark is optional.** The red X and its flicker can be turned off.
- **A tidier Kill Banner screen.** The picker's sections fold (skins open by default, customising closed) and a skin's variants sit under the big preview. The preview plays headshots, as the game's own banner previews do.

## Added
- Kill Banner options: **Headshots** (Head hits / Every kill), **Headshot Banner** (on), **Kill Mark** (on). The old "HEADSHOT Text" option is retired.
- `killbanner/template.properties`: the game's kill banner animation per kill count, frame by frame (emblem, ring, frame and pip channels, the pips' Up and hover opacities, size and outward offset, the spin in degrees, the mark frame).
- `killbanner/accent.properties`: each skin's PrimaryColor per variant from its KillBannerData (91 skins, every variant).
- Each skin's pip Up texture (`pip_up.png`, per variant where the game has one) and headshot badge (`emblem_hs.png`) where the game has one.
- `tools/killbanner/game_template.py` (the template from the exported widget animations and Blueprints), `game_map.py` (matches each client skin to its game data by pixel-identical art, resolving the data classes' inheritance), `game_data.py` (colours, Up textures and badges from that data), `umg_dump.py` (dumps every widget animation of an export).
- Headshots from arrows: the client follows its own arrows in flight and takes where one crosses a living entity's box as the hit; the top quarter is a head hit (1.8.9 and 26.x).

## Changed
- A banner the next kill cuts short now leaves for a moment before the next one starts, as in the game, instead of being replaced on the spot.
- The 83 per-skin motion files measured from preview videos are gone: the game's own animation replaces them all.
- The kill mark and its flicker now show on headshots only (as in the game); the Kill Mark option turns them off there.
- Champions 2024 and 2025 (one picture per kill count) turn from the previous count's picture into this one's as the mark lands.
- The HEADSHOT label is drawn as a box in the skin's colour with the word across it, on Reaver and Rogue as well, instead of bare white text.
- The Kill Banner picker: dropdown sections, variants under the preview.
- Still skins' art draws at 0.76 cell pixels an art pixel (was 0.73).

## Fixed
- Still skins' animations no longer glitch, jitter or drift: there is nothing measured any more, every channel is the game's own curve.
- The kill pips arrive, slide in and spin as the game's do, on every skin and every kill count (including the ace's two turns).
- The emblem rests exactly on the ring centre and pops, pulses and shrinks as the game's does.
- Headshot banners show again: the option that hid them is gone and the new one starts on; every skin's box is its real colour.
- A kill while a banner is up no longer snaps the old banner away.

## Known limitations
- The game's flipbook FX (sparks and flames per kill tier), its per-skin optional effects and the headshot particles are not drawn; the client keeps its own droplet spray from two kills up.
- The ring's dissolve is a top-down wipe in the game; the client fades it in over the same half second.
- Arrow headshots are the client's own reading of its arrow's flight, a tick or so behind the server; a very close shot may count as a body hit.
