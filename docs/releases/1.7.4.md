# Lads Client 1.7.4

Kill banners only: every skin now plays Valorant's own kill banner animation and FX, with the game's own colours and textures, and every skin has a headshot banner.

## Highlights
- **Every kill banner is imported from the game.** The banner's motion is no longer measured off preview videos: it is Valorant's own animation, read from the game files (the KillBanner widgets' sequencer curves and their Blueprint timers) and evaluated frame by frame. Every skin plays exactly that, because in the game they all share it and differ only in their art, their colour, their pip radius and their FX; those come from each skin's own KillBannerData too.
- **The motion, as the game does it.** The emblem drops in from 30 px up; the wheel grows into place from a tenth large as it dissolves in over half a second; the frame dissolves in over 0.3 s. At 0.15 s every kill pip lights up a fifth large and 15 px out, its hover glowing to full, then slides into place by 0.75 s and settles at 0.6. At 0.75 s the wheel spins the way the game's `FInterpTo` does: an exponential turn (speed 8 a second) to a pip's spacing (half a turn for two kills, a third for three, a quarter for four), snapping to its goal; the ace turns two full turns the other way (speed 5). At 1.8 s the emblem shrinks to 0.6 and dissolves over 0.3 s, and at 1.9 s the whole banner fades as the wheel grows back out. The ace plays all of this at the game's 0.3 speed (7 seconds), with its pips and spin in real time.
- **The game's FX, drawn in full.** The game's flipbooks play behind the banner from its FX event, tinted the skin's colour: the hero flame behind the emblem on every kill; from two kills the skin's tier sparks left and right (tier 1 for 2-3 kills, tier 2 for 4, tier 3 for an ace: the Base set for most skins, Sovereign's and Champions 2024/2025's own sets, ORA by OneTap's circle animation); on an ace the large sparks and the four X sparks on top. 16 flipbooks, 2.6 MB of atlases, played a frame per 1/fps second one-shot exactly as the game's AnimatedSpriteWidget does.
- **The headshot flicker, as the game does it.** On a headshot the emblem flashes red four times, every 6 frames, easing in and out, and pulses larger with each flash (1.155, 1.106, 1.076, 1.049); the X over it flashes white against the red; the reticle lands at twice its size and shrinks onto the emblem over 0.25 s. As in the game, the X and the flicker belong to headshots: a body kill shows none.
- **Reaver, Rogue and the Standard banner rebuilt from the game.** They no longer play recorded video strips: Reaver (Soulstealer in the game files, with its four variants), Rogue (its four variants) and the Standard banner (Base, now with its real frame and ring) are built from their game textures and play the same game animation and FX as every other skin. The strips are kept in `artifacts/1.7.4/backup-og`.
- **HEADSHOT banner for every skin, in the skin's colour.** A box with HEADSHOT under the banner, tinted the skin's PrimaryColor at the game's 30% over the dark backdrop: the same colour its pips light up in. On by default. A skin with a headshot badge in the game (Holo Meridian) swaps it in for its emblem on a headshot.
- **Pips as the game builds them.** Each pip is the skin's Up texture under its coloured hover texture, the hover lit in the skin's colour as it arrives and settling at 60%.
- **One skin a line.** The same line's episodes and a bundle's siblings are now one skin with the others as variants, each keeping its own art, colour, FX and sounds: Araxys, Gaia's Vengeance (+ EP 7), Ion (+ EP 5), Magepunk (+ EP 3, EP 6), Mystbloom, Neptune, Oni, Prelude to Chaos (+ V25), Reaver (+ EP 5), RGX 11z Pro (+ EP 4, EP 9), Sentinels of Light (+ EP 7), Singularity (+ EP 9), Sovereign (+ EP 8), Glitchpop (+ 2.0), Prime (+ 2.0), VCT, Champions (2021-2023), ORA by OneTap (five skins), Bubblegum Deathwish (four variants), R.E.S (three skins). The picker lists 61 skins instead of 91; saved choices of the folded entries still resolve to the right banner.
- **The next kill replaces the banner at once**, as in the game: no gap, no leaving animation, kills can be chained as fast as they land.
- **Head hits count as headshots.** An arrow that hits the head and kills, or a kill whose last hit landed with the crosshair on the head. "Every kill" is there too.
- **The kill mark is optional.** The red X and its flicker can be turned off.
- **A tidier Kill Banner screen.** The picker's sections fold (skins open by default, customising closed) and a skin's variants sit under the big preview. The preview plays headshots, as the game's own banner previews do.

## Added
- Kill Banner options: **Headshots** (Head hits / Every kill), **Headshot Banner** (on), **Kill Mark** (on). The old "HEADSHOT Text" option is retired.
- `killbanner/template.properties`: the game's kill banner animation per kill count, frame by frame (emblem, ring, frame and pip channels, the pips' Up and hover opacities, size and outward offset, the spin in degrees, the mark frame).
- `killbanner/fx/*.png` + `fx.properties` + `fx-skins.properties`: the game's 16 FX flipbooks as atlases, their frame rates and frame sequences, and the skins that play other tiers than the Base set.
- `killbanner/accent.properties`: each skin's PrimaryColor per variant from its KillBannerData (every variant).
- `killbanner/merged.properties`: the folded skins (hidden aliases to their skin and variant) and the variants that keep their own sounds.
- Each skin's pip Up texture (`pip_up.png`, per variant where the game has one), headshot badge (`emblem_hs.png`) where the game has one, and a folded variant's own frame and ring where they differ.
- `tools/killbanner/game_template.py` (the template from the exported widget animations and Blueprints), `game_map.py` (matches each client skin to its game data by pixel-identical art, resolving the data classes' inheritance), `game_data.py` (colours, Up textures, badges and FX sets from that data), `game_fx.py` (the flipbook atlases), `merge_skins.py` (folds the episode duplicates), `umg_dump.py` (dumps every widget animation of an export).
- Headshots from arrows: the client follows its own arrows in flight and takes where one crosses a living entity's box as the hit; the top quarter is a head hit (1.8.9 and 26.x).
- The Kill Banner QA world's target stands again the same tick it dies, and the player swings without an attack cooldown, so kills can be spammed.

## Changed
- The 83 per-skin motion files measured from preview videos are gone: the game's own animation replaces them all. The client's own droplet spray is gone too: the game's flipbooks replace it.
- Reaver and Rogue are still skins now (their recorded 60 fps strips, strobe tints and Reaver's heart overlay are removed); the Standard banner has its real frame and ring.
- The kill mark and its flicker now show on headshots only (as in the game); the Kill Mark option turns them off there.
- A kill while a banner is up replaces it at once instead of letting it leave first.
- Champions 2024 and 2025 (one picture per kill count) turn from the previous count's picture into this one's as the mark lands.
- The HEADSHOT label is drawn as a box in the skin's colour with the word across it instead of bare white text.
- The Kill Banner picker: dropdown sections, variants under the preview, 61 skins.
- Still skins' art draws at 0.76 cell pixels an art pixel (was 0.73).

## Fixed
- Still skins' animations no longer glitch, jitter or drift: there is nothing measured any more, every channel is the game's own curve.
- The kill pips arrive, slide in and spin as the game's do, on every skin and every kill count (including the ace's two turns).
- The emblem rests exactly on the ring centre and pops, pulses and shrinks as the game's does.
- Headshot banners show again: the option that hid them is gone and the new one starts on; every skin's box is its real colour.
- Reaver's, Rogue's and the Standard banner's animations match every other skin's instead of playing older footage.

## Known limitations
- The game's per-skin optional effects (OptionalEffects widgets, e.g. extra wings or glows on a few skins) are not drawn.
- The ring's dissolve is a top-down wipe in the game; the client fades it in over the same half second.
- Arrow headshots are the client's own reading of its arrow's flight, a tick or so behind the server; a very close shot may count as a body hit.
