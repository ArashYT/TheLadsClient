# Lads Client 1.7.4

A single fix: every kill banner now animates exactly like Reaver and Rogue.

## Highlights
- **All kill banners play the real Valorant animation.** The 88 Kingdom Archives skins and the Base banner now move exactly as Rogue's banner does, frame for frame. Rogue's recorded animation was measured frame by frame and turned into a template that every other skin plays with its own emblem, ring, frame and pips, on 1.8.9, 26.2 and 26.3.

## Added
- `tools/killbanner/gen_template.py` (developer tool) measures Rogue's shipped frames into `killbanner/template.properties`, the motion every still skin plays, and can draw Rogue's own layers with the template next to its real frames to check the match.

## Changed
- **Every still skin's banner now looks like the real thing.** The emblem pops in at its place, hops up and drops back; its kill pips fade in a little outside their place and glow in the skin's own colour, then slide in while the ring fades in; the outer frame fades in around it; the kill mark lands and the emblem strobes red as before. From two kills, droplets fly out to the sides. The 5-kill ace drops its ring in together with the emblem and sends its pips round the ring. On the way out the frame goes first, the emblem shrinks and darkens, then the ring, and the pips last. The timing is Rogue's: 1-kill banners move for 52 frames, 2 to 4 kills for 78 to 89, the ace for 223, then hold for what the Duration option leaves, then 16 frames out (14 for the ace).
- The invented parts of the 1.7.3 animation are gone: the emblem no longer drops in from above with an overshoot and a glow behind it, the frame no longer zooms in, the ring no longer opens out, the pips no longer fly in oversized, and no glint runs round the ring.
- Champions 2024 and 2025 (one picture per kill count) still turn from the previous kill's art into this kill's as the mark lands, with Rogue's motion.
- Reaver's and Rogue's own banners are unchanged.

## Fixed
- Kill banners for the still skins no longer play an animation that differs from Reaver's and Rogue's.

## Known limitations
- Rogue's blood splash and Reaver's purple smoke are their own recorded effects; the other skins have no such art, so they play the shared motion with droplets in their accent colour instead.
- A skin's ring, frame, emblem and pips are Kingdom Archives' still pictures; where a skin's picture already includes its ring (Bolt, Cyrax), that ring fades in with the picture it is part of.
