# Lads Client 1.13.13

A new host screen for your own worlds, pictures and GIFs in friend messages, and spectating fixes.

## Highlights

- **New host screen**: Host a world from a sidebar with switches and sliders for every LAN option, plus a Make Operator button.
- **Pictures and GIFs in messages**: send a screenshot or a Tenor/Giphy GIF to a friend and see it in the chat itself.
- **Spectating fixes**: watching a player no longer locks your keys, and effect timers show correctly.

## Added

- **Host screen**: sections for rules, cheats, LAN port, game modes and force settings, Make Operator for a guest, and a render distance cap per guest (26.3). Game rules use the right names on 26.x, and a host can share their resource pack with guests (26.3).
- **Friends button on the Multiplayer screen** (top left, or beside the button already in the corner) that opens your Invites.
- **Friend messages**: pick a screenshot from your screenshots folder, or paste a Tenor or Giphy link to send a GIF. Pictures show inline and GIFs play.

## Changed

- **Invites** have their own tab with Accept and Decline.

## Fixed

- **Messages on 26.x**: friend messages never appeared on 26.x versions.
- **Switching servers**: after about ten world changes, a new server no longer saw you as using the Lads Client.
- **Spectating**: the E key and movement keys work again while you watch a player, and endless effects show an infinity sign instead of a running timer.
