# The Lads Client 1.4.6

## Highlights
- **Valorant kill banners.** The Kill Banner module plays the Reaver and Rogue banners with their own animations and sounds: every variant (Reaver Base, Red, Black and White; Rogue Base, Green, Red and Blue), one to five kills, the HEADSHOT label for head kills, and each skin's way out. The Kill Banner now also works on 1.21.1 and 1.21.11.
- **Sixteen mods are now part of Lads Core** instead of separate downloads. They behave as before, keep their settings files, and do not add new modules to the Lads menu.
- **A tidier title screen and pause menu.** Essential's buttons sit in a row above your account name, the pause menu has a fullscreen button, and buttons grow slightly and glow when you hover over them.
- **Legacy Swing fixes.** Switching items plays the normal pull-out animation again with Legacy Swing on, and the 1.8.9 swing now matches 26.3.

## Added
- **Kill Banner skins.** Style: Base, Reaver or Rogue, with a variant picker for each skin, Headshot Text, Sound, Volume, Duration (1 to 5 seconds) and Size. A kill counts as a headshot when your last hit, within 3 seconds, landed on the top quarter of the target.
- **Fullscreen button** at the top right of the pause menu, using the usual four-corner icon. It does the same as F11.
- **Your account name on the pause menu**, at the bottom left, as on the title screen.

## Changed
- **Built into Lads Core:** Capes, Controlling (with Searchables), Entity Culling, Entity Texture Features, Entity Model Features, Fix Book GUI, Hovering Hotbar, Ksyxis, Lazy AI, NBT Autocomplete, Optimized Cushions, Quick-Pack, Server Pinger Fixer, Fast IP Ping, Tooltips TXF, World Play Time Reborn and ImmediatelyFast. The launcher retires the old separate jars. If you install one of these mods yourself, Lads Core's copy turns itself off.
- **Removed from the pack:** Gamma Utils, Motion Blur Plus, Sound Physics Remastered and Client Sort. Puzzles Lib and Forge Config API Port are removed where no mod needs them any more (they stay on 1.21.11 for Paper Doll).
- **Essential's buttons** (Social, Wardrobe, Pictures, Host world and Essential settings) are Lads buttons in a row at the bottom left of the title screen and pause menu, above your account name, instead of being hidden behind More and "Essential & extras". They show their names when there is room, and icons with tooltips when there isn't.
- **Hover animation.** Title screen and pause menu buttons grow slightly and glow while the pointer is over them.
- **Armor HUD** shows the helmet at the top and the boots at the bottom.
- **FancyMenu runs in modpack mode.** Its customisation overlay and the loading screen mod's (Drippy) title screen button are hidden.
- The Capes, ImmediatelyFast and Entity Culling entries are removed from the Lads menu; these mods now run inside Lads Core with their defaults and existing settings files.

## Fixed
- **Item switching with Legacy Swing on** happened instantly, without the pull-out animation, on every version.
- **1.8.9 Legacy Swing** looked different from 26.3's.
- **The title screen broke after visiting "More" (or Essential's screens).** Essential put its menu back half-hidden when you returned; it now stays in the bottom-left row.

## Known limitations
- Quick-Pack is built from its last MIT-licensed release (1.4.0). It lacks 1.5's faster mipmap and font loading, which only shorten resource reloads.
- Kill banners appear for player kills the server reports in its statistics. The Kill Banner is not available on 1.8.9.
