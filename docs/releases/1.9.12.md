# Lads Client 1.9.12

Lads Client 1.9.12 makes **every version faster** (1.8.9 most of all: Java 25, a new window and input layer, and the Ionium renderer within 3% of OneClient; 1.20.1 up to 2.5x), and brings **The Lads SMP Reborn 0.4.0**: the Arise SMP round, with about 45 new mods and around 70 Arise features rebuilt into the pack.

## Highlights

- **1.8.9 about 50% faster**: 1,883 -> ~2,840 FPS in our benchmark world with OptiFine, with better 1% and 0.1% lows; title screen in ~8 s instead of ~25 s. OptiFine, shaders and every Lads module stay.
- **Ionium is the new default 1.8.9 renderer** (Sodium/Celeritas): ~3,490 FPS in the same world, within 3% of OneClient (~3,590). If you use an OptiFine shader pack you keep OptiFine; anyone can switch back to OptiFine in the launcher's Mods page.
- **1.20.1 up to 2.5x faster, 26.3 faster with many mobs**: 1.20.1 spawn view with the HUD 410 -> ~1,040 FPS, 200 mobs +75%, flying 2.1x; 26.3 200 mobs +33%, flying +12%, HUD-off +14% (40% ahead of OneClient there), lows +25-40%, and it starts in ~25 s (OneClient: 35 s).
- **The Lads SMP Reborn 0.4.0**: parkour, a fishing overhaul, vein mining, biome creepers, Farmer's Delight foods, 190 achievements, mailboxes, Botany Pots, fairy lights, Tom's Storage and much more from Arise SMP.

## Added

- **Server rules** (all versions): mods some servers ban (the minimap and world map, Stack Refill, use-while-mining) are off on servers known to ban them and on servers the client does not know, with a per-server **Bypass** behind a red "at your own risk" warning. Singleplayer, LAN, The Lads SMP and Zero SMP servers keep them.
- **1.8.9 on Java 25** (through 8to25): +8-20% FPS in every Lads benchmark scene and much smoother busy scenes (particle 1% lows +60-80%). The launcher downloads Java 25 by itself.
- **1.8.9 window and input on LWJGL 3 + SDL3** (Pylon): faster startup, lower input and display overhead, no lag with high-polling-rate mice, no stuck keys.
- **Ionium for 1.8.9** (on by default, Mods page): a Sodium-class terrain renderer. While it is on: no OptiFine shaders, CTM/CIT/custom sky or Dynamic Lights; turn it off to get OptiFine back.
- **The Lads SMP Reborn 0.4.0 gameplay** (built into the Lads SMP mod):
  - Parkour: vault, wall jump, ledge grab and climb, slide, dodge roll and breakfall, with a stamina bar.
  - Fishing: hold to charge your cast, a catch minigame, 20 new fish by biome, weather and time, a Fishing Journal, lava fishing with a fireproof line.
  - Vein mining: hold ` and break to mine a whole ore vein or tree (costs hunger and durability).
  - 15 biome creepers (desert, snowy, swamp, jungle, ocean and more), each with its own twist.
  - Farmer's Delight foods and drinks, 190 new achievements, Target Dummy, Botany Pots, mailboxes with delayed delivery, cosmetic armor slots, a holstered weapon on your back, turtle armor, quicksand, themed dungeons.
  - Cauldron upgrades (dyed water, potion mixing), wall lanterns, hanging sign items and more vanilla block upgrades; fairy lights, bunting and garlands between fences.
  - QoL: hide-nametags key, hitmarker, empty-hand key, "inventory full" warning, numeric ping, quit confirmation, trade uses on villager trades, stonecutter memory, chat without brackets, panorama screenshots, world list opens first.
  - Server tools: admin claims, per-dimension access, forced gamerules, item despawn time, spawn blacklist, voice-chat broadcast.
- **The Lads SMP Reborn 0.4.0 mods**: Tom's Storage, Nemo's Woodcutter, More Villagers, It Takes a Pillage, Eternal Nether, Climate Rivers, AdoraBuild Structures, Fast Paintings, ImageFrame, Audio Player, Essential Commands, Vanish, Krypton, Debugify, Chat Patches, Radial, FallingTree, Advanced Netherite, Crawl and more (335 mods in total).
- **The Lads SMP Reborn setup from Arise SMP**: its video, chat, sound and keybind defaults (for new players), server list, Carry On, voice chat, sword blocking, Figura and Emotecraft settings, 7 resource packs and 2 shader packs.

## Changed

- **1.8.9 switches to Ionium** on the first launch after the update, unless you had an OptiFine shader pack selected (then OptiFine stays). Ionium needs the new window layer (Pylon), so it stays off if Pylon is off.
- **1.20.1 and 26.3 profiles**: FancyMenu, Drippy Loading Screen, Konkrete and Melody are no longer installed (the Lads menus and loading screen replace them; they cost 10-14% FPS and ~3 s of startup). 1.20.1 also drops FramePacer (Lads' own input-latency pacing stays): +6-8% FPS, better lows.
- **1.20.1 and 26.3 performance**: the Lads HUD no longer looks up installed mods every frame and is drawn from a cache between updates, and Xaero's minimap is redrawn at the HUD frame-rate cap instead of every frame (1.20.1), entity textures/models skip their pack scans when no pack uses them, Dynamic FPS checks the window less often, capes and other cosmetics allocate less.
- **1.8.9 needs Java 25** (downloaded by the launcher). A custom Java 8 path on the 1.8.9 profile is cleared automatically.

## Fixed

- **1.8.9: clicking two servers quickly** no longer joins both (you saw chat from the other server); **Cancel** while connecting really cancels.
- **Server blocks were saved as Off**: after leaving a server that blocked the Minimap or Stack Refill, they stayed off.
- **Anticheat bans on PvP servers (Minemen.club)**: Stack Refill's hidden inventory clicks looked like an AutoRefill cheat, and 1.7 Animations' "use while mining" started a block or eat mid-dig, a packet order 1.8.9 never sends. Before, both were only turned off on Hypixel; now they follow the new server rules (see Added).
- **Minimap on servers that ban it**: Xaero's Minimap and World Map now switch off on Hypixel, Minemen, PvP Land, Lunar Network, Stray, CubeCraft, MCC Island, Hoplite, Pika, Jartex, BlocksMC, Mineplex and GommeHD (and on unknown servers, see Added), and come back where they are allowed. Settings shows "Server Block" there.
- **26.3 crash about 5 s after taking a screenshot (F2)**: the screenshot toast closed its texture while sliding out.
- **1.20.1 crash when a block falls** (sand, gravel, anvils) with connected textures on.
- **Fullbright on 1.8.9** turned the screen white at any Brightness above 0%: it now lifts brightness step by step as set.
- **1.8.9 HUD with OptiFine Fast Render** (the Lads default): the Lads HUD's 60 FPS cache never worked there, so the whole HUD was redrawn every frame. It now caches properly: about +12% FPS for most 1.8.9 players.
- **1.8.9 input-latency pacing** steps aside above ~2,000 FPS, where it only cost frame rate: +6% FPS on fast PCs.
- **Shaders on 1.8.9** no longer remove your resource packs (OptiFine's shader loader choked on the non-square chest and sign textures baked into the block atlas).
- **1.8.9 sky colour**: a Forge bug recomputed the sky colour from thousands of biomes twice every frame with Fancy graphics (up to +18% FPS on its own).
- **The Lads SMP Reborn**: a chicken jockey could crash the server, the first chicken of each session failed to spawn, and some swamp ruined portals hung world generation.
