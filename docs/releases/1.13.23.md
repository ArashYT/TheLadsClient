# Lads Client 1.13.23

The launcher's Friends tab is now a full social hub: chat with friends, send screenshots and GIFs, and run your party, without starting the game.

## Highlights

- **Chat in the launcher**: click a friend to open a chat. Messages, screenshots and GIFs reach them in game or in their own launcher, and your history stays.
- **Parties in the launcher**: invite friends to a party, accept party invites, and lead it (promote, remove, leave) straight from the Friends tab.
- **Skins**: every friend shows their own Minecraft skin head.

## Added

- Friends tab chat: text messages, unread counts on each friend and on the Friends tab, and a chat history kept per account.
- Send a screenshot or any picture from the picture button (it opens your screenshots folder); the launcher shrinks it to a JPEG under 1 MB.
- Send a GIF by pasting a Tenor or Giphy link; GIFs play in the chat. The GIF button searches Tenor in your browser.
- Party card: members with their skins, the leader, and Promote / Remove / Leave. Party invites and the leader's "follow me to this server" show with Accept and Decline.
- While your game runs, the launcher's chat and party buttons work through the game.

## Fixed

- A party now survives switching between the launcher and the game: the one that takes over is told its party and pending party invites.
