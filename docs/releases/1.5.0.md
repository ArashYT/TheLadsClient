# The Lads Client 1.5.0

## Highlights
- **Sixteen more Lads modules on 1.8.9**: PingView, Threads, Nametags, Kill Banner, Crosshair Tweaks, Chat, AutoReconnect, TabList, RenderScale, FarBlockEntities, AppleSkin, EnhancedTooltips, SignalLoss, BetterScreenshots and Clumps, plus the Discord card as on the other versions. ShulkerBoxUtils is marked unavailable there: shulker boxes arrived in Minecraft 1.11.
- **A new modules list.** Modules that are on are green and modules that are off are red. Click a module to switch it, hover for its description.
- **Nametags** get text shadows, nicknames for other players, your own display name in the tab list and chat, and an option to remove nametag backgrounds, including the one behind Essential's logo.
- **Cancel a launch** while the launcher is preparing the game or while the game is starting.
- **A tidier pause menu** on every version.

## Added
- **Cancel launch.** A Cancel button in the launch screen while the launcher verifies, downloads and installs, and in the "Starting Minecraft" pop-up until the game window opens. A cancelled launch is not treated as a crash.
- **Nametags:** text shadow (on by default), nicknames for other players, your own display name in the tab list and chat (only you see these), and nametags without a background.
- **Module descriptions as tooltips:** rest the pointer on a module for 0.75 seconds.
- **1.8.9 modules:**
  - **Kill Banner** with every skin, variant and sound, and the same settings picker.
  - **Crosshair Tweaks** with every shape, the drawing editor and the bow gap.
  - **AutoReconnect** with its countdown, filters and actions, and **Chat** with its size, background, animation, timestamps and screenshot buttons.
  - **PingView and TabList** (coloured ping numbers, every tab option) and **Threads**.
  - **AppleSkin** (saturation, exhaustion, food previews), **EnhancedTooltips**, **SignalLoss** warnings and **Clumps** (XP orbs merge in your own worlds).
  - **RenderScale**, which draws the world at a lower or higher resolution, and **FarBlockEntities** (chests, signs and heads drawn further away).
  - **BetterScreenshots**: a preview after each screenshot, and a gallery (F10) with open, copy, delete and folder.

## Changed
- **Modules list:** green cards for modules that are on, red for off, no ON/OFF button. Clicking a card switches the module; its Settings button now spans the bottom of the card, is taller and has a gear icon. Cards glow when you hover over them and no longer show descriptions.
- **Pause menu:** Back to Game at the top; Advancements and Statistics; Options and Lads Client; Multiplayer and the world's options; Replays and Extras; and Save and Quit at the bottom, set apart from the rest.
- **Essential:** The Lads Client accepts Essential's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for you (unless you declined them before) and turns off Essential's Discord status. The Mods page says so on Essential's row.

## Fixed
- **Essential's background behind its logo** stayed visible next to name tags on 26.2 when Render Background was off.

## Known limitations
- **1.8.9** sends no damage events, so a Kill Banner kill there counts when your own melee blow (or a server kill message right after it) finishes the target; arrow kills don't count.
- **1.8.9** has no attack cooldown, item cooldowns, spyglass, signed chat or off-hand, so the options for those do nothing there.
- **RenderScale on 1.8.9** switches itself off with OptiFine's Fast Render, antialiasing or a shader pack, and the hand stays at full resolution.
- **AppleSkin on 1.8.9** shows exhaustion only in your own worlds; 1.8.9 servers don't send it.
- **Nicknames** replace whole-word matches in chat, the tab list and every name tag (holograms too), only on your screen.
- **Essential:** accepting its terms for you also skips Essential's own prompt, which asks you to confirm you are 13 or older with a parent's consent, or 18 or older. If you declined Essential's terms before, that choice is kept.
