# Lads Client 1.9.18

Lads Client 1.9.18 fixes three **Zero SMP** problems: the HUD and inventory drawn in the wrong place while Quark's Hotbar Changer rows are open, Shift/Ctrl/Alt + key bindings that could no longer be set, and a key binding the Emotes key took over even after you had changed it.

## Highlights

- **Hotbar Changer rows fixed (Zero SMP)**: with Quark's three hotbar rows open, the armour, hunger and XP bar now move up with the hearts instead of sitting on the rows, and the inventory, its dark background and other mods' text (Mahou's mana) are no longer drawn lower down the screen.
- **Shift + key bindings work again (Zero SMP)**: the key binds screen binds Shift, Ctrl or Alt + a key again, as Forge does.

## Added

- Nothing new.

## Changed

- **Emotes key (B)**: it only takes B from Xaero's New Waypoint or ReplayMod's Player Overview when that binding is still on its default. A binding you changed yourself (for example Shift + B) is left alone.

## Fixed

- Zero SMP: Quark's Hotbar Changer left the armour, hunger and XP bar on its open rows, and pushed the inventory, its dark background and other overlays down the screen. The Lads HUD's Raised lift no longer throws away the move Quark makes for those bars.
- Zero SMP: holding Shift in the key binds screen bound Left Shift itself, so Shift + key bindings could not be set; Reset now also restores a binding's default modifier.
- Shift + B (or another modifier + B) on Xaero's New Waypoint was unbound once by the Emotes key.
