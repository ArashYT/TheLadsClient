# Lads Client 1.13.8

A fix for POV spectating on The Lads Network (26.3).

## Highlights

- POV spectating shows the player's whole hotbar, and your hand no longer sways when you move the mouse.

## Fixed

- **POV spectating hotbar**: when a server locks your view to another player (POV spectate in the PvP arena and duels), the hotbar, hearts and bars now show everything the server sends, instead of only the item in that player's hand.
- **POV spectating hand**: moving the mouse no longer swings the held item around while your view is locked to someone else.

## Known limitations

- Only the 26.3 client has this fix so far.
