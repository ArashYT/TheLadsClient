# Lads Client 1.13.5

A small update: friends' skins in the Friends tab, clearer hosting errors and a quicker start on 26.3.

## Highlights

- Your friends' Minecraft skins in the Friends tab.
- Start Hosting tells you what went wrong instead of doing nothing.

## Added

- **Friend skin heads**: every row in the Friends tab shows that friend's Minecraft skin head (face and hat layer), with an online dot on its corner.

## Changed

- **Faster 26.3 start**: the old-log cleanup now runs in the background instead of holding up startup.

## Fixed

- **Start Hosting**: when the world can't be opened to friends, a toast now says why instead of nothing happening.
