# Lads Client 1.14.1

A smoother title screen, a tidier launcher, and a batch of fixes for skins, boss bars, tray icons and friend chat.

## Added

- Title screen: a "Layout" menu to switch between the Legacy and New layout. It replaces the "Legacy layout" button.
- Title screen: buttons grow slightly when you hover them, and the LADS CLIENT logo has a faint red glow that sweeps across it.
- Launcher: Experiences has its own place in the side menu, right below Play.
- Launcher: a new modpack can start with the same controls as your Lads profile. Experimental Horizon takes them over by itself the first time it starts.

## Changed

- Title screen: the Panorama and Layout menus stay open after you pick something. They close when you click elsewhere or move the mouse away.
- Title screen: the arrows on Singleplayer and Multiplayer move smoothly at any refresh rate.
- Title screen: the Update button looks like the one in the launcher.
- Multiplayer screen: the buttons at the top sit on one line, and the grid button now says "Columns".
- Launcher: the Servers page starts with your server list, without the description above it.
- The game and the launcher now always show the same version number.

## Fixed

- Your skin on the title screen no longer turns into Alex after a slow or failed skin lookup.
- The server rules notice no longer covers the boss bar. Boss bars and the Jade tooltip move below it while it shows.
- Screenshots you just took now show up when you attach a picture to a message in game.
- The launcher no longer leaves old icons behind in the Windows tray after it closes.
- Launcher Friends page: every friend has visible buttons, and the picture and GIF buttons line up with the message box.
