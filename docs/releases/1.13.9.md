# Lads Client 1.13.9

A big update: friends who join your world get your mods by themselves, block-hitting works with any item, the title screen gets an Update button and a panorama picker, and The Lads Network gets a real spectator camera.

## Highlights

- **ModSync**: accept a friend's invite and the mods their world needs are fetched for you.
- **Block-hit with anything**: the 1.7 block pose and block-hit swing work with any item or block.
- **Update from the title screen**: one button updates the launcher and the game and starts your version again.
- **Spectator camera** on The Lads Network (26.3) and a new **Twerking** emote.

## Added

- **ModSync** (26.3 and 1.20.1): when a friend's hosted world uses mods you do not have, the client gets them before you join. Mods that are on Modrinth or CurseForge are downloaded from there. A mod that exists only on your friend's PC is sent to you directly, and only after you answer one red "trust this host's mods?" question. Every file is checked against its hash, then the game restarts with those mods and rejoins the world by itself. Your own mods and settings are never changed: the next normal Play is your own setup again. A game that runs a friend's mods only joins that friend's world, your own PC, your LAN and Lads servers.
- **Update button** on the title screen (every version): it shows when a launcher update is ready. One press closes the game, installs the update and starts the same version again.
- **Change panorama** button on the title screen (every version): steps through the menu backgrounds of other Minecraft releases.
- **Spectator camera** (26.3, The Lads Network): spectating in the PvP arena now uses a real camera on the player you watch, with their hotbar and hearts, a "SPECTATING name" bar, and next or previous player on the arrow keys and mouse buttons.
- **Twerking emote** with jiggle physics (26.3, 26.2, 1.20.1 and 1.8.9).
- **Verified Lads Client**: a server that uses the Lads API can now check that a player really runs the Lads Client. The proof is tied to your Minecraft account and sends nothing else about you. The Lads Network uses it for its client perks, so older clients need this update to keep them.
- **Window icon**: the game window and the launcher carry the Lads logo in every size.
- **Server-set settings** (26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11 and 1.20.1): a server that uses the Lads API can set or block client settings while you are on it, per world or game mode. The Lads Network lobby turns Fullbright off this way. It lasts only for that visit: your saved settings are never changed, and your own setup is back the moment you leave.

## Changed

- **Block-hit and 1.7 animations**: on 1.20.1, 1.21.11, 26.2 and 26.3 any item in your main hand takes the block pose while your off-hand shield blocks, blocks and 3D items use the 1.7 block pose, and an attack click swings over any use pose (shield, spyglass, trident, modded items). On 1.8.9 a block or 3D item in use keeps the 1.7 swing. This is drawn on your screen only; nothing extra is sent to the server.
- **Bundled mods updated**: Sodium 0.8.14 with Iris 1.10.8 on 1.21.11, Xaero's Minimap and World Map, ViaFabricPlus, Simple Voice Chat 2.6.27, JEI, Not Enough Animations, More Culling and smaller ones on 26.3, 26.2 and 1.21.11; Pylon on 1.8.9.

## Fixed

- **Chat keys** (26.3 and 1.20.1): Up, Down and Tab stay in the chat box. Up brings back your last message and Tab completes commands.
- **Start Hosting did nothing**: a saved "Off" choice made the button stop hosting without a word. The host screen no longer offers Off, and its options no longer run off the right edge on a small window.
- **"Game crashed" after a normal close**: the launcher no longer reports a crash when the game simply closed, and the 26.3 client now finishes its background work before it closes, which stops a graphics driver crash on exit.
- **Flat grey menu background**: the Panorama Version setting showed plain grey for newer Minecraft releases. The real images are now downloaded and checked.
- **POV spectating** (26.3, from the unreleased 1.13.8): the whole hotbar shows, and your hand no longer sways when you move the mouse.

## Known limitations

- ModSync and the spectator camera were tested on 26.3 only. ModSync is also built into 1.20.1; the other versions do not have either yet.
- The Twerking emote is not on 1.21.11, which has no emotes.
- The Update button does not show in games started from the Experiences and Modpacks pages.
- Server-set settings were not tested on 1.8.9.
