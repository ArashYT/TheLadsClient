# Lads Client 1.9.1

Lads Client 1.9.1 brings quality-of-life mods and accurate ping display to Hypixel, introduces Wavy Capes and primed TNT countdown timers, adds a customizable Action Bar module, brings custom skin menus to 1.8.9, and delivers expanded kill banner detection and HUD improvements across 1.8.9, 1.20.1, and 26.3.

## Highlights

- **Hypixel mod suite and real ping display.** A dedicated settings tab adds Auto GG, Auto Tip, Chat Cleanup, and accurate ping display on Hypixel.
- **Action Bar module and smart placement.** Full action bar customization with adjustable size and look, while kill banners automatically dodge active action bars.
- **Hypixel kill banners and audio controls.** Kill banners catch every kill across Hypixel modes, 1.8.9 audio plays in stereo, and game settings gain volume sliders.
- **Wavy Capes and TNT Timer.** Smooth physics cape animations built into the client, plus color-coded countdown timers above primed TNT and lit minecarts.
- **1.8.9 Skin Customization and changer.** Modern skin layer settings come to 1.8.9 alongside an in-game skin changer supporting links, files, and usernames.
- **Text shadows and HUD layout.** Text shadow is on by default client-wide, Coordinates displays facing direction, and chat sits above the armor bar.
- **Combo Sounds library, trimmer, and bow sounds.** Sound library with search and favourites, built-in waveform trimmer, sound sets, unlimited combo sounds, and long shot and headshot bow sounds.

## Added

- **Hypixel settings tab**: dedicated Hypixel tab with Auto GG (on), Auto Tip (on, every 15 min), Chat Cleanup (on), Auto Friend (off), Auto Play Again (off), and Auto Who (off), with commands spaced at least 1.5 s apart.
- **Chat Cleanup**: hides lobby join spam, Soul Well and box finds, gifts, tips, boosters, store ads, Watchdog news and Karma, with optional filters for coin/XP lines and other players' ads.
- **Sound volume sliders**: new Kill Banners and Combo Sounds sliders in Options > Music & Sounds.
- **Action Bar module** (on by default): move, resize (50–200%), hide, and customize background colour/opacity, text colour, and duration/fade, with text shadow on by default.
- **Coordinates facing direction**: Coordinates shows facing direction (N/NE.. + axes) on the right ("Show Direction", on by default).
- **Wavy Capes module** (on by default): physics cape animation (Smooth/Blocky, wind, weight, smoothness, other players; works with Lads, vanilla and OptiFine capes).
- **TNT Timer module** (on by default): floating countdown ("3.45s" green->yellow->red) above primed TNT and lit TNT minecarts, with customizable size, background, shadow, and through-blocks option.
- **1.8.9 Skin Customization menu**: Options > Skin Customization on 1.8.9 is the Lads menu (turnable preview, every layer toggle, 3D layers).
- **1.8.9 Skin changer**: switch skins via username, UUID, link or PNG with classic and slim models, saved between sessions and rendered with 3D Skin Layers.
- **Sound library**: every Combo / Kill Banner / bow sound slot has Choose: one list with search and favourites, Play, Trim, Delete, Upload.
- **Trimmer**: drag over the waveform to keep up to 12 s of any MP3/WAV (files up to 5 minutes), preview, save.
- **Sound Sets**: save Combo 1-5 as named sets and swap them in one click ("Default pling" built in).
- **After Combo 5**: unlimited extra sounds, played Random (never the same twice in a row), In Order, or Shuffle.
- **Bow sounds**: Long Shot sound when your arrow hits a player from 50+ blocks (10-120 adjustable, mobs optional) and a Headshot sound for arrow hits to the head; built-in chimes until you pick your own.

## Changed

- **Text shadow on by default**: text shadow is On by default (configs on the old default Soft switch once); boss bar names, Signal Loss, and crosshair counters follow it.
- **Chat above armor bar**: chat sits above the armor bar in survival including absorption ("Above Armor Bar" in Chat, on by default).
- **Server action bars**: server action bars including Hypixel show exactly as sent.
- **Kill banner action bar avoidance**: kill banners that start while an action bar shows play just above it.
- **1.8.9 kill banner and Combo Sounds audio**: 1.8.9 kill banner and Combo Sounds play in both ears (were one-sided) and the Base chime is as loud as game sounds.
- **Wavey Capes mod replacement**: the separate Wavey Capes mod is removed from the 1.20.1 and 26.3 packs (built-in Wavy Capes replaces it).

## Fixed

- **Hypixel ping display**: on Hypixel and servers that fake everyone's ping as 1 ms, your Ping HUD and tab list show your real ping; other players show "-" because Hypixel doesn't send their real ping.
- **Hypixel kill banner detection**: kill banners catch every Hypixel kill (cosmetic kill messages, fireball/rod knock-offs, Pit multi kills, /nick, "+coins (Kill)" lines), one banner per kill.
- **Kill streak tip notices**: tip notices no longer reset your kill streak.
- **1.20.1 Kill Banner settings tiles**: 1.20.1 Kill Banner settings tiles show their banners again.
- **Keystrokes editor box**: Keystrokes editor box matches the keys exactly, fixing misaligned snapping.
- **Damaged MP3 files**: a damaged MP3 is refused with a message instead of failing.
