# Lads Client 1.9.1

Lads Client 1.9.1 brings quality-of-life mods and accurate ping display to Hypixel, introduces Wavy Capes and primed TNT countdown timers, adds a customizable Action Bar module, brings custom skin menus to 1.8.9, and delivers expanded kill banner detection and HUD improvements across 1.8.9, 1.20.1, and 26.3.

## Highlights

- **Hypixel settings tab and real ping display.** A dedicated "Hypixel" settings tab (active only on Hypixel) adds Auto GG (on), Auto Tip (on, every 15 min), Chat Cleanup (on), Auto Friend (off), Auto Play Again (off), and Auto Who (off) with commands spaced at least 1.5 s apart. On Hypixel and 1 ms servers, Ping HUD and tab list now show your real ping, while other players show "-" because Hypixel doesn't send their ping.
- **Action Bar module and smart placement.** New Action Bar module lets you move, resize (50–200%), hide, and customize action bars with background colour/opacity, text colour, duration, fade, and text shadow on by default (on by default; its default place and look are vanilla's); server action bars including Hypixel show exactly as sent, and kill banners that start while an action bar shows play just above it.
- **Hypixel kill banners and audio improvements.** Kill banners catch every Hypixel kill (cosmetic kill messages, fireball/rod knock-offs, Pit multi kills, /nick, "+coins (Kill)" lines), one banner per kill; 1.8.9 kill banner and Combo Sounds play in both ears (were one-sided) and the Base chime is as loud as game sounds; new Kill Banners and Combo Sounds sliders in Options > Music & Sounds; tip notices no longer reset your kill streak; and 1.20.1 Kill Banner settings tiles show their banners again.
- **Wavy Capes and TNT Timer.** New Wavy Capes brings physics cape animation (Smooth/Blocky, wind, weight, smoothness, other players; works with Lads, vanilla and OptiFine capes; on by default; replaces the separate Wavey Capes mod on 1.20.1 and 26.3), and new TNT Timer displays a floating countdown ("3.45s" green->yellow->red) above primed TNT and lit TNT minecarts with customizable size, background, shadow, and a through blocks option (on by default).
- **1.8.9 Skin Customization and Skin changer.** 1.8.9 Options > Skin Customization is now the Lads menu (turnable preview, every layer toggle, 3D layers), accompanied by a new Skin changer on 1.8.9 (username, UUID, link or PNG, slim/classic, saved between sessions, shows on your player including 3D Skin Layers).
- **Text shadows and HUD alignment.** Text shadow is On by default (configs on the old default Soft switch once), with boss bar names, Signal Loss, and crosshair counters following it; Coordinates shows facing direction (N/NE.. + axes) on the right ("Show Direction", on); chat sits above the armor bar in survival including absorption ("Above Armor Bar" in Chat, on); and Keystrokes editor box matches the keys exactly.

## Added

- **Hypixel settings tab**: new "Hypixel" settings tab, only active on Hypixel: Auto GG (on), Auto Tip (on, every 15 min), Chat Cleanup (on), Auto Friend (off), Auto Play Again (off), Auto Who (off). Every automatic command is spaced at least 1.5 s apart.
- **Chat Cleanup**: hides lobby join spam, Soul Well/box finds, gifts, tips, boosters, store ads, Watchdog news and Karma; optional filters for coin/XP lines and other players' ads.
- **Sound settings volume sliders**: new Kill Banners and Combo Sounds sliders in Options > Music & Sounds.
- **Action Bar module**: new Action Bar module to move, resize (50–200%), hide, and customize background colour/opacity, text colour, and duration/fade (text shadow on by default; on by default).
- **Coordinates facing direction**: Coordinates shows facing direction (N/NE.. + axes) on the right ("Show Direction", on).
- **Wavy Capes module**: new Wavy Capes (Smooth/Blocky, wind, weight, smoothness, other players; works with Lads, vanilla and OptiFine capes; on by default).
- **TNT Timer module**: new TNT Timer ("3.45s" green->yellow->red, primed TNT + lit TNT minecarts, size/background/shadow, through blocks option; on by default).
- **1.8.9 Skin Customization menu**: 1.8.9 Options > Skin Customization is the Lads menu (turnable preview, every layer toggle, 3D layers).
- **1.8.9 Skin changer**: new Skin changer on 1.8.9 (username, UUID, link or PNG, slim/classic, saved between sessions, shows on your player incl. 3D Skin Layers).

## Changed

- **Text shadow On by default**: text shadow is On by default (configs on the old default Soft switch once); boss bar names, Signal Loss, and crosshair counters follow it.
- **Chat above armor bar**: chat sits above the armor bar in survival incl. absorption ("Above Armor Bar" in Chat, on).
- **Server action bars**: server action bars incl. Hypixel show exactly as sent.
- **Kill banner action bar avoidance**: kill banners that start while an action bar shows play just above it.
- **1.8.9 kill banner and Combo Sounds audio**: 1.8.9 kill banner + Combo Sounds play in both ears (were one-sided) and the Base chime is as loud as game sounds.
- **Wavey Capes mod replacement**: the separate Wavey Capes mod is removed from the 1.20.1 and 26.3 packs (Wavy Capes replaces it).

## Fixed

- **Hypixel ping display**: on Hypixel (and servers that fake everyone's ping as 1 ms), your Ping HUD and tab list show your real ping; other players show "-" because Hypixel doesn't send their real ping.
- **Hypixel kill banner detection**: kill banners catch every Hypixel kill (cosmetic kill messages, fireball/rod knock-offs, Pit multi kills, /nick, "+coins (Kill)" lines), one banner per kill.
- **Kill streak tip notices**: tip notices no longer reset your kill streak.
- **1.20.1 Kill Banner settings tiles**: 1.20.1 Kill Banner settings tiles show their banners again.
- **Keystrokes editor box**: Keystrokes editor box matches the keys exactly.
