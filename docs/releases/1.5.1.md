# The Lads Client 1.5.1

## Highlights
- **1.7 Animations is here, on every version.** The module was missing from the Lads menu; it now brings back Minecraft 1.7's combat and item animations on 1.8.9, 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.2 and 26.3.

## Added
- **1.7 Animations** module, with one switch for each part:
  - **Blockhitting** and the **1.7 sword block pose**. On 1.21.x and 26.x, which have no sword blocking, hold a sword and block with a shield: the shield is hidden and the sword blocks the 1.7 way.
  - The **1.7 bow, fishing rod and eating/drinking positions**, and **swinging while using items**.
  - **1.7 third-person items** and blocking arm, on you and on other players.
  - **Red armour on hurt**, **no heart flashing**, the **instant sneak camera** and **2D dropped items**.
  - **Low Fire** and, on 1.21.x and 26.x, **Low Shield** and **no attack-cooldown dip**.
- Legacy Swing, Vertical Bobbing and Old Damage Tilt stay separate modules and work together with 1.7 Animations.

## Fixed
- **"1.7 Animations" was missing from the Lads menu** on every version.
