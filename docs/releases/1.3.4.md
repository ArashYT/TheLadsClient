# The Lads Client 1.3.4

## Highlights
- Vulkan comes first, with OpenGL available for compatibility and Iris shaders.
- Find screenshots from Modrinth, CurseForge and Prism in one gallery.
- Record and revisit your gameplay with Flashback.
- Arrange your HUD faster with centered groups and quick On/Off controls.

## Added
- Automatic screenshot discovery, **Rescan**, source labels and custom folders. External originals stay in their own instance.
- **Multiplayer** and **Replays** shortcuts in the pause menu.
- Right-click **Group**, **Ungroup** and **Center stack** in the HUD editor.

## Fixed
- Changing a keybind keeps the controls list at the same scroll position.
- Autohide fades Lads HUD widgets along with Minecraft's hotbar.

## Changed
- A cleaner What's New window with short highlights and optional full notes.
- Vulkan applies to Minecraft 26.2/26.3. Older versions use OpenGL. Iris shader mods resume with OpenGL; saved mod choices are kept.
- Flashback downloads directly from its official Modrinth release and remains a third-party mod.
