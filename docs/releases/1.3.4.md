# The Lads Client 1.3.4

## Highlights
- Vulkan comes first on 26.2 and 26.3, with OpenGL available for compatibility and Iris shaders.
- Find screenshots from Modrinth, CurseForge and Prism in one gallery.
- Record and revisit your gameplay with Flashback.
- Arrange your HUD faster with centered groups and quick On/Off controls.

## Added
- Automatic screenshot discovery, **Rescan**, source labels, and custom folders you can add or remove. External originals stay in their own instance.
- **Multiplayer** and **Replays** shortcuts in the 26.2 and 26.3 pause menu.
- Right-click **Group**, **Ungroup** and **Center stack** in the HUD editor.

## Fixed
- Changing a keybind keeps the controls list at the same scroll position.
- On 26.2 and 26.3, Autohide fades Lads HUD widgets along with Minecraft's hotbar.
- Large downloads such as Flashback no longer time out on slower connections.

## Changed
- A cleaner What's New window with short highlights and optional full notes.
- Grouped HUDs in one column now share its center and width. Use **Ungroup** to separate any you want apart.
- Older versions keep OpenGL. Iris shader mods resume with OpenGL; saved mod choices are kept.
- Flashback downloads directly from its official Modrinth release and remains a third-party mod.
