# The Lads Client 1.4.4

## Highlights
- **New Lads loading screen on every version.** It replaces the Mojang Studios screen with the Lads Client logo on the dark burgundy theme and a Windows 11 style spinner. The spinner is drawn live every frame, not a video or GIF, so it animates at your full frame rate.
- **Minecraft 1.8.9 gets the full Lads look:** the Lads title screen, the More and Accounts screens, the redesigned pause menu, Lads-styled buttons everywhere and dark Lads backgrounds instead of dirt.
- **Minecraft 1.8.9 launches again**, on Forge with OptiFine. It now includes **Essential** and **Resourcify**.
- **The HUD FPS limiter no longer flickers.** The HUD stays on screen and updates at the frame rate you choose. On 1.21.11, 26.2 and 26.3 the limit now covers the whole in-game HUD: vanilla, other mods and the Lads HUD.
- **The game starts at Realtime priority** and drops back to Normal once it has loaded.
- **The Forge advert page no longer opens** in your browser when 1.8.9 installs or launches.

## Added
- **Lads loading screen.** It shows at startup and on resource reloads (F3+T, resource pack changes). The burgundy gradient, glow, logo, spinner and a thin progress bar replace vanilla's red Mojang screen, and the fade into the title screen is unchanged. On 1.8.9 it replaces Forge's loading screen and now runs at your monitor's refresh rate instead of Forge's 100 FPS cap.
- **1.8.9 title screen.** Singleplayer, Multiplayer, Lads Mods, Options, More... and Quit, on the same artwork as the newer versions. More holds Forge's Mods list, Language, Realms, Accounts and Essential's actions (Host world, Social, Wardrobe, Pictures, Essential settings). Turn off the "TitleScreen" module for the vanilla screen.
- **1.8.9 pause menu.** It has the Lads logo and a two-column grid, plus Multiplayer (which asks before leaving your world) and Lads Client. Essential's buttons sit behind "Essential & extras...".
- **1.8.9 buttons and backgrounds.** Every vanilla and mod button uses the Lads button style. Menus and lists (worlds, servers, packs, controls, language) use the dark Lads background.
- **Essential and Resourcify on 1.8.9.** Both download with the 1.8.9 pack and run alongside OptiFine and the Lads Core. Essential brings the newer Mixin that Resourcify's OptiFine shader screen needs, so keep both on together.
- **Whole-HUD FPS limit on 1.21.11, 26.2 and 26.3.** At the chosen rate the HUD is rebuilt (vanilla HUD, mod HUDs such as minimaps, and the Lads HUD). On the frames in between, the last build is drawn again. The crosshair and the F3 debug screen are unaffected. On 1.21.1 and 1.8.9 the limit still applies to the Lads HUD only.
- **Realtime startup priority.** While the game loads, the launcher raises it to Realtime. Windows grants High unless the launcher runs as administrator. Once loading finishes ("Sound engine started") or after 3 minutes, the game goes back to Normal so it can't starve your mouse, keyboard or audio during play.

## Fixed
- **1.8.9 was stuck on "Verifying Java 8".** 1.4.2 added Essential to the 1.8.9 pack. Essential's jar is a Forge loader with no mod information, so the launcher downloaded about 64 MB with no progress shown, then refused it as a "Wrong Fabric mod". The launcher now recognises Essential's jar, and mod downloads show their progress on the launch screen.
- **The Forge advert page (adfoc.us) opened in the browser.** The Forge installer library opened it on every Forge install. The launcher now installs Forge 1.8.9 itself from the official installer, pinned by its SHA-256, and the files it writes are identical. Nothing opens a browser any more.
- **The HUD FPS limiter made the HUD flicker.** The limit (on at 60 by default) skipped drawing the HUD on the frames between updates.
- **1.8.9 keeps its own worlds again.** 1.4.2 linked your shared `.minecraft` saves, resource packs and shader packs into 1.8.9, and removed the safety checks in both the launcher and the game. Opening a newer world in 1.8.9 corrupts it. The links are removed on the next launch (your shared content is untouched), and 1.8.9 uses its own folders and options.txt again. The server list is still shared.
- **Module settings arrows.** On a `< value >` option, the left side now goes back and the right side forward. Before, both sides went forward. This applies to every version.
- **1.8.9 Raw Input** no longer writes 55 "security seal" errors to the log on every start.
- Window titles and the title screen show the real version, not a hard-coded "1.4.2".
- The 1.8.9 module list no longer marks the non-existent "OldAnimations" and "Crosshair" entries as built in. 1.7 Animations and Crosshair Tweaks are not on 1.8.9 yet.
- The launcher's "wrong mod downloaded" errors no longer say "Fabric" on Forge.
- The 1.8.9 in-game self-test is updated for the modules added in 1.4.1 and 1.4.2 and covers the new screens (92 checks). The 26.2 in-world test passes again.

## Known limitations
- On the newer versions, some mods hold up the game for a few seconds during startup, and the loading screen holds still until they finish. The vanilla screen did the same.
- If Forge's splash screen is turned off in `config/splash.properties` on 1.8.9, the vanilla Mojang screen shows instead.
- On 1.8.9, smooth scrolling, the controls search and the pack filter from the newer versions are not available yet.
