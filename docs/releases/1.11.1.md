# Lads Client 1.11.1

Lads Client 1.11.1 adds an invite-only experimental mode for testers: Lads Horizon, an early Lads Client for Minecraft 26.3 built on Horizon, a Minecraft 26.3 rewritten from scratch in Rust. Nothing changes for anyone who is not invited: the option does not show and every version plays as before.

## Highlights

- **Lads Horizon (experimental, invite only)** (launcher, 26.3): invited players can turn on Lads Horizon in Settings > General > Experimental. Play on a 26.3 profile then starts Lads Horizon instead of the Lads Client. It is off by default, and turning it on shows a red "use at your own risk" warning first.

## Added

- **Lads Horizon** (launcher, 26.3): built on Horizon (github.com/ludovic111/horizon, MIT), which reads your own Minecraft 26.3 files: the launcher downloads Lads Horizon once, prepares it from your 26.3 install the first time (about a minute) and keeps its worlds apart from your Lads worlds. Your Microsoft account signs in as usual.
- **Lads look in Lads Horizon**: The Lads Client window, icon and title screen.
- **Lads HUD in Lads Horizon**: FPS, CPS, Keystrokes, Coordinates and Lads Zoom (C, scroll while held to zoom further), each on or off and in any corner (Options > Mods > Lads HUD).
- **Lads Modules screen in Lads Horizon**: Right Shift lists every Lads Client module; the ones not in Lads Horizon yet are greyed out as "Coming soon to Lads Horizon".
- **Server rules in Lads Horizon**: Fullbright, Auto Reconnect, inventory drag and sort, the minimap, mod commands and long-range block info stay off on servers that ban them and on unknown servers; Horizon Settings > Lads Rules can allow one on a single server after a red warning.
- **Crash reports** (launcher): if Lads Horizon crashes, its crash report goes to the Lads team like the Lads Client's, only when Send crash reports is on.

## Changed

- **Experimental access** (launcher): the Experimental section shows only for Microsoft accounts the Lads team invited.
