# Lads Client 1.9.11

Lads Client 1.9.11 closes the last portable feature gaps between Minecraft **1.8.9** and **26.3**: **Jade**, **Minimap** and **Dynamic FPS** now work on 1.8.9 too.

## Highlights

- **Jade on 1.8.9**: the look-at info panel for the block or mob under your crosshair is built into the 1.8.9 client, with its own settings screen.
- **Minimap on 1.8.9**: the launcher's 1.8.9 pack now installs **Xaero's Minimap**, controlled by the Lads **Minimap** module as on 26.3.
- **Dynamic FPS on 1.8.9**: background, minimized and idle frame-rate profiles with volume fades, replacing 1.8.9's simpler Unfocused FPS cap.

## Added

- **Jade (`1.8.9`)**: names the block, fluid or mob you look at, with its icon and mod, plus health and armor, crop growth, redstone power, repeater delay, comparator mode, lever state, note block instrument and pitch (pitch in singleplayer only), cauldron level, furnace state, spawner mob, sign text, banner layers, harvest tool and level, baby/owner/horse speed and jump, and the item in an item frame. **Settings and addons** opens its settings: every info line, fluids, only while sneaking, hide in creative, hide with F3, position (9 anchors with offsets), scale and opacity. Off on Hypixel, as on every version. Container contents, villager levels and container names are not shown: 1.8.9 servers never send them to the client.
- **Minimap (`1.8.9`)**: the 1.8.9 pack downloads **Xaero's Minimap** 21.10.41 from Modrinth. The Lads **Minimap** switch turns Xaero's map on and off (Xaero's own toggle and settings change the switch too), hides it on Hypixel, and **Map and waypoint settings** opens Xaero's settings. Waypoints, the map's position (Xaero's own HUD editor) and server rules stay Xaero's. Xaero's start-up Patreon and update checks are skipped: their server always answered too late, which cost about 2 seconds of every game start, and the launcher keeps the mod updated.
- **Dynamic FPS (`1.8.9`)**: as on 26.3: **Unfocused FPS** (60, or 15 in Aggressive mode) and 25% volume while the window is in the background, **Hidden FPS** (30, or 5) with no sound and no rendering while minimized, 60 FPS while the mouse is over the unfocused window, and 10 FPS after 5 minutes without input. **Balanced** waits 3 seconds before slowing down, so alt-tabbing costs nothing. Volume fades in and out. 0 FPS pauses rendering; it never raises your own FPS limit. **Background Profiles** edits the volumes and the idle profile.

## Changed

- **Unfocused FPS cap (`1.8.9`) is now Dynamic FPS**: if you had changed the cap, its on/off and FPS limit carry over to Dynamic FPS (unless you had already changed Dynamic FPS on another version).
- **Launcher**: Forge mod ids are read case-insensitively, so the 1.8.9 pack can manage mods such as Xaero's Minimap whose id has capitals.

## Fixed

- **1.8.9 module list**: Jade, Minimap and Dynamic FPS no longer show as "Unavailable" on 1.8.9.
