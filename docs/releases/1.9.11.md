# Lads Client 1.9.11

Lads Client 1.9.11 brings the first **cosmetic: the Groove emote**, seen by every Lads player around you, fixes **1.7 Animations** for the trident, the off hand and modded items, rebuilds **Better F3** to look like the classic BetterF3 and stop glitching when you spam F3, starts **shaders only when you ask for them** on 1.20.1 and 26.3, launches **CurseForge, Prism and Modrinth instances straight from the Lads launcher**, and closes the last portable gaps on **1.8.9** (Jade, Minimap, Dynamic FPS).

## Highlights

- **Emotes (all versions)**: hold **B** for the emote wheel and pick **Groove**, a looping dance. Move and it hands over smoothly to your walk; in first person you see your own arms dance. Other Lads Client players on the same server see it too.
- **1.7 Animations fixed for the off hand, the trident and modded items**: the off hand is now a true mirror of the main hand (no more giant bow in your face), the trident keeps its real placement, and every item you can use, modded ones included, gets the right 1.7 pose or keeps its own.
- **Better F3, the classic look**: colored keys and values, two columns, no boxes, and it slides in and out smoothly however fast you press F3. Every line can be switched off.
- **Shaders on demand (1.20.1 + 26.3)**: Iris now sits out until you want shaders, so the game starts faster and runs lighter. **Shader packs...** in Video Settings restarts the game with shaders and puts you back in your world or server.
- **External instances launch directly**: CurseForge, Prism/MultiMC and Modrinth App instances in the launcher's External tab now start Minecraft right away with your Lads account, without opening their launcher.
- **1.8.9 parity**: Jade, Minimap (Xaero's) and Dynamic FPS on 1.8.9.

## Added

- **Emotes (`1.8.9`, `1.20.1`, `26.3`)**: **Emotes** key (default **B**): hold to open an 8-slot wheel, release over **Groove** to play; tap B again or move to stop. Groove is a 4-beat step-touch at 115 BPM: knee bounce, hip sway, fists pumping in front of your chest, head nodding. Starting blends in over 0.2 s; moving, jumping, sneaking, attacking, using an item, taking damage, dying or changing dimension blends it out over 0.3 s into the normal walk. In first person both dancing arms are shown, then your hand comes back up. Works with 3D skin layers and capes, in the inventory preview and in F5.
- **Cosmetics module**: **Show other players' emotes** and **Share my emotes** (both on). Emotes reach other Lads Client players on the same server through the Lads cosmetics service (your Minecraft account is verified the same way servers verify it); nothing is ever sent to the Minecraft server, so it is safe on Hypixel and every other server.
- **Better F3 options**: Style (BetterF3 / Lads classic), Background, Text Shadow, Slide In + Animation Speed, Hide Lads HUD, Rainbow, Axis Colors, Show Other Lines, key and value colors per section, and a switch for every line.
- **Shaders on demand (`1.20.1`)**: as on 26.3: Iris and its add-ons are switched off until you turn shaders on with **Shader packs...** (Video Settings); **Turn shaders off (restart)** goes back. If you already used a shader pack on 1.20.1, shaders stay on after the update.
- **External instances (launcher)**: **Play** on a CurseForge, Prism/MultiMC or Modrinth App instance starts the game directly with your Lads account and the instance's own memory and Java arguments. Minecraft versions, libraries and loaders go into the Lads cache; nothing is written into the other launcher's folders, so the instance keeps working there. **↗** opens it in its own launcher. Instances the Lads launcher cannot start safely (Prism jar mods or custom components, LiteLoader) open in their own launcher and say why.
- **Jade, Minimap and Dynamic FPS on `1.8.9`**: the look-at info panel, Xaero's Minimap controlled by the Lads Minimap module (downloaded by the 1.8.9 pack), and background/minimized/idle frame-rate profiles with volume fades (replacing the old Unfocused FPS cap; your settings carry over).

## Changed

- **Better F3 default look**: left column FPS, XYZ (colored per axis), Block, Particles, Entities, then Dimension, Facing, Rotation, Light (client and server), Highest Block (client and server), Biome, Local Difficulty, Day Ticks, Days Played, Slime Chunk; right column Targeted Block and Block ID, then time, Java, memory, allocation rate, CPU, display, GPU, GPU utilization, OpenGL and driver. Text shadow on, no background boxes. Everyone is switched to the new defaults once; your other Better F3 settings stay. Lines a version cannot know are hidden there (on 1.8.9: server light, heightmaps, GPU utilization).
- **HUD colors**: **Plates** is now **Backgrounds** and **Shadow** is now **Text Shadow**; both sit next to the **Colors** button in the mod menu and in the HUD editor (they used to be inside the Colors popup). Text Shadow is now a plain on/off switch (its "Soft" step looked exactly like On).
- **1.7 Animations rules**: eating/drinking, blocking and bows (including modded bows and a charging crossbow) get the 1.7 poses; the trident, spears, spyglass, goat horn, brush, bundle, a loaded crossbow, riptide and any modded use animation keep their own placement with the 1.7 swing. A modded item that is used without a use animation (guns, staffs) keeps its own transform.
- **Unfocused FPS cap (`1.8.9`) is now Dynamic FPS** (see Added).
- **Launcher**: Forge mod ids are read case-insensitively.

## Fixed

- **1.7 Animations: off-hand items** were held by the wrong corner: a bow in the off hand filled the screen and an off-hand sword pointed the wrong way. Same fix for Main Arm: Left.
- **1.7 Animations: trident** (26.3) was drawn as a giant flat icon in the bow pose; riptide stacked two spins.
- **Better F3 animation**: pressing F3 again within half a second showed the lines already finished or half slid in, and a slow first frame could skip the slide. It now reverses smoothly from where it is, slides out on close, and never pops.
- **Better F3 (`1.8.9`)**: Xaero's Minimap no longer draws under the F3 text when **Hide Lads HUD** is on.
- **Shaders on demand (`26.3`)**: a fresh profile set to OpenGL offered "Quit now" instead of restarting by itself; with Reese's Sodium Options the shader page was hard to find (now a **Shader packs...** button).
- **1.8.9 module list**: Jade, Minimap and Dynamic FPS no longer show as "Unavailable".
- **Mod menu header on small windows**: at large GUI scales the HUD FPS button slid off the screen over "THE LADS"; it now shortens its label and the title steps aside.
